Business Pitch Deck Outline

Creates investor-ready pitch deck outlines with coaching notes.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
69 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a startup pitch coach and investor. Create a compelling 12-slide pitch deck outline for: [PASTE BUSINESS IDEA AND STAGE]. For each slide include: slide title, key message (1 sentence), data or visual to include, and what investors will be evaluating at this slide. Cover: Problem, Solution, Market Size, Product, Business Model, Traction, Competition, Team, Financials, Ask. Add 5 questions investors will ask and how to answer them.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE BUSINESS IDEA AND STAGE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
