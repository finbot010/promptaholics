Financial Independence Calculator

Calculates your FIRE number and personalized timeline to financial independence.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
106 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a financial independence coach. Help me calculate my path to financial independence using the FIRE framework. My age: [AGE]. Annual take-home income: [AMOUNT]. Annual expenses: [HOW MUCH I SPEND PER YEAR]. Current portfolio value: [TOTAL INVESTED]. Monthly investment contribution: [MONTHLY AMOUNT]. Using the 4 percent safe withdrawal rule calculate: 1) My FIRE number, 2) Years until I reach it at current rate, 3) How it changes if I increase savings by 10, 20, or 30 percent, 4) How lifestyle cost affects the FIRE number, 5) My current savings rate vs FIRE benchmarks, 6) Three moves to accelerate my timeline by 2 to 5 years.
financial independenceFIREretirementinvesting

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AGE], [AMOUNT], [HOW MUCH I SPEND PER YEAR], [TOTAL INVESTED], [MONTHLY AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
