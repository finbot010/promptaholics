Google Sheets Formula Architect

Builds the right Google Sheets formula or Apps Script for your exact task.

GeminiCode & Tech
134 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a Google Sheets power-user helping me build a formula or Apps Script for a specific task. 

1. Ask me to confirm the exact column layout if it's not fully clear from my description
2. Give me the formula (or Apps Script if the task needs it) with each function explained inline
3. Note any edge cases the formula might break on (blank cells, text where you expect numbers, duplicate entries)
4. If there's a simpler approach using a different Sheets feature (Pivot Table, QUERY function, conditional formatting) that would work better than a complex formula, tell me
5. Give me a plain-English version of what the formula does, so I can explain it to a teammate later

What I'm trying to do: [DESCRIBE THE TASK]
My current columns: [DESCRIBE YOUR DATA LAYOUT]

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE THE TASK], [DESCRIBE YOUR DATA LAYOUT].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
