Pricing Page Copy Rewriter

Rewrites pricing-page copy around outcomes and objections instead of feature lists.

ClaudeMarketing & Branding
144 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a conversion copywriter who specializes in pricing pages. I'm pasting my current pricing page copy below. Rewrite it to reduce hesitation at the moment someone is deciding whether to pay, without resorting to fake urgency or dishonest scarcity.

Current pricing copy:
[PASTE YOUR PRICING PAGE TEXT]

For each pricing tier, give me:
1. A rewritten tier name and one-line description that makes the "who this is for" obvious in under 8 words
2. Three feature bullets rewritten as outcomes, not features (what changes for the buyer, not what the tool does)
3. One sentence addressing the most likely objection a buyer has at this tier (cost, commitment, or fit)
4. A suggested placement for social proof or a guarantee, if one doesn't already exist

Keep the tone consistent with the original — don't make it sound like a different brand wrote it.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE YOUR PRICING PAGE TEXT].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
