Digital Detox Weekend Plan

Plans a realistic digital detox weekend with rules, offline activities and a gentle return.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan a digital detox weekend for me.

My biggest screen habits: [e.g. scrolling in bed, checking email, gaming]
What I still need my phone for: [e.g. maps, emergencies, family contact]
People involved: [ALONE / PARTNER / KIDS]
Interests: [LIST]

Give me: clear rules for the weekend; how to prepare on Friday (notifications, auto-replies, apps to log out of); a schedule of offline activities; what to do when I feel the urge to check; and a plan for Monday to keep one or two new habits.
digital detoxscreen timebalance

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ALONE / PARTNER / KIDS], [LIST].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
