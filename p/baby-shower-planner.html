Baby Shower Planner

Plans a baby shower with a theme, guest list timeline, games, food and a gift registry note.

GeminiLifestyle, Fun & Games
91 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me plan a baby shower for [PARENT-TO-BE] on [DATE].

Guests: [NUMBER] — mix of [FRIENDS / FAMILY / COWORKERS]
Style: [CLASSIC / CO-ED / BRUNCH / VIRTUAL / SURPRISE]
Theme or colors: [IDEA, or "suggest 3"]
Budget: [AMOUNT]
Venue: [HOME / RESTAURANT / PARK]

Give me: a planning timeline from 8 weeks out; invitation wording including the registry; a menu that's easy to make ahead; 5 games that aren't embarrassing for the guest of honor; a decoration list; and a minute-by-minute plan for the day. Add a thank-you note template.
baby showerparty planningcelebrations

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PARENT-TO-BE], [DATE], [NUMBER], [FRIENDS / FAMILY / COWORKERS], [CLASSIC / CO-ED / BRUNCH / VIRTUAL / SURPRISE], [IDEA, or "suggest 3"], [AMOUNT], [HOME / RESTAURANT / PARK] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
