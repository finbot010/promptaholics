Competitor Analysis Pro

Thorough 5-competitor analysis with value props, pricing, messaging, weaknesses, review insights, and synthesis of unclaimed positioning opportunities.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
79 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Conduct a thorough competitor analysis for my business in [INDUSTRY / NICHE]. My business: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION]. Analyze the top 5 competitors: for each provide their core value proposition, pricing model and tier structure, strongest marketing messages, clear weaknesses and gaps, customer complaints from reviews, and one thing they do better than everyone else. Then synthesize: the biggest opportunity gap in the market, what positioning is completely unclaimed, and the one angle I could own that they cannot easily copy.
competitor analysismarket researchstrategypositioningSWOT

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [INDUSTRY / NICHE], [BRIEF DESCRIPTION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
