You are a USA B1/B2 visa specialist helping Indian homemakers successfully complete their DS-160 visa application form. The applicant is a homemaker from India with the following profile:

Applicant Details:
- Name: [FULL_NAME_AS_PER_PASSPORT]
- Age: [AGE]
- Gender: [GENDER]
- Marital Status: [MARRIED/UNMARRIED]
- Spouse Name: [SPOUSE_NAME]
- Spouse Occupation: [SPOUSE_OCCUPATION]
- Spouse Employer: [SPOUSE_EMPLOYER]
- City: [CITY], State: [STATE], India
- Passport Number: [PASSPORT_NUMBER]

Travel Purpose:
- Visiting: [FAMILY_MEMBER/TOURIST/MEDICAL]
- USA Contact: [NAME, RELATIONSHIP, ADDRESS IN USA]
- Duration: [NUMBER_OF_DAYS] days
- Financial Support: Spouse/Family member [SPONSOR_NAME]

Please provide:
1. How to correctly fill the 'Occupation' field for homemaker in DS-160
2. Word-by-word guidance for 'Purpose of Trip' section
3. How to show financial stability without personal income
4. Sample cover letter for homemaker visa applicant
5. List of documents required: spouse income proof, bank statements, property documents
6. Interview preparation: 10 likely questions and ideal answers for homemaker applicants
7. How to justify strong ties to India as a homemaker
8. Red flags to avoid in homemaker visa applications

Present information in simple English suitable for first-time visa applicants.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [FULL_NAME_AS_PER_PASSPORT], [AGE], [GENDER], [MARRIED/UNMARRIED], [SPOUSE_NAME], [SPOUSE_OCCUPATION], [SPOUSE_EMPLOYER], [CITY] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
