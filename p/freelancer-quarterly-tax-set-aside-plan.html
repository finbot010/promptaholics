Freelancer Quarterly Tax Set-Aside Plan

Plans how much a freelancer should set aside for taxes and when estimated payments may be due.

ClaudePersonal Finance & Investing
102 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm self-employed and want to stop being surprised at tax time.

Country/state: [LOCATION]
Expected income this year: [AMOUNT]
Business expenses: [ESTIMATE]
Other income: [e.g. a part-time job with taxes withheld]
How I get paid: [MONTHLY RETAINERS / IRREGULAR PROJECTS]

Explain in plain English how estimated taxes generally work where I live (and what to confirm with the official tax site or an accountant), suggest a percentage of each payment to set aside, a calendar of likely due dates to verify, a simple two-account system, and the records to keep. Give me a short checklist I can follow every time I get paid.
freelancer taxesself-employedquarterly taxes

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LOCATION], [AMOUNT], [ESTIMATE], [MONTHLY RETAINERS / IRREGULAR PROJECTS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
