Stretching Routine for Desk Workers

Creates short stretching and movement breaks for people who sit all day.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
109 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create stretching breaks for my workday. If I mention pain or an injury, keep it gentle and tell me to check with a doctor or physical therapist.

Work setup: [DESK / STANDING DESK / LAPTOP ON COUCH]
Where I feel tight: [NECK / SHOULDERS / LOWER BACK / HIPS / WRISTS]
Time per break: [2 / 5 / 10 MINUTES]
Can I get on the floor: [YES / NO]

Give me 3 routines (2, 5 and 10 minutes), each move described in one sentence with how long to hold it, an easier version, and what to avoid. Add a simple schedule for the day and how to set reminders.
stretchingdesk workposture

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESK / STANDING DESK / LAPTOP ON COUCH], [NECK / SHOULDERS / LOWER BACK / HIPS / WRISTS], [2 / 5 / 10 MINUTES], [YES / NO].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
