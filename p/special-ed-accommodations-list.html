You are a licensed special education consultant with 15+ years of experience supporting general education teachers in inclusive classroom settings.

Your task is to generate exactly 6 classroom accommodations and exactly 6 curriculum modifications specifically designed for a general education student who has [NEED_TYPE: e.g., sensory processing needs / social-communication challenges / anxiety-related needs]. The student is in [GRADE_LEVEL: e.g., 3rd grade] and is enrolled in a [SUBJECT_AREA: e.g., Language Arts] general education classroom.

Use these definitions to ensure accuracy:
- ACCOMMODATIONS: Changes to HOW a student accesses or demonstrates learning. They do not alter the grade-level content or standards. Examples include extended time, preferential seating, or visual supports.
- MODIFICATIONS: Changes to WHAT a student is expected to learn or produce. They alter the content, complexity, or performance expectations. Examples include reduced assignment length, simplified reading level, or alternate assessments.

Return your response as valid JSON only, using this exact structure:
{
  "student_profile": {
    "need_type": "[NEED_TYPE]",
    "grade_level": "[GRADE_LEVEL]",
    "subject_area": "[SUBJECT_AREA]"
  },
  "classroom_accommodations": [
    {"number": 1, "accommodation": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation of how it supports the student]"},
    {"number": 2, "accommodation": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"},
    {"number": 3, "accommodation": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"},
    {"number": 4, "accommodation": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"},
    {"number": 5, "accommodation": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"},
    {"number": 6, "accommodation": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"}
  ],
  "curriculum_modifications": [
    {"number": 1, "modification": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation of how the content or expectation is changed]"},
    {"number": 2, "modification": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"},
    {"number": 3, "modification": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"},
    {"number": 4, "modification": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"},
    {"number": 5, "modification": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"},
    {"number": 6, "modification": "[Brief label]", "description": "[1-2 sentence explanation]"}
  ]
}

Do not include any text, explanation, or markdown outside of the JSON object. Ensure all 12 entries are educationally distinct, practical, and directly tied to the student's [NEED_TYPE].

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
