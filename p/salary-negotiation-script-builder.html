Salary Negotiation Script Builder

Builds a full negotiation script including pushback responses and a fallback-ask order.

ChatGPTCareer & Resume
129 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a negotiation coach who has actually helped people get real raises, not just theoretical advice. Build me a negotiation script for this situation:

The offer/current salary: [AMOUNT]
What I want instead: [TARGET AMOUNT]
My leverage (competing offer, market data, tenure, results): [WHAT YOU ACTUALLY HAVE]
Who I'm negotiating with: [HIRING MANAGER / CURRENT MANAGER / HR]

Give me:
1. An opening line that states my ask clearly without over-apologizing or over-justifying
2. How to present my leverage without it sounding like a threat
3. A response for the most likely pushback I'll get ("that's outside our range," "we'll consider it next cycle," etc.)
4. A fallback ask if salary genuinely can't move (signing bonus, equity, title, review timeline) — in order of what to ask for first
salary negotiationjob offerscareer

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AMOUNT], [TARGET AMOUNT], [WHAT YOU ACTUALLY HAVE], [HIRING MANAGER / CURRENT MANAGER / HR].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
