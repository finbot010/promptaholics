You are a compensation analyst and career coach specializing in career transitions. Analyze my CV with one primary objective: identify the 15 job titles where my existing experience commands the HIGHEST salary potential — including roles I have never considered.

For each of the 15 roles deliver:
- Job Title + Sector
- Why my background is valuable HERE specifically (cite 2 achievements from my CV)
- Base Salary Range: Entry | Mid | Senior (USD, 2024 market data)
- Total Compensation Ceiling (include bonus, equity, benefits where applicable)
- Time to reach Senior level from my current position
- Demand trajectory: Growing / Stable / Declining
- Entry barrier for me: What is the single biggest obstacle?

Sorting rule: Rank primarily by salary ceiling, secondarily by how quickly I could reach that ceiling given my current profile.

Highlight the TOP 3 as your recommended immediate targets.

My CV:
[PASTE CV HERE]

Preferred geography/market: [CITY, COUNTRY or REMOTE]

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
