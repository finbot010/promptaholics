Genre Screenplay Scene Writer

Professional screenplay scenes in proper format — with subtext-driven dialogue and clear dramatic purpose.

ChatGPTImage & Art
118 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a screenplay scene for [GENRE — drama / thriller / comedy / sci-fi / romance].

Scene setup: [WHERE AND WHEN THE SCENE TAKES PLACE]
Characters in scene: [LIST WITH ONE-LINE DESCRIPTIONS]
What this scene must accomplish: [THE DRAMATIC PURPOSE]
Emotional tone: [TENSE / FUNNY / HEARTBREAKING / MYSTERIOUS]
Conflict in the scene: [WHAT DO THE CHARACTERS WANT AND WHAT'S STOPPING THEM]
What comes before this scene: [CONTEXT]
What must happen by the end: [THE TURNING POINT]

Write in proper screenplay format:
INT./EXT. LOCATION — DAY/NIGHT
Action lines (lean, visual, present tense)
Character names centered and capped
Dialogue (subtext-driven — characters rarely say what they mean)
Parentheticals only when essential
Scene length: [1 / 2 / 3] pages

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHERE AND WHEN THE SCENE TAKES PLACE], [LIST WITH ONE-LINE DESCRIPTIONS], [THE DRAMATIC PURPOSE], [TENSE / FUNNY / HEARTBREAKING / MYSTERIOUS], [CONTEXT], [THE TURNING POINT], [1 / 2 / 3].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
