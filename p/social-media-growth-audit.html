Social Media Growth Audit

Comprehensive social media audit with 30-day growth plan.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
54 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Audit [BRAND/ACCOUNT] on [PLATFORM].

Evaluate:
1. Profile optimization score
2. Top 5 performing content types and why
3. Posting frequency vs engagement correlation
4. Audience alignment with target customer
5. 3 competitor comparisons
6. Content gaps
7. 5 growth tactics to implement now
8. 30-day action plan week by week

Context: [BRAND + GOALS]
social mediaauditstrategygrowth

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BRAND/ACCOUNT], [PLATFORM], [BRAND + GOALS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
