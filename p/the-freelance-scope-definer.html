The Freelance Scope Definer

Defines a clear, client-readable scope of work with explicit revision and out-of-scope boundaries.

ClaudeBusiness & Strategy★ Featured
50 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me define a clear scope of work for a freelance [service] project with [client type]. Include: exact deliverables, number of revision rounds included, what counts as 'out of scope' and gets billed separately, and a rough timeline. Write it so a client with no industry background would understand it.
