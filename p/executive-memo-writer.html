Executive Memo Writer

Writes a dense, signal-first executive memo instead of a padded one.

ClaudeMarketing & Branding
124 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write an executive memo based on the information I provide. Executive memos need to be dense with signal, not padded with context the reader already has. Structure:

**Header**: To / From / Date / Re
**Bottom Line Up Front**: The recommendation or key finding in 1-2 sentences, before any explanation
**Situation**: 2-3 sentences of essential context only
**Analysis**: The reasoning, in the fewest words that still make the case rigorously
**Recommendation**: Specific, actionable, with a clear owner and timeline if applicable
**Risks**: What could go wrong with this recommendation, stated honestly

Target length: under 400 words unless the topic genuinely requires more.

What I need to communicate: [DESCRIBE THE SITUATION, DATA, OR DECISION]
Audience: [WHO IS READING THIS - e.g. CEO, board, cross-functional leads]

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE THE SITUATION, DATA, OR DECISION].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
