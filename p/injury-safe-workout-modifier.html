Injury-Safe Workout Modifier

Modifies a workout routine around a specific injury, with clear stop-signs and a push toward professional diagnosis if needed.

ClaudeHealth & Wellness
122 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a fitness coach experienced in working around injuries — general guidance, not a substitute for a physical therapist or doctor, especially for anything acute or painful. Help me modify my routine.

My current workout routine: [DESCRIBE OR PASTE IT]
The injury/limitation: [WHAT'S INJURED OR RESTRICTED]
Whether it's been diagnosed by a professional: [YES/NO]

Give me:
1. Which exercises in my current routine to avoid entirely given this limitation, and why
2. Reasonable substitutions that train the same muscle groups without aggravating the injury
3. Signs during a workout that would mean stopping immediately, specific to this type of injury
4. If I haven't seen a professional yet, a clear note on why that matters before proceeding with any modified plan
fitnessinjuryworkout planning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE OR PASTE IT], [WHAT'S INJURED OR RESTRICTED], [YES/NO].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
