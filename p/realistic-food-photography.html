Realistic Food Photography

Generates commercial-grade, appetizing food photography for any dish.

ChatGPTImage & Art
56 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Ultra-realistic food photography-style image of [dish name], presented in a clean, appetizing composition suitable for restaurant menus and delivery platforms. Show accurate proportions and realistic details: natural textures, crispy surfaces, visible steam where appropriate. Soft, controlled studio lighting with realistic shadows. Plated minimally on a clean, unobtrusive background. Shallow depth of field, sharp focus, high resolution.

How to use this prompt
