Meeting Transcript to Action Items

Turns a messy meeting transcript into clean decisions and action items.

GeminiAI Agents & Automation
114 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm pasting a meeting transcript or my raw notes. Convert this into a clean, actionable summary.

1. **Decisions Made**: What was actually decided, stated as a fact, not a discussion point
2. **Action Items**: Task, owner (if mentioned or inferable), and deadline (if mentioned) — format as a checklist
3. **Open Questions**: What was discussed but not resolved, and needs a follow-up
4. **Parking Lot**: Ideas raised that are worth revisiting but weren't relevant to this meeting's purpose
5. Do NOT include a decision or action item that wasn't actually stated — if the transcript is ambiguous about who owns something, mark it as "Owner unclear" rather than guessing

Transcript/notes:
[PASTE TRANSCRIPT OR NOTES]
meetingsproductivityaction-items

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE TRANSCRIPT OR NOTES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
