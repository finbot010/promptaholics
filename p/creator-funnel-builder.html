Build a complete creator funnel from discovery to conversion for [CREATOR/BRAND] selling [OFFER]. Map every stage: 1. Discovery — how new people find them (platform, content type, hook style) 2. Attention — what makes someone stop and watch/read 3. Interest — how they build intrigue and establish authority 4. Connection — how they create emotional resonance 5. Nurture — how they deliver value and build trust 6. Conversion — how they move people to buy without being pushy 7. Retention — how they keep customers coming back. For each stage provide the content type, the message, and the metric to track.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CREATOR/BRAND], [OFFER].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
