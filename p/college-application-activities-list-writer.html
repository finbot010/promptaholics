College Application Activities List Writer

Turns your activities into strong, specific entries that fit tight character limits.

ChatGPTEducation & Learning
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me write my college application activities list.

Activities: [NAME, ROLE, YEARS, HOURS PER WEEK, WHAT I DID, ANY RESULTS — for each]
Character limits: [e.g. 50 for position, 150 for description]

For each activity, write a position title and a description with strong verbs and specific numbers (people reached, money raised, events run) within the limits. Suggest the order to list them in, from most to least impressive, and tell me which activities to combine or drop. Keep everything accurate to what I gave.
college applicationsactivities liststudents

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
