Villain Origin Story

Creates psychologically rich villain origin stories where the reader understands — even sympathizes — with the antagonist.

ChatGPTImage & Art
109 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a compelling villain origin story for [CHARACTER NAME], the antagonist of a [GENRE] story.

Their evil: [WHAT THEY DO]
Their world before: [THEIR ORIGINAL LIFE]

Craft a 600-word origin that:
- Makes the reader genuinely sympathize with them before they turn
- Shows the exact moment everything broke
- Reveals what they wanted that they could never have
- Includes one defining trauma that warped their worldview
- Ends with the decision that crossed the point of no return

Rule: The reader should be able to understand, even if not condone, every step of the path. The best villains believe they are the hero of their own story.
villainfictioncharacterstorytellingcreative writing

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CHARACTER NAME], [GENRE], [WHAT THEY DO], [THEIR ORIGINAL LIFE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
