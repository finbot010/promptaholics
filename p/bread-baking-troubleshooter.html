Bread Baking Troubleshooter

Diagnoses bread problems like dense crumb, flat loaves or a pale crust, and tells you what to change.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
78 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me fix my bread.

Bread type: [SOURDOUGH / SANDWICH LOAF / BAGUETTE / ENRICHED]
Recipe and method I used: [PASTE]
Kitchen temperature: [TEMPERATURE]
What went wrong: [e.g. dense, flat, gummy, huge holes, pale crust, burnt bottom]
Photo (optional): [UPLOAD]

Give me the most likely causes ranked, how to tell which one it is, and exactly what to change on my next bake (one change at a time). Include how to judge proofing without relying on the clock.
bread bakingsourdoughtroubleshooting

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SOURDOUGH / SANDWICH LOAF / BAGUETTE / ENRICHED], [PASTE], [TEMPERATURE], [UPLOAD].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
