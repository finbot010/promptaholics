Knife Skills and Kitchen Technique Guide

Teaches knife skills and kitchen techniques with professional instruction.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking

Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a culinary school instructor. Write a knife skills and kitchen technique guide for: [PASTE SKILL LEVEL AND FOCUS: beginner/intermediate, specific techniques needed]. Cover: essential cuts (dice, mince, julienne, chiffonade) with description of proper hand position and motion, how to keep a knife sharp and safe, mise en place philosophy and setup, heat control fundamentals, seasoning techniques (when and why), and the 5 techniques that will most improve home cooking. Write as if teaching in person.
