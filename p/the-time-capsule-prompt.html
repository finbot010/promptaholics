The Time Capsule Prompt

A guided letter to your future self covering honest reflection, goals, predictions, and a personal promise.

ChatGPTMore Prompts
145 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me write a detailed letter to my future self, dated exactly one year from today. Guide me through reflecting on: (1) Where I Am Now — my current situation in career, relationships, health, finances, and personal growth — the honest, unfiltered version, (2) What I'm Proud Of — 5 specific achievements from this past year, no matter how small, (3) What I'm Struggling With — the 3 biggest challenges I'm facing and what I've tried so far, (4) My Predictions — what I think my life will look like in 12 months, (5) My Non-Negotiable Goals — 5 specific, measurable goals with deadlines, (6) The Promise — one commitment I'm making to myself that I refuse to break, (7) The Secret Dream — the one thing I want but haven't told anyone. End with a message of encouragement from present me to future me.
reflectiongoal settingpersonal growthjournalingself-awareness

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
