Stress and Burnout Recovery

Personalized burnout recovery protocol with immediate stabilization, 2-week restoration plan, long-term prevention, and boundary-setting scripts.

Create a personalized burnout recovery and stress management protocol for someone experiencing: [DESCRIBE CURRENT SITUATION — work stress / creative burnout / emotional exhaustion]. Design a tiered recovery system: (1) Immediate stabilization — what to do today to stop the bleeding, (2) Short-term recovery — a 2-week restoration protocol with daily structure, (3) Long-term prevention — systemic changes to prevent recurrence. Include evidence-based techniques: nervous system regulation, sleep optimization, energy management, and boundary-setting scripts for common situations.
