Trip Budget Estimator

Estimates the full cost of a trip with current prices for flights, stays, food, transport and activities.

GeminiTravel
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Estimate a realistic budget for my trip using current prices where you can find them.

Destination: [PLACE]
Dates and length: [DATES]
Travelers: [NUMBER]
Travel style: [BUDGET / MID-RANGE / COMFORT]
Plans: [ACTIVITIES, DAY TRIPS]

Break down: flights, accommodation, local transport, food per day, activities and tickets, SIM or data, insurance, and a 10% buffer. Show a total per person and per day, where I could save, and costs travelers often forget (tourist taxes, tips, airport transfers). Note which prices you found and which are estimates.
travel budgetcostsplanning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PLACE], [DATES], [NUMBER], [BUDGET / MID-RANGE / COMFORT], [ACTIVITIES, DAY TRIPS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
