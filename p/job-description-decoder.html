Job Description Decoder

Reads a job posting and reveals what the employer really wants, the keywords to use, and how to position yourself.

ClaudeCareer & Resume
89 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Decode this job posting for me.

Job description:
[PASTE]
My background:
[SHORT SUMMARY OR RESUME]

Tell me:
1. The 3 things this employer most needs, reading between the lines.
2. Must-have vs nice-to-have requirements.
3. Keywords and phrases to mirror in my resume and cover letter.
4. Where I am a strong match and where I am weak, honestly.
5. How to address my weakest point.
6. Red flags or questions about the role or company worth asking in the interview.
7. A 3-sentence pitch for why I fit.
job searchjob descriptionapplication

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE], [SHORT SUMMARY OR RESUME].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
