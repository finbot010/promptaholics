The Concept Explainer

Any concept explained at 4 levels — child, freshman, professional, and expert — with examples that stick.

ChatGPTEducation & Learning★ Featured
104 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Explain [COMPLEX TOPIC] to me using 4 different levels of complexity: Level 1 — Like I'm 10 years old (use a fun analogy and zero jargon), Level 2 — Like I'm a college freshman (introduce proper terminology with definitions), Level 3 — Like I'm a professional in a related field (connect to adjacent concepts and real applications), Level 4 — Like I'm an expert peer (dive deep into nuances, edge cases, and current debates in the field). For each level, include one memorable example that makes the concept stick. End with 3 questions I should ask myself to test if I truly understand it.
learningexplanationteachingsimplificationeducation

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [COMPLEX TOPIC].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
