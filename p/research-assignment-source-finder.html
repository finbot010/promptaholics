Research Assignment Source Finder

Finds credible, current sources for a school or college research topic and explains how to use each.

GeminiEducation & Learning
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me research this assignment using current, credible sources.

Topic / research question: [QUESTION]
Level: [HIGH SCHOOL / UNIVERSITY]
Citation style: [APA / MLA / CHICAGO]
Sources required: [NUMBER AND TYPES, e.g. 3 peer-reviewed]

Search and give me: 8 sources with title, author, year, publisher and link; one line on what each contributes; a credibility note for each; 2 sources representing a different viewpoint; and formatted citations in my style. Flag any source I should verify in my library database because it may be paywalled or outdated.
researchsourcescitations

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [QUESTION], [HIGH SCHOOL / UNIVERSITY], [APA / MLA / CHICAGO], [NUMBER AND TYPES, e.g. 3 peer-reviewed].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
