Content Repurposing From a Google Doc

Turns one long piece in Google Drive into a week of posts for different platforms.

GeminiWriting & Content
76 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Drive Repurpose my document "[DOC NAME]" into content for other platforms.

Platforms: [LINKEDIN / INSTAGRAM / X / NEWSLETTER / YOUTUBE SHORTS]
Voice: [HOW I SOUND]

Create: 3 LinkedIn posts, 5 short posts for X, 1 Instagram carousel outline (slide by slide), 1 short video script (under 60 seconds), and a newsletter teaser. Each should stand alone and use a different angle or quote from the document. Keep facts exactly as written in my doc.
repurposingsocial mediagoogle docs

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DOC NAME], [HOW I SOUND].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
