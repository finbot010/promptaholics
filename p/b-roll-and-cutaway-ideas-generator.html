B-Roll and Cutaway Ideas Generator

Suggests specific b-roll shots for each line of your script so the video never feels static.

Suggest b-roll for my video script.

Script:
[PASTE]
Resources: [e.g. phone only, stock footage subscription, screen recordings, home location]

For each paragraph or key line: 2-3 b-roll ideas (what to film or find), whether to shoot it or use stock (with search terms), and on-screen text if useful. Mark the moments where cutting back to the speaker works better than b-roll.
