Follow-Up Finder for Unanswered Emails

Finds emails you sent that never got a reply and drafts polite follow-ups.

GeminiEmail, Sales & Communication
57 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Gmail Find emails I sent in the last [NUMBER] weeks that have not received a reply.

Ignore newsletters, receipts and automated messages. For each important one: who, subject, date sent, what I was asking for, and a short follow-up draft (under 70 words) that makes it easy to answer. Rank them by importance based on: [YOUR PRIORITIES].
gmailfollow-upinbox

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER], [YOUR PRIORITIES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
