Car Lease vs Buy Calculator

Compares the full cost of leasing versus financing versus paying cash for a car.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
84 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Compare leasing vs buying this car.

Car and price: [MAKE, MODEL, PRICE]
Lease offer: [MONTHLY, TERM, DOWN, MILEAGE LIMIT, FEES]
Loan offer: [RATE, TERM, DOWN PAYMENT]
How many miles/km I drive per year: [NUMBER]
How long I usually keep cars: [YEARS]

Show the total cost of each option over [YEARS] in a table: payments, fees, insurance differences, expected resale value, and mileage overage risk. Tell me which fits my habits and why, the questions to ask the dealer, and the fees people commonly miss.
car leasecar loancomparison

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MAKE, MODEL, PRICE], [MONTHLY, TERM, DOWN, MILEAGE LIMIT, FEES], [RATE, TERM, DOWN PAYMENT], [NUMBER], [YEARS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
