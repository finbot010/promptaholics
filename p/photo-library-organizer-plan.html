Photo Library Organizer Plan

Creates a system to organize, back up and find your photos in Google Photos or Drive.

GeminiImage & Art
88 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me organize my photo library.

Where photos are now: [PHONE / GOOGLE PHOTOS / DRIVE / HARD DRIVES]
Approximate number of photos: [NUMBER]
What I want to find easily: [e.g. family events, client shoots, travel, best edits]
Storage plan and budget: [DETAILS]

Give me: a folder or album structure, naming rules, how to use Google Photos search, faces and albums to save time, a backup plan with at least two copies, a one-time cleanup plan in small weekly sessions, and a monthly routine to keep it tidy.
google photosorganizationbackup

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PHONE / GOOGLE PHOTOS / DRIVE / HARD DRIVES], [NUMBER], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
