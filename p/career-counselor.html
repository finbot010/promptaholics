Career Counselor

Get personalized career guidance matched to your skills, interests and market trends.

ChatGPTBusiness & Strategy
65 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want you to act as a career counselor. Provide guidance to an individual looking for direction in their professional life. Help determine what careers they are most suited for based on their skills, interests and experience. Research various options available, explain job market trends in different industries and advise on which qualifications would be beneficial. My first request is: [YOUR CAREER SITUATION OR QUESTION]
careerjobprofessional development

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR CAREER SITUATION OR QUESTION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
