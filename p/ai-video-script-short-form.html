AI Video Script (Short Form)

Second-by-second short-form video scripts optimized for the specific platform algorithm and format.

AIWriting & Content
76 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a [15/30/60]-second script for a viral short-form video about [TOPIC] for [PLATFORM: TikTok/Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts].

Target viewer: [WHO IS WATCHING]
Goal: [ENTERTAIN/EDUCATE/SELL/BUILD TRUST]
My niche: [DESCRIBE]
Tone: [CASUAL/AUTHORITATIVE/FUNNY/EMOTIONAL]

Format each second of the script:
[0-3s] HOOK: (what stops the scroll)
[3-10s] SETUP: (context or problem)
[10-20s] PAYOFF: (the value, twist, or lesson)
[20-25s] CTA: (what to do next)

Also provide:
- On-screen text overlays
- B-roll suggestions
- Sound/music mood
- Caption for the post

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [15/30/60], [TOPIC], [PLATFORM: TikTok/Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts], [WHO IS WATCHING], [ENTERTAIN/EDUCATE/SELL/BUILD TRUST], [DESCRIBE], [CASUAL/AUTHORITATIVE/FUNNY/EMOTIONAL], [0-3s] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and send it.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
