Ultimate Research Agent

Elite research agent produces a comprehensive intelligence report with executive summary, key players, trends, debates, and actionable implications.

ChatGPTAI Agents & Automation★ Featured
83 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are an elite research agent with access to deep analytical capabilities. Thoroughly research [TOPIC / QUESTION] and produce a comprehensive intelligence report. Structure your report: Executive Summary (3 bullet key findings), Background and Context, Current State of the Field, Key Players and Their Positions, Emerging Trends and Signals, Contradictions and Debates in the Literature, Practical Implications for [MY CONTEXT], and Recommended Next Steps for Deeper Research. Cite your reasoning. Flag areas of uncertainty. Distinguish between established consensus and emerging or contested claims.
researchagentanalysisreportcomprehensiveintelligence

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC / QUESTION], [MY CONTEXT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
