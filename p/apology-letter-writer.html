Apology Letter Writer

A sincere, effective apology that takes full responsibility without excuses — for any format or relationship.

ChatGPTMore Prompts
129 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me write a sincere and effective apology for [SITUATION].

What happened: [DESCRIBE THE SITUATION AND WHAT YOU DID]
Who was affected: [RELATIONSHIP — friend / partner / colleague / client / family]
How they were hurt: [DESCRIBE THE IMPACT ON THEM]
What I've already said or done: [PREVIOUS ATTEMPTS IF ANY]
My genuine remorse: [WHAT YOU FEEL AND WHY]
What I'm committing to change: [CONCRETE ACTIONS]
Format needed: [IN PERSON SCRIPT / LETTER / TEXT / EMAIL]

Write an apology that:
- Takes full responsibility (no 'I'm sorry if you felt…')
- Acknowledges the specific impact on them
- Shows you understand why it was wrong
- Doesn't over-explain or make excuses
- States what you will do differently
- Doesn't demand forgiveness
- Fits the format requested
apologycommunicationrelationshipswritingpersonal

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SITUATION], [DESCRIBE THE SITUATION AND WHAT YOU DID], [DESCRIBE THE IMPACT ON THEM], [PREVIOUS ATTEMPTS IF ANY], [WHAT YOU FEEL AND WHY], [CONCRETE ACTIONS], [IN PERSON SCRIPT / LETTER / TEXT / EMAIL].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
