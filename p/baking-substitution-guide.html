Baking Substitution Guide

Tells you how to substitute ingredients in a baking recipe and what will change in the result.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
76 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I need to substitute ingredients in this baking recipe.

Recipe:
[PASTE]
What I need to replace and why: [e.g. no eggs, dairy-free, gluten-free, out of buttermilk]
What I have available: [LIST]

For each substitution: the best option and amount, how it changes taste, texture and rise, and any adjustment to other ingredients, time or temperature. Tell me if the recipe is too dependent on that ingredient to substitute well, and suggest a better recipe type instead.
bakingsubstitutionsallergies

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE], [LIST].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
