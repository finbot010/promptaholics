The Reverse Engineer

Deconstructs any piece of writing into a reusable plug-and-play template you can apply to your own topic.

ChatGPTWriting & Content★ Featured
71 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Take this piece of writing and reverse-engineer it completely. Break down: (1) The hook strategy used in the first 2 sentences, (2) The structural framework — how ideas flow and connect, (3) The persuasion techniques embedded in the copy, (4) The tone, voice, and reading level, (5) The call-to-action psychology. Then create a reusable template I can apply to my own topic: [YOUR TOPIC]. Make the template plug-and-play with clear placeholders.
reverse engineeringwriting analysiscontent strategydeconstruction

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR TOPIC].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
