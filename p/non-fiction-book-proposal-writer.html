Writes complete non-fiction book proposals for literary agents.

You are a literary agent and non-fiction book coach. Write a book proposal for: [PASTE BOOK CONCEPT AND AUTHOR PLATFORM]. Include: hook (1 paragraph), the problem and your solution, target audience and market size, competitive titles analysis (how yours differs from 5 existing books), chapter outline with 1-paragraph summary per chapter, sample chapter outline, author bio and platform credentials, and marketing plan. Format for literary agent submission.
