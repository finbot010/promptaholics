Audience Obsession Analyzer

Analyze what this audience is obsessed with and generate content ideas that tap into those obsessions. Audience: [DESCRIPTION]. Niche: [NICHE]. Identify their top 5 obsessions — the things they talk about constantly, search for obsessively, and share without being asked. For each obsession: describe it in detail, explain the psychology behind it, generate 5 content ideas that tap into it, write an opening hook for the strongest idea, and suggest the best format and platform. End with the one obsession that creates the most viral content potential and a full content brief for the first piece.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIPTION], [NICHE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
