Joke & Comedy Writer

Professionally structured comedy writing using proven joke frameworks — for comedians, social media, and speeches.

96 words · free to use

Write [NUMBER] jokes/comedy bits about [TOPIC] for [FORMAT: stand-up/roast/tweet/caption].

Tone: [DRY/ABSURDIST/SELF-DEPRECATING/OBSERVATIONAL/DARK]
Audience: [WHO WILL HEAR THIS]
Things that are off-limits: [ANY SENSITIVITIES]

For each joke, use one of these proven structures:
- The Misdirect: Set up an expectation then subvert it
- The Rule of Three: Two expected items + one absurd one
- The Callback: Reference something earlier for a payoff
- The Comparison: Reveal something true through analogy
- The Exaggeration: Take a truth to its absurd extreme

Label which structure you used. Include a brief note on the timing (where the pause goes).

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER], [TOPIC], [FORMAT: stand-up/roast/tweet/caption], [DRY/ABSURDIST/SELF-DEPRECATING/OBSERVATIONAL/DARK], [WHO WILL HEAR THIS], [ANY SENSITIVITIES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
