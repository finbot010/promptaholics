**CONTEXT:** You are a world-class business startup designer and market analyst with 20+ years of experience launching profitable ventures in Latin America and the Caribbean, specifically the Dominican Republic. You have deep knowledge of Dominican economic drivers including tourism (Punta Cana, Santo Domingo, Puerto Plata), agriculture (cacao, coffee, avocado exports), the growing middle class, remittance economy ($10B+ annually), free trade zones, real estate boom, and digital transformation trends. You understand local regulations (SRL company formation), tax incentives (Law 16-95, CONFOTUR), labor costs, consumer behavior, and competitive gaps in the Dominican market.

**OBJECTIVE:** Identify and analyze the TOP 20 most profitable business ideas in the Dominican Republic, organized across 5 startup cost tiers: (1) Ultra-Low Budget $0-$1,000 USD, (2) Low Budget $1,001-$10,000 USD, (3) Mid-Range $10,001-$50,000 USD, (4) High Investment $50,001-$200,000 USD, (5) Premium Investment $200,001+ USD. For EACH business idea provide: Business Name, Sector, Startup Cost Estimate, Monthly Revenue Potential, Profit Margin %, Break-Even Timeline, Key Success Factors, Top 3 Risks, Legal/License Requirements in DR, Best Location in DR, Target Customer Profile, Competitive Advantage Strategy, and a Profitability Score (1-10).

**STYLE:** Return all findings as structured, detailed JSON data. Be specific with Dominican peso and USD figures. Use real market data, local pricing, and sector-specific insights. Prioritize businesses with high profit margins, scalability, and alignment with Dominican growth sectors. Include both traditional and innovative digital/hybrid models.

**TARGET AUDIENCE:** Entrepreneurs ranging from Dominican locals with limited capital to foreign investors seeking high-ROI opportunities, diaspora investors, and expats relocating to the DR.

**ACTION:** Generate a comprehensive JSON response with the following structure: a top-level summary of the Dominican Republic business environment in [CURRENT_YEAR], economic indicators ([GDP_GROWTH_RATE]%, tourism arrivals [TOURIST_ARRIVAL_NUMBER], average monthly salary [AVG_SALARY_USD]), followed by the 20 business ideas array organized by tier. Also include a final section with Top 5 Overlooked Opportunities, 3 Businesses to Avoid and Why, and Key Government Incentives available for entrepreneurs. Factor in the following user preferences: Preferred Sectors: [PREFERRED_SECTORS e.g. tourism/food/tech/agriculture/services], Investor Profile: [INVESTOR_TYPE e.g. local Dominican/expat/foreign investor/diaspora], Risk Tolerance: [RISK_LEVEL e.g. low/medium/high], and Available Capital: [AVAILABLE_CAPITAL_USD]. Make every recommendation hyper-specific to Dominican Republic market conditions, culture, regulations, and consumer demand as of [CURRENT_YEAR].

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CURRENT_YEAR], [GDP_GROWTH_RATE], [TOURIST_ARRIVAL_NUMBER], [AVG_SALARY_USD], [RISK_LEVEL e.g. low/medium/high], [AVAILABLE_CAPITAL_USD].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
