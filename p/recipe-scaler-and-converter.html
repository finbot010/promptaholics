Recipe Scaler and Converter

Scales a recipe up or down and converts units, with notes on what does not scale linearly.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
68 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Scale and convert this recipe.

Recipe:
[PASTE]
Original servings: [NUMBER]
Servings I need: [NUMBER]
Units I want: [METRIC / US CUPS / GRAMS FOR BAKING]
Pan or equipment I have: [SIZE / TYPE]

Give me the scaled ingredient list, adjusted steps, and notes on what does not scale exactly (baking times, pan size, spices, salt, leavening). Tell me how to adjust cooking time and temperature for my pan.
recipe scalingconversionsbaking

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE], [NUMBER], [METRIC / US CUPS / GRAMS FOR BAKING], [SIZE / TYPE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
