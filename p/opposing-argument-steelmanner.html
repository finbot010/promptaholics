Opposing Argument Steelmanner

Builds the strongest possible opposing argument so you can pressure-test your own position.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
138 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I hold a position on [TOPIC/ISSUE]. Before I argue for it publicly, I want to understand the strongest possible case against it — not a weak version I can easily knock down.

1. Construct the steelman: the version of the opposing argument that a smart, well-informed person who disagrees with me would actually make
2. Identify which parts of my position it most directly threatens
3. Identify any part of the opposing case that's actually correct, even if I still disagree with the overall conclusion
4. Tell me honestly: after constructing this steelman, does it change how confident I should be in my original position?

My position: [STATE YOUR POSITION AND YOUR REASONING FOR IT]

Don't just perform devil's advocate — genuinely try to find the best version of the counter-argument, including angles I may not have considered.
debateargumentationcritical-thinking

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC/ISSUE], [STATE YOUR POSITION AND YOUR REASONING FOR IT].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
