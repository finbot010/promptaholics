Claude Thinking Mode Prompt

Optimized for Claude's extended thinking mode — ideal for complex reasoning, strategy, and multi-factor decisions.

AIMore Prompts
101 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I have a complex problem that requires deep reasoning. Use extended thinking to work through this carefully.

Problem: [DESCRIBE YOUR COMPLEX PROBLEM]
Context: [RELEVANT BACKGROUND]
Constraints: [ANY LIMITATIONS OR REQUIREMENTS]
What I have already tried: [PREVIOUS ATTEMPTS]
What a good solution looks like: [SUCCESS CRITERIA]

Please:
1. Map the full problem space before proposing solutions
2. Consider at least 3 fundamentally different approaches
3. Identify hidden assumptions in how I framed the problem
4. Recommend the best approach with clear reasoning
5. Identify what could go wrong with your recommendation
6. Give me the first concrete step to take right now
Claudeextended thinkingreasoningcomplex problemsAI

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE YOUR COMPLEX PROBLEM], [RELEVANT BACKGROUND], [ANY LIMITATIONS OR REQUIREMENTS], [PREVIOUS ATTEMPTS], [SUCCESS CRITERIA].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and send it.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
