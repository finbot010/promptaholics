Zero Based Budget Builder

Builds a complete zero-based budget tailored to your income and goals.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
100 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a certified financial planner. Help me build a zero-based budget where every dollar has a job. My monthly take-home income: [AMOUNT AFTER TAXES]. My fixed monthly expenses: [LIST ALL FIXED BILLS]. My variable expenses last month: [GROCERIES, GAS, DINING, ENTERTAINMENT WITH AMOUNTS]. My financial goals: [DEBT PAYOFF / EMERGENCY FUND / INVESTING]. Build a zero-based budget that: 1) Allocates every dollar to a category, 2) Identifies where I am overspending, 3) Prioritizes my goals in the right order, 4) Includes a sinking fund strategy for irregular expenses, 5) Gives me a realistic lifestyle budget I can stick to.
budgetzero basedpersonal financemoney management

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AMOUNT AFTER TAXES], [LIST ALL FIXED BILLS], [DEBT PAYOFF / EMERGENCY FUND / INVESTING].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
