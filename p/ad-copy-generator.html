Ad Copy Generator

Complete ad copy for Facebook/Instagram, Google Search, and YouTube — 3 Facebook angles, Google ad sets, and YouTube hook script.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
66 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write high-converting ad copy for [PRODUCT / SERVICE] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Create complete ad sets for: (1) Facebook/Instagram feed ad — headline, primary text, and description for 3 different angles: problem-aware, solution-aware, and social proof. (2) Google search ads — 3 headlines and 2 descriptions following character limits. (3) YouTube 5-second unskippable hook script. For each, lead with the customer's pain or desire, not the product features.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT / SERVICE], [AUDIENCE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
