Character Backstory Builder

Creates a rich psychologically complex character backstory with formative experiences, defining wound, deepest fear, core lie, and key memories.

ChatGPTImage & Art
70 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a rich, psychologically complex backstory for this character: [NAME, ROLE, BASIC DESCRIPTION]. Develop: their childhood and formative experiences that shaped who they are, a defining wound or trauma and how they compensate for it, their deepest fear and greatest desire, the lie they believe about themselves or the world, key relationships that define them, and 3 specific memories that encapsulate their personality. Make them feel completely real and contradictory.
characterbackstoryfictionworldbuildingdepthwriting

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME, ROLE, BASIC DESCRIPTION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
