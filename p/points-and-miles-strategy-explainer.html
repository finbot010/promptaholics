Points and Miles Strategy Explainer

Explains how to use travel points and miles you already have for a specific trip. Educational only.

ChatGPTTravel
89 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me use my travel points and miles. Educational only; I will check current award prices and card terms myself.

Points and miles I have: [PROGRAMS AND BALANCES]
Trip: [FROM, TO, DATES, CABIN]
Flexibility: [DATES / AIRPORTS]

Explain: which of my programs could book this trip, transfer partners worth checking, how to judge whether a redemption is good value (cents per point vs cash price), taxes and fees to watch for, and step-by-step how to search award space. Warn me about common mistakes like transferring points before confirming availability.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PROGRAMS AND BALANCES], [FROM, TO, DATES, CABIN], [DATES / AIRPORTS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
