Newsletter Draft From Google Drive Notes

Turns your notes and recent work in Google Drive into a ready-to-edit newsletter.

GeminiEmail, Sales & Communication
76 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Drive Use my documents "[DOC NAMES]" to draft this week's newsletter.

Audience: [WHO SUBSCRIBES]
Newsletter style: [e.g. personal and casual, curated links, how-to]
Length: [WORDS]
Call to action: [WHAT I WANT READERS TO DO]

Give me: 5 subject lines with preview text, the newsletter with a personal opening, 2-3 sections based on my notes, and a clear call to action. Mark anything you inferred rather than took from my documents so I can check it.
newslettergoogle driveemail marketing

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DOC NAMES], [WHO SUBSCRIBES], [WORDS], [WHAT I WANT READERS TO DO].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
