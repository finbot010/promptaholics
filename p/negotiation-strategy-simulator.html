Negotiation Strategy Simulator

Roleplays your actual negotiation counterpart so you can practice before the real thing.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
142 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Act as my negotiation counterpart so I can practice before a real negotiation. 

1. First, based on what I tell you about the situation, adopt a realistic persona for the other party — their likely priorities, constraints, and negotiating style (not an easy pushover, not an unreasonable villain)
2. Negotiate with me realistically across multiple turns, including pushing back, making counter-offers, and using real tactics (anchoring, time pressure, bundling) where it fits their likely style
3. After I say "debrief," break character and give me honest feedback: what I did well, what a skilled negotiator would have done differently, and what leverage I didn't use

The situation: [DESCRIBE WHAT YOU'RE NEGOTIATING - salary, contract terms, a deal, etc.]
What you know about the other party: [THEIR LIKELY POSITION/CONSTRAINTS]
My goal and my walk-away point: [YOUR TARGETS]

Start the negotiation now, in character.
negotiationstrategyroleplay

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [THEIR LIKELY POSITION/CONSTRAINTS], [YOUR TARGETS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
