Home Staging on a Budget

Gives a room-by-room staging plan to make a home sell faster without spending much.

ClaudeReal Estate
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me stage my home for sale on a budget of [AMOUNT].

Home: [TYPE, ROOMS]
Current state: [e.g. lived-in, lots of furniture, dated colors, kids' toys everywhere]
Target buyers: [WHO]
Listing photos are on: [DATE]

Give me a room-by-room plan: what to remove, rearrange, clean and add; cheap upgrades with the biggest impact (light bulbs, paint, bedding, plants); curb appeal fixes; a photo-day checklist; and how to keep it show-ready while still living there.
home stagingselling a homedecor

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AMOUNT], [TYPE, ROOMS], [WHO], [DATE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
