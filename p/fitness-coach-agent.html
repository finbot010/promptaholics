Fitness Coach Agent

Elite personal trainer designs 12-week program with weekly split, daily workouts, nutrition framework, recovery protocols, progress tracking, and plateau troubleshooting.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
98 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Act as my elite personal fitness coach. My current situation: [FITNESS LEVEL / AVAILABLE TIME / EQUIPMENT / PHYSICAL LIMITATIONS / GOAL]. Design a complete 12-week training program including: a weekly training split optimized for my goal and schedule, specific workouts for each day with sets, reps, rest periods, and progression rules, a nutrition framework — not a rigid diet but flexible principles and macro targets, recovery protocols — sleep, mobility, active rest, how to track progress beyond the scale, and how to troubleshoot the most common plateaus I will likely hit at weeks 3, 6, and 9.

How to use this prompt
