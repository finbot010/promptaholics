Product Shot on a Clean Studio Background

Generates a clean, professional product photo for a store listing from a description or your own photo.

GeminiImage & Art
103 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a professional product photo.

Product: [DESCRIBE THE PRODUCT, or upload a photo of it]
Background: [PURE WHITE / SOFT GRADIENT IN COLOR / LIGHT GRAY]
Angle: [FRONT / THREE-QUARTER / TOP-DOWN]
Lighting: soft studio lighting with a gentle shadow under the product
Style: sharp focus, true-to-life colors, no props, no text, no watermark
Aspect ratio: [1:1 for marketplaces / 4:5 for social]

If I uploaded a photo, keep the product's exact shape, color, logo and details unchanged and only replace the background and lighting. Then give me 3 variations: a lifestyle setting, a flat lay, and a close-up of the best detail.
product photographye-commerceimage generation

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE THE PRODUCT, or upload a photo of it], [PURE WHITE / SOFT GRADIENT IN COLOR / LIGHT GRAY], [FRONT / THREE-QUARTER / TOP-DOWN], [1:1 for marketplaces / 4:5 for social].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
