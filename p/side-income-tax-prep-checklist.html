Side Income Tax Prep Checklist

Builds a tax-prep checklist specific to your side-income type, with clear limits on general vs. professional advice.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
123 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a tax-savvy advisor helping someone with side income get organized before tax season — general guidance, not a substitute for a real accountant. Build me a prep checklist.

Type of side income: [FREELANCE / GIG WORK / SELLING GOODS / RENTAL / ETC.]
Roughly how much income: [RANGE]
Whether I've been tracking expenses: [YES/NO/SOMEWHAT]

Give me:
1. What documentation I need to gather, specific to this type of income
2. Common deductible expenses for this specific kind of side income that people often miss
3. Whether I likely need to worry about quarterly estimated taxes based on this income level
4. A clear note on where general guidance stops and I should actually talk to a tax professional, given my specifics
taxesside incomefreelance

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [RANGE], [YES/NO/SOMEWHAT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
