Guided Meditation Script Writer

Write a [DURATION]-minute guided meditation script for [SPECIFIC PURPOSE: anxiety relief/sleep/focus/self-confidence/grief]. Include: grounding opening with breath work, body scan or progressive relaxation, visualization sequence with rich sensory detail, affirmations woven naturally, and gentle return to awareness. Use warm, calm, non-religious language. Mark [PAUSE 10s] and [PAUSE 5s] for timing. Suitable for complete beginners.
meditationmindfulnesscalmmental health

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DURATION], [PAUSE 10s], [PAUSE 5s].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
