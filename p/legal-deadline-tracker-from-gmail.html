Legal Deadline Tracker From Gmail

Finds contract renewals, notice periods and legal deadlines in your Gmail and puts them on your calendar.

GeminiLegal & Admin
91 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Gmail @Google Calendar Find emails from the last [NUMBER] months that mention contracts, renewals, notice periods, filing deadlines, court dates, insurance renewals or lease terms.

List each deadline in a table: what it is, the date, who it involves, what I need to do, and the email it came from. Flag auto-renewals I might want to cancel and notice periods (e.g. "30 days before renewal"). Then add calendar reminders 2 weeks and 2 days before each deadline. Mark any date you are unsure about so I can check the original document.
deadlinescontractsgmail

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
