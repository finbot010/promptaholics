Lab Results Plain-English Explainer

Explains what each value on a lab report means and which questions to ask your doctor about it.

ClaudeHealth & Wellness
93 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Explain my lab results in plain English. I know you cannot diagnose me and my doctor will interpret them in context.

Age and sex: [DETAILS]
Lab report (values and the reference ranges shown):
[PASTE — remove name and ID numbers]

For each result: what the test measures, what my value means compared with the range shown on my report, and common non-serious reasons it can be slightly off. Group results by body system. Then list the results worth discussing with my doctor and the questions to ask. Do not suggest treatments or doses.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DETAILS], [PASTE — remove name and ID numbers].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
