The Ultimate Brainstorm Session

A structured four-round creative brainstorming session that consistently produces breakthrough ideas — used by innovation teams.

ChatGPTMore Prompts★ Featured
108 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Facilitate a world-class brainstorming session for [PROBLEM/OPPORTUNITY].

Context: [BACKGROUND INFORMATION]
Goal: [WHAT WE ARE TRYING TO ACHIEVE]
Constraints: [BUDGET/TIME/RESOURCES]
What we have already tried: [PREVIOUS ATTEMPTS]
Wild card: [ONE CRAZY THING WE HAVE CONSIDERED]

Run four brainstorming rounds:

Round 1 — QUANTITY (20 ideas, no judgment, even bad ones)
Round 2 — STEAL & ADAPT (how would Apple/Disney/NASA/a 5-year-old solve this?)
Round 3 — INVERSION (how would we make this problem worse? Now reverse it)
Round 4 — SYNTHESIS (combine 3 of the best ideas into one hybrid solution)

End with: Top 3 ideas ranked by impact vs effort, and the very next action for the best one.
brainstormcreativityideasproblem solvinginnovation

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PROBLEM/OPPORTUNITY], [BACKGROUND INFORMATION], [WHAT WE ARE TRYING TO ACHIEVE], [BUDGET/TIME/RESOURCES], [PREVIOUS ATTEMPTS], [ONE CRAZY THING WE HAVE CONSIDERED].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
