Recovery & Rest Day Optimizer

Complete recovery protocol covering active rest, sleep, nutrition timing, mobility, and deload strategy.

Design an optimal recovery protocol for my training.

Sport/activity: [WHAT YOU TRAIN — weightlifting / running / CrossFit / sports / mixed]
Training frequency: [DAYS PER WEEK]
Current recovery methods: [WHAT YOU DO NOW]
Problems: [SORENESS / POOR SLEEP / SLOW PROGRESS / INJURY PRONE]
Age: [AGE] | Budget for recovery: $[MONTHLY]
Available time for recovery work: [MINUTES PER DAY]

Optimize:
1. Active recovery protocol (rest day activities)
2. Sleep optimization for athletes
3. Nutrition timing for recovery (what to eat post-workout and before bed)
4. Supplementation evidence review (what actually works)
5. Mobility and stretching routine (10-15 min daily)
6. Cold/heat therapy guide (if applicable)
7. Weekly deload structure
8. Signs you're under-recovering and need an extra rest day

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DAYS PER WEEK], [WHAT YOU DO NOW], [AGE], [MONTHLY], [MINUTES PER DAY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
