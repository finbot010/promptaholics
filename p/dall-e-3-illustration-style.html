DALL-E 3 Illustration Style

Optimized DALL-E 3 prompting strategy with revision instructions — gets consistently better results than basic prompts.

AIImage & Art
73 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a [STYLE: editorial illustration/children's book/infographic/conceptual art] for [SUBJECT/CONCEPT] in DALL-E 3.

Style details: [DESCRIBE THE VISUAL STYLE]
Color palette: [COLORS OR MOOD]
Composition: [HOW TO FRAME IT]
Mood and tone: [EMOTIONAL QUALITY]
Purpose: [WHERE THIS WILL BE USED]

Specify:
- No text in the image unless specified
- [PORTRAIT/LANDSCAPE/SQUARE] orientation
- [PHOTOREALISTIC/ILLUSTRATED/ABSTRACT] render style

After generating, ask DALL-E to revise: make the [specific element] more [specific quality] while keeping the overall composition.
DALL-E 3illustrationeditorialstyledigital art

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SUBJECT/CONCEPT], [DESCRIBE THE VISUAL STYLE], [COLORS OR MOOD], [HOW TO FRAME IT], [EMOTIONAL QUALITY], [WHERE THIS WILL BE USED], [PORTRAIT/LANDSCAPE/SQUARE], [PHOTOREALISTIC/ILLUSTRATED/ABSTRACT] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and send it.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
