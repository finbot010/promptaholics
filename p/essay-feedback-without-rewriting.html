Essay Feedback Without Rewriting

Gives detailed, teacher-style feedback on an essay so the student improves it themselves.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
80 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Give feedback on this essay like a supportive teacher. Do not rewrite it for me.

Assignment prompt: [PASTE]
Grade level / course: [LEVEL]
Rubric (if any): [PASTE]
Essay:
[PASTE]

Feedback on: thesis clarity, structure and flow, evidence and analysis, counterarguments, style and clarity, grammar patterns (show 2 examples, not every error). For each area, say what works, what to improve, and one question that pushes my thinking. End with the 3 changes that would raise the grade most, in order.
essaywriting feedbackstudents

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE], [LEVEL].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
