The STAR Method Interview Answer Builder

Structures a messy work story into a clean, interview-ready STAR answer.

ChatGPTCareer & Resume
53 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want to answer an interview question about a time I [BRIEFLY DESCRIBE THE SITUATION, e.g. 'resolved a conflict between two team members']. Help me structure this into a STAR answer: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Ask me clarifying questions first if you need more detail on any part before writing the final answer.
interview prepSTAR methodcareer

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
