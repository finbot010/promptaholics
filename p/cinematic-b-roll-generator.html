Generate 10 cinematic B-roll shot prompts for a video about [TOPIC] using Runway or Kling. Each prompt should specify: subject in motion, camera movement, depth of field, time of day, color temperature, and the story beat this shot supports. Include the emotional rhythm across all 10 shots.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and send it.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
