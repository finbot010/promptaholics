Partnership or Co-Founder Agreement Outline

Outlines the key terms co-founders or business partners should agree on, with questions to discuss first.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help my business partner(s) and me outline an agreement. We will have a lawyer draft the final version.

Business: [DESCRIPTION]
Partners and roles: [NAMES / ROLES]
Money and time each puts in: [DETAILS]
Country/state: [LOCATION]

Give me: the 12 questions we must agree on (ownership split, vesting, roles and decision-making, salaries, adding partners, what happens if someone leaves, disputes, IP ownership, non-compete, exit and sale); options for each with pros and cons; common mistakes partners regret; and a one-page term outline we can take to a lawyer.
co-founderpartnershipbusiness law

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIPTION], [NAMES / ROLES], [DETAILS], [LOCATION].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
