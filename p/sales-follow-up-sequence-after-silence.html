Sales Follow-Up Sequence After Silence

Builds a multi-touch follow-up sequence where each email adds something new instead of just 'checking in.'

ChatGPTEmail, Sales & Communication
123 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a sales rep who's good at re-engaging a prospect who's gone quiet without sounding needy or annoyed. Build me a follow-up sequence.

What was discussed/proposed: [BRIEF CONTEXT]
How long they've been silent: [TIMEFRAME]
Number of follow-ups I want in the sequence: [NUMBER]

Write each follow-up email so that:
1. None of them say "just following up" or "checking in" — every one adds something new (information, a different angle, a lower-commitment ask)
2. They get progressively shorter, not longer
3. The final one in the sequence is a genuine "breakup" email that gives them a graceful exit while leaving the door open
4. None of them guilt-trip or imply they've wasted my time

Space out the suggested timing between each email.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BRIEF CONTEXT], [TIMEFRAME], [NUMBER].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
