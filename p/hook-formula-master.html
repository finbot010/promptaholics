Hook Formula Master

10 proven hook formulas that stop the scroll — essential for any content creator or copywriter.

You are a master of attention and hooks. For the topic [TOPIC], write 10 different opening hooks using these proven formulas:

1. The Curiosity Gap: Tease information they need but do not have
2. The Bold Claim: State something counterintuitive
3. The Relatable Struggle: Make them feel seen
4. The Shocking Stat: Open with a number that reframes everything
5. The Story Open: Start in the middle of action
6. The Direct Question: Make them answer in their head
7. The Controversial Take: Say what others are afraid to say
8. The Before/After: Paint the contrast
9. The Listicle Tease: Promise specific value
10. The Empathy Open: Validate their exact feeling

For each: write the hook and explain which emotion it triggers.
