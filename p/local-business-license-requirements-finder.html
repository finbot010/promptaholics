Local Business License Requirements Finder

Searches current official sources for the licenses and permits you need to start your business locally.

GeminiLegal & Admin
103 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Find the licenses and permits I need to start my business.

Business type: [DESCRIBE, e.g. home bakery, mobile car detailing, online store, food truck]
Location: [CITY, COUNTY, STATE / COUNTRY]
Run from: [HOME / RETAIL SPACE / ONLINE ONLY / MOBILE]
Employees: [YES / NO]

Search official government sources and list: business registration, local business license, zoning or home-occupation rules, industry-specific permits (health, food, alcohol, professional licenses), sales tax registration, and employer registrations if I hire. For each: who issues it, approximate cost and time, and the official link. Tell me which ones to confirm with my city or a small business center.
business licensepermitssmall business

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY, COUNTY, STATE / COUNTRY], [HOME / RETAIL SPACE / ONLINE ONLY / MOBILE], [YES / NO].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
