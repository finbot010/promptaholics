The Contract Renewal Negotiation

Opens a contract renewal negotiation with enough lead time to actually negotiate.

ClaudeBusiness & Strategy
41 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Our contract with [vendor/client] renews on [date] at [current terms]. I want to renegotiate [specific term — price, scope, length] before renewing. Draft an opening message that raises this with enough lead time to negotiate properly, not at the last minute.
contractsrenewalsnegotiation

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
