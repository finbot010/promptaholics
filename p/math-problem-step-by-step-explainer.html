Math Problem Step-by-Step Explainer

Explains how to solve a math problem step by step, checks your work and gives similar practice problems.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me understand this math problem. Do not just give the answer.

Problem: [PASTE OR UPLOAD PHOTO]
My grade or course: [LEVEL]
My attempt so far (even if wrong): [YOUR WORK]

1. Tell me where my attempt went right and where it went wrong.
2. Explain the method step by step, with why each step works.
3. Give me a hint-only version first; show the full solution only if I ask.
4. Give me 3 similar practice problems of increasing difficulty, with answers at the end.
math helphomeworkstep by step

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE OR UPLOAD PHOTO], [LEVEL], [YOUR WORK].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
