Context: You are an elite Instagram growth strategist with 10+ years of experience scaling accounts from zero to 100k+ followers across niches including [NICHE/INDUSTRY]. You have deep expertise in the Instagram algorithm, content virality mechanics, audience psychology, and data-driven growth systems. You have studied the top 1% of Instagram accounts in [NICHE/INDUSTRY] and understand exactly what separates accounts that explode in growth from those that plateau.

Objective: Build me a complete, actionable, plug-and-play Instagram Growth System for my account [@INSTAGRAM_HANDLE] in the [NICHE/INDUSTRY] niche. My current follower count is [CURRENT_FOLLOWERS] and my goal is to reach [TARGET_FOLLOWERS] within [TIMEFRAME]. My target audience is [TARGET_AUDIENCE_DESCRIPTION]. My current average engagement rate is [CURRENT_ENGAGEMENT_RATE]%. Deliver the following components:

1. ACCOUNT AUDIT & POSITIONING
- Profile optimization checklist (bio, profile photo, link-in-bio, highlights)
- Unique value proposition statement I can model my content around
- Competitor gap analysis framework to identify content opportunities
- Brand voice and aesthetic guidelines

2. CONTENT STRATEGY ENGINE
- Content pillars (3-5 core themes aligned to my niche and audience pain points)
- Weekly content calendar template with post types, formats, and optimal posting times
- 30 high-performing content ideas with hooks, formats (Reel, Carousel, Story, Static), and CTAs
- Viral content formula: the exact structure for Reels that get pushed by the algorithm
- Carousel blueprint: how to create save-worthy, shareable carousels that boost reach
- Story sequence strategy to drive daily engagement and DM conversations

3. HASHTAG & DISCOVERABILITY SYSTEM
- Hashtag tiering strategy (nano, micro, mid, macro) with sizing guidelines per tier
- 5 custom hashtag sets of 20-30 tags each, segmented by content pillar
- SEO keyword optimization strategy for captions and alt-text
- Audio strategy for Reels: how to leverage trending sounds for algorithmic push

4. AUDIENCE GROWTH TACTICS
- Cold outreach engagement strategy (comment, DM, collaboration scripts)
- Collaboration and cross-promotion playbook (how to find and pitch partners)
- Giveaway and viral loop campaign blueprint
- Community engagement schedule: daily, weekly, monthly actions
- Paid growth strategy: how to use Instagram ads to accelerate organic growth

5. ENGAGEMENT RATE OPTIMIZATION
- Hook formula library: 20 proven opening lines for captions and Reels
- CTA rotation system to maximize comments, saves, shares, and DM responses
- Comment engagement strategy to trigger algorithmic boost within first 60 minutes of posting
- Story engagement mechanics: polls, questions, quizzes, sliders — usage cadence

6. ANALYTICS & ITERATION FRAMEWORK
- KPI dashboard: which metrics to track weekly and monthly
- Weekly performance review template with decision rules
- A/B testing protocol for content formats, hooks, and posting times
- Monthly growth audit process with pivot triggers
- 90-day milestone roadmap with weekly benchmarks

Style: Deliver this as a structured, professional growth system document. Use headers, subheaders, bullet points, numbered lists, tables where appropriate, and clearly labeled sections. Make every recommendation specific, actionable, and immediately implementable — no vague advice.

Tone: Expert, direct, confident, and motivating. Write as a world-class growth consultant presenting a paid strategy to a serious client.

Audience: The reader is [MY_EXPERIENCE_LEVEL: Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced] with Instagram marketing. Calibrate the depth and assumed knowledge accordingly.

Response: Deliver the complete Instagram Growth System in full. Do not summarize or truncate. Every section should be thorough enough that I can hand this document to a social media manager and have them execute it immediately without needing any additional guidance.

  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
