The Mood Painter

Detailed mood board concept with hex codes, font pairings, textures, and anti-references — designer-ready.

ChatGPTImage & Art
107 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a detailed mood board concept for [PROJECT/BRAND/STORY]. Define: (1) Color Palette — 5 core colors with hex codes and the emotion each evokes, (2) Typography Direction — 2 font pairings (heading + body) with personality descriptions, (3) Visual Texture — materials, patterns, and surfaces that define the aesthetic, (4) Photography Style — lighting, composition, subject treatment, (5) Inspirational References — describe 5 specific images that capture the vibe, (6) Keywords — 10 adjective pairs that define the mood, (7) What This Is NOT — 3 anti-references to clarify boundaries. Make it so specific that any designer could execute the vision perfectly without a single follow-up question.
mood boardcreative directionvisual identityart directionaesthetic

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PROJECT/BRAND/STORY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
