Daily Brief From Gmail and Calendar

Creates a short morning brief of today's meetings, urgent emails and the one thing to focus on.

GeminiAI Agents & Automation
75 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Calendar @Gmail Give me my daily brief for today.

Include:
1. Today's meetings with time, who, and what I need to prepare for each.
2. Emails from the last 24 hours that need action today.
3. Deadlines today or tomorrow mentioned in my email or calendar.
4. Free blocks of 60+ minutes for focused work.
5. The one thing I should focus on today, based on these priorities: [PRIORITIES].
Keep it under 200 words.
daily planningmorning briefgmail

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRIORITIES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
