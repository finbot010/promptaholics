Travel Journal and Memory Keeper

Turns quick daily notes and photos from a trip into a well-written travel journal.

ClaudeTravel
75 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Turn my trip notes into a travel journal.

Trip: [WHERE AND WHEN]
Daily notes (rough is fine):
[PASTE]
Tone: [FUNNY / REFLECTIVE / FOR FAMILY / FOR A BLOG]

For each day: a title, a 150-250 word entry that keeps my details and voice, and a highlight line. End with a reflection on the whole trip and a list of recommendations for someone going there. Do not add events that are not in my notes.
travel journalmemorieswriting

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHERE AND WHEN], [PASTE], [FUNNY / REFLECTIVE / FOR FAMILY / FOR A BLOG].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
