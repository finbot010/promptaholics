Picky Eater Meal Ideas for Kids

Suggests meals and gentle strategies for picky eaters without turning dinner into a battle.

GeminiFood & Cooking
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
My [AGE]-year-old is a picky eater.

Foods they reliably eat: [LIST]
Foods they refuse: [LIST]
Textures or colors they avoid: [DETAILS]
Family dinner style: [WE ALL EAT THE SAME / I COOK SEPARATELY]

Give me 10 meals that start from their safe foods and add one small new element each time, a week of dinners the whole family can eat, low-pressure ways to introduce new foods, and phrases to use and avoid at the table. Mention when it's worth asking a pediatrician about picky eating.
picky eaterskids mealstoddlers

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AGE], [LIST], [DETAILS], [WE ALL EAT THE SAME / I COOK SEPARATELY].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
