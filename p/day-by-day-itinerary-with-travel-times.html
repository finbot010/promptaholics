Day-by-Day Itinerary With Travel Times

Builds a realistic day-by-day itinerary that groups nearby sights and includes real travel times from Google Maps.

GeminiTravel
80 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Plan a [NUMBER]-day itinerary for [CITY].

Staying at: [HOTEL OR AREA]
Interests: [FOOD / HISTORY / ART / NATURE / SHOPPING / NIGHTLIFE]
Pace: [RELAXED / MODERATE / PACKED]
Getting around: [WALKING / TRANSIT / CAR]
Must-sees: [LIST]

Group sights that are close together each day, include travel times between stops, a lunch and dinner suggestion near each day's route, opening hours or closed days to watch for, and one rainy-day alternative. Keep each day realistic, with breaks.
itinerarygoogle mapstrip planning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER], [CITY], [HOTEL OR AREA], [RELAXED / MODERATE / PACKED], [WALKING / TRANSIT / CAR], [LIST].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
