Personality Analyzer

Uncovers your personality traits, blind spots, and communication style through writing analysis.

ChatGPTEducation & Learning
102 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Analyze my personality based on how I write and communicate.

I will give you 3 writing samples from different contexts — a work email, a casual text, and a personal reflection.

[PASTE SAMPLE 1 — Work]
[PASTE SAMPLE 2 — Casual]
[PASTE SAMPLE 3 — Personal]

From these, tell me:
- My likely communication style and personality traits
- My biggest strengths in how I connect with others
- My blind spots and potential friction points
- What I probably value most in relationships and work
- One thing most people probably misread about me

Be honest and specific. Avoid vague generalities.
personalityself awarenesspsychologyanalysis

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE SAMPLE 1 — Work], [PASTE SAMPLE 2 — Casual], [PASTE SAMPLE 3 — Personal].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
