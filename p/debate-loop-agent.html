Debate Loop Agent

Debate loop that argues for, then against, then synthesizes a final evidence-based position.

ClaudeAI Agents & Automation
83 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a debate loop agent. Topic: [YOUR TOPIC OR DECISION].

Run 3 rounds:

ROUND 1 — Argue FOR the position as strongly as possible. Best evidence, strongest logic.

ROUND 2 — Argue AGAINST the position as strongly as possible. Steelman the opposition.

ROUND 3 — Synthesize both sides. What is actually true? What does the evidence support? Make a final recommendation with your confidence level (Low/Medium/High).

Do not hedge in rounds 1 and 2 — commit fully to each side before synthesizing.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR TOPIC OR DECISION].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
