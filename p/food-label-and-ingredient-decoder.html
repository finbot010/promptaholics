Food Label and Ingredient Decoder

Explains a food product's ingredients and nutrition label so you can compare products quickly.

GeminiFood & Cooking
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Explain this food label to me. Educational only; if I have a medical diet, I will check with my doctor or dietitian.

Product: [NAME]
Ingredients list and nutrition facts: [PASTE OR UPLOAD A PHOTO]
What I care about: [e.g. added sugar, sodium, protein, allergens, fewer additives]

Tell me: what each unfamiliar ingredient is and why it is used; what the nutrition numbers mean per realistic serving; allergen warnings; and how it compares with typical similar products. Suggest 2 things to look for when comparing alternatives.
nutrition labelsingredientsgrocery

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME], [PASTE OR UPLOAD A PHOTO].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
