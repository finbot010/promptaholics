The Talking-Head Script Structurer

Structures rough notes into a spoken-language video script with hook, body, and close.

ClaudeVideo & Film
52 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Turn these rough notes into a talking-head YouTube script structure for a video about [topic], target length [X] minutes: '[paste notes]'. Structure: hook, promise (what they'll get), 3 main points with a transition line between each, and a close with a specific next action. Write it as spoken language, not an essay.
