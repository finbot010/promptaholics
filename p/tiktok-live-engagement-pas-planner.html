Act as a TikTok Live engagement expert. Using the PAS framework, help me plan interactive scene elements for my live stream. Problem: My viewers drop off after [DROP_OFF_TIME] minutes. Agitate: Without engagement hooks, I lose followers and gifts. Solution: Create a scene-by-scene engagement plan for a [STREAM_LENGTH] minute live stream in the [NICHE] niche. Include: opening scene setup, mid-stream interactive scene transitions, engagement triggers like polls or challenges, visual cues for gifts and follows, and a closing scene. My current average viewers are [AVERAGE_VIEWERS].

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DROP_OFF_TIME], [STREAM_LENGTH], [NICHE], [AVERAGE_VIEWERS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
