Content Batching Plan for Creators

Plans one batching session that produces a month of content across platforms.

ClaudeMarketing & Branding
84 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want to create a month of content in one or two sessions.

Platforms: [LIST]
Posts per week per platform: [NUMBERS]
Content types I like making: [TEXT POSTS, SHORT VIDEOS, CAROUSELS, NEWSLETTER]
Time I have for batching: [HOURS]
Topics: [MY CONTENT PILLARS]

Give me: a batching session schedule hour by hour; how to turn one long piece (a video or article) into 10+ smaller posts; a naming and folder system; a posting calendar for the month; and a quick checklist before scheduling each post.
content batchingcreator workflowconsistency

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST], [NUMBERS], [TEXT POSTS, SHORT VIDEOS, CAROUSELS, NEWSLETTER], [HOURS], [MY CONTENT PILLARS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
