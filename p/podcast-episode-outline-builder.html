Podcast Episode Outline Builder

Creates complete podcast episode outlines with production notes.

55 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a podcast producer and content strategist. Build a complete episode outline for: [PASTE TOPIC, GUEST IF ANY, TARGET AUDIENCE]. Include: episode title and hook, cold open teaser, intro segment, 4-6 main talking points with sub-questions, 2-3 tangent prevention notes, sponsored segment placement, listener Q&A section, key takeaways, outro CTA, and show notes draft.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE TOPIC, GUEST IF ANY, TARGET AUDIENCE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
