College Savings Plan for Parents

Estimates what to save for a child's education and builds a monthly plan.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
101 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me plan savings for my child's education.

Child's age: [AGE]
Country: [COUNTRY]
Type of school I'm planning for: [PUBLIC / PRIVATE / ABROAD / TRADE SCHOOL / UNSURE]
How much I want to cover: [ALL / HALF / A SPECIFIC AMOUNT]
Already saved: [AMOUNT]
What I can save monthly now: [AMOUNT]

Estimate a target (show your assumptions about cost growth and returns), the monthly amount needed, and what my current amount gets me. Explain the types of education savings accounts that commonly exist in my country so I can look them up, and how to balance this against retirement savings.
college savingseducation fundkids

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AGE], [COUNTRY], [PUBLIC / PRIVATE / ABROAD / TRADE SCHOOL / UNSURE], [ALL / HALF / A SPECIFIC AMOUNT], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
