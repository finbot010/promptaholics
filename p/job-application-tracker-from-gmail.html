Job Application Tracker From Gmail

Finds your job application emails in Gmail and builds a tracker with status and follow-up dates.

GeminiCareer & Resume
72 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Gmail Find emails about my job applications from the last [NUMBER] weeks (confirmations, interview invites, rejections, recruiter messages).

Build a table: company, role, date applied, current status, last contact, next step, and follow-up date (7 days after last contact if no reply). Flag interview invites I have not answered and applications with no response after 2 weeks. Format it for Google Sheets and draft 2 follow-up emails for the oldest open applications.
job searchgmailtracking

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
