Thought Leadership Content Calendar

Creates a 30-day thought leadership content calendar for LinkedIn.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
79 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a content strategist specializing in thought leadership. Create a 30-day LinkedIn content calendar for me. My expertise: [YOUR FIELD AND KNOWLEDGE AREAS]. My audience: [DESCRIBE YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE]. My brand pillars: [LIST 3 TOPICS TO BE KNOWN FOR]. My goal: [GROW FOLLOWERS / ATTRACT CLIENTS / BUILD CREDIBILITY]. For each week provide 5 post ideas with hooks, content type, engagement potential, and the underlying message each post reinforces. Include 2 viral post formulas adapted to my niche.
thought leadershipcontent calendarLinkedInpersonal brand

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR FIELD AND KNOWLEDGE AREAS], [DESCRIBE YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE], [LIST 3 TOPICS TO BE KNOWN FOR].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
