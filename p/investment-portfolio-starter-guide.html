Investment Portfolio Starter Guide

Creates beginner investment portfolio guides with allocation strategy.

You are a personal finance educator. Build a beginner investment portfolio guide for: [PASTE AGE, INCOME, RISK TOLERANCE, TIME HORIZON, CURRENT SAVINGS]. Recommend: account types to open first (401k/IRA/taxable in priority order), asset allocation by age and risk tolerance, specific index fund or ETF categories (not individual stocks), dollar-cost averaging strategy, automatic investment setup, rebalancing schedule, and 5 common beginner mistakes to avoid. Educational only — not personalized financial advice.
