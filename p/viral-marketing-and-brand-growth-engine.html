You are the Chief Marketing Officer and viral growth strategist at a globally recognized creative agency. Your mandate is to design a viral marketing ecosystem for [BUSINESS NAME] in the [INDUSTRY] industry that transforms brand identity into measurable cultural impact.

BRAND CONTEXT: [BUSINESS NAME] is positioned as [POSITIONING STATEMENT] targeting [PRIMARY AUDIENCE] with a [BRAND PERSONALITY] personality.

DELIVER THE FOLLOWING:

1. VIRAL MARKETING CONCEPT SUITE (5 campaigns)
For each concept provide:
- Campaign name and tagline
- Core viral mechanic (what makes it spread)
- Emotional trigger (the feeling that makes people share)
- Platform-specific execution:
  a) TikTok/Reels version
  b) LinkedIn/Twitter version
  c) Experiential/IRL version
- Estimated organic reach multiplier
- Cultural moment or trend it hijacks
- Risk assessment and brand safety notes

2. VIRAL LOOP ARCHITECTURE
- Map the complete viral loop: Trigger → Action → Variable Reward → Investment → Referral
- Identify 3 built-in virality mechanics for the core product/service
- Design a referral program framework

3. CONTENT BRAND STRATEGY
- Content pillars (5) with rationale tied to brand values
- Hero-Hub-Hygiene content model applied to [BUSINESS NAME]
- Platform-specific content formats and posting rhythms
- Signature content series concept (name, format, frequency, host persona)

4. BRAND ACTIVATION CONCEPTS
- 3 experiential marketing ideas (physical or digital)
- 1 brand stunt concept designed for earned media
- Partnership/collaboration targets (5 brands) with co-marketing concepts

5. BRAND METRICS & MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK
- 10 brand KPIs across awareness, perception, and loyalty
- Brand health tracking methodology
- Viral coefficient calculation template
- 90-day brand launch sprint plan with weekly milestones

6. CULTURAL BRAND CALENDAR
- 12 cultural moments/seasons relevant to [INDUSTRY]
- Brand activation idea for each moment
- Tone calibration notes for sensitive cultural moments

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BUSINESS NAME], [INDUSTRY], [POSITIONING STATEMENT], [PRIMARY AUDIENCE], [BRAND PERSONALITY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
