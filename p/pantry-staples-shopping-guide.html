Pantry Staples Shopping Guide

Builds a pantry staples list for your cooking style and budget so you can make meals without daily shopping.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me stock my pantry.

How I cook: [CUISINES I MAKE MOST]
Household: [NUMBER OF PEOPLE]
Storage space: [SMALL / MEDIUM / LARGE]
Budget to start: [AMOUNT]
Dietary needs: [DETAILS]

Give me: a staples list by category (grains, canned goods, oils and vinegars, spices, sauces, baking, freezer), quantities to start with, what to buy first if my budget is tight, how long each lasts, and 10 meals I can make from the pantry alone.
pantrystocking a kitchenbudget cooking

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CUISINES I MAKE MOST], [NUMBER OF PEOPLE], [SMALL / MEDIUM / LARGE], [AMOUNT], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
