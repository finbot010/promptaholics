The Small Business Legal Starter Kit

Complete legal startup checklist — structure, registrations, documents, insurance, taxes, and compliance calendar.

ChatGPTLegal & Admin
109 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm starting a [BUSINESS TYPE] in [STATE]. Create a comprehensive legal checklist covering: (1) Business Structure — pros/cons of LLC vs S-Corp vs Sole Proprietorship for my specific situation, (2) Required Registrations — federal EIN, state registrations, local permits and licenses, (3) Essential Documents — every legal document I need from day one, (4) Insurance Requirements — what coverage I need and why, (5) Tax Obligations — quarterly filings, sales tax, employment tax, (6) Compliance Calendar — month-by-month legal deadlines for year one, (7) Common Legal Mistakes — the top 5 things new business owners in my industry get sued for. Note: Consult a licensed attorney for state-specific advice.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BUSINESS TYPE], [STATE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
