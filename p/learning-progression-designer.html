Learning Progression Designer

Map the complete learning progression for [SKILL] from novice to expert.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
46 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Map the complete learning progression for [SKILL] from novice to expert. Define 5 developmental stages, the characteristic errors at each stage, the instructional move that helps a learner progress to the next stage, and the assessment task that reliably places a student in their current stage.
progressionscaffoldingcurriculum

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SKILL].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
