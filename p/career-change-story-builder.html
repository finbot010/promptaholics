Career Change Story Builder

Builds a convincing story for switching careers, mapping transferable skills to the new role.

ClaudeCareer & Resume
100 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me explain a career change convincingly.

From: [CURRENT FIELD AND ROLE]
To: [TARGET FIELD AND ROLE]
Experience I have: [SUMMARY]
Steps I have already taken (courses, projects, volunteering): [LIST]
What worries me about how employers will see me: [CONCERNS]

Give me: a skills map (old skill → how it applies in the new role → proof I can show), a 60-second "why I am switching" answer, a LinkedIn headline and About section, 3 gaps I should close first with the fastest way to close each, and answers to the 3 toughest questions an interviewer will ask about my switch.
career changetransferable skillsinterview

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CURRENT FIELD AND ROLE], [TARGET FIELD AND ROLE], [SUMMARY], [LIST], [CONCERNS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
