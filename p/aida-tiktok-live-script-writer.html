Include: an attention-grabbing opening line, an interest-building segment with talking points, a desire-driven interactive activity such as polls or challenges, and a strong call-to-action closing. How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DURATION], [LIVE THEME].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
