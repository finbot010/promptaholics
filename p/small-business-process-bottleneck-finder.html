Small Business Process Bottleneck Finder

Maps how work flows through your small business and finds the bottlenecks costing you time.

ClaudeBusiness & Strategy
82 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me find bottlenecks in my business.

Business: [WHAT YOU DO, TEAM SIZE]
How an order or client moves through the business, step by step: [DESCRIBE]
Where things get delayed or go wrong: [EXAMPLES]
Tools used: [LIST]

Map the process; mark each step as value-adding, necessary admin, or waste; find the top 3 bottlenecks with the time or money they cost; suggest fixes (simplify, automate, delegate, template) with effort and impact; and give me a 30-day plan to fix the biggest one.
operationsefficiencysmall business

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT YOU DO, TEAM SIZE], [DESCRIBE], [EXAMPLES], [LIST].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
