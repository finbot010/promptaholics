Money Goals Planner

Turns several money goals into a clear priority order and a monthly savings plan for each.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
77 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me plan my money goals.

Goals (what, amount, by when): [LIST, e.g. emergency fund, vacation, car, wedding, house deposit]
Monthly amount I can save: [AMOUNT]
Current savings and debts: [DETAILS]

Put the goals in a sensible priority order with reasons; show how much to put toward each per month; when each goal will be reached; what to do if my income changes; and where to keep the money for each goal (short-term vs long-term). Educational only.
financial goalssavings planplanning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AMOUNT], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
