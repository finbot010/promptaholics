AI Business Idea Machine

Silicon Valley advisor generates 10 AI startup ideas with business model, pricing, MVP, and growth strategy for each.

Act as a Silicon Valley startup advisor.

Generate 10 AI startup ideas based on:
- current market trends
- underserved niches
- low competition opportunities
- recurring revenue potential

For each idea include:
- business model
- target audience
- pricing strategy
- MVP concept
- growth strategy
