Moving Abroad Relocation Checklist

Builds a relocation checklist for moving to another country, from paperwork to the first week.

GeminiLifestyle, Fun & Games
94 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm moving from [COUNTRY] to [COUNTRY] around [DATE].

Who's moving: [ME / FAMILY WITH KIDS / PETS]
Reason: [WORK / RETIREMENT / STUDY / REMOTE WORK / FAMILY]
What I'm bringing: [EVERYTHING / A FEW BOXES / JUST SUITCASES]

Give me a timeline checklist starting 6 months out: visas and residency (things to verify officially), documents to collect and get apostilled or translated, banking and money transfer, health insurance, shipping vs selling belongings, pets, phone and mail, taxes to ask an accountant about, and a first-week-on-arrival list. Flag the items that take the longest.
moving abroadrelocationexpat

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [COUNTRY], [DATE], [ME / FAMILY WITH KIDS / PETS], [WORK / RETIREMENT / STUDY / REMOTE WORK / FAMILY], [EVERYTHING / A FEW BOXES / JUST SUITCASES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
