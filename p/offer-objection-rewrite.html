Offer Objection Rewrite

Rewrite these objections and responses to be more persuasive, empathetic, and conversion-focused. Objections: [LIST OBJECTIONS]. For each: rewrite the acknowledgment to sound more empathetic, reframe the objection as a sign of the right buyer, rewrite the response with specific evidence, add a story that dissolves it emotionally, and write a preemptive sales page version. Make each response feel like a trusted advisor, not a sales pitch.
objectionssalespersuasionrewrite

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST OBJECTIONS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
