Restaurant Review Writer

Writes professional restaurant reviews with dish critiques.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
71 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a food critic. Write a compelling restaurant review for: [PASTE RESTAURANT NAME, TYPE, DISHES TRIED, EXPERIENCE]. Structure: opening hook (not 'I went to...'), restaurant concept and atmosphere paragraph, 3 dishes reviewed in detail (appearance, aroma, taste, technique), service and overall experience, value assessment, comparison to similar restaurants, and verdict (who should go, when, what to order). Tone: authoritative but approachable. Length: 500 words. Avoid clichés like 'burst of flavor.'
foodrestaurant reviewwritingcritique

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
