Demand Letter for Unpaid Invoice

Writes a firm, professional demand letter for an overdue invoice with a clear deadline and next steps.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
134 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a demand letter for an unpaid invoice. Firm and professional, not threatening or emotional.

From: [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS]
To: [CLIENT NAME / COMPANY]
Invoice number(s), date(s), amount(s): [DETAILS]
Work delivered: [SHORT DESCRIPTION + DELIVERY DATE]
Previous reminders sent: [DATES AND HOW]
Contract or written agreement: [YES/NO — QUOTE PAYMENT TERMS IF YES]
Payment deadline I will give: [e.g. 10 business days]
What I will do if unpaid: [e.g. small claims court, collections agency, late fee per contract]
Jurisdiction: [COUNTRY/STATE]

Include: a factual timeline, the exact amount owed (plus late fees only if the contract allows them), how to pay, the deadline, and the next step I will take. Then give me a shorter email version, and a note on anything in my situation that a lawyer should check before I send it.
invoicepaymentcollections

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS], [CLIENT NAME / COMPANY], [DETAILS], [SHORT DESCRIPTION + DELIVERY DATE], [DATES AND HOW], [YES/NO — QUOTE PAYMENT TERMS IF YES], [COUNTRY/STATE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
