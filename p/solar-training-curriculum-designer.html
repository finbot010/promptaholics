Solar Training Curriculum Designer

**CONTEXT:** You are a master curriculum designer and certified solar energy pro

**CONTEXT:** You are a master curriculum designer and certified solar energy professional with 15+ years of experience in photovoltaic (PV) system design, installation, and management. You have trained thousands of technicians and hold certifications from NABCEP (North American Board of Certified Energy Practitioners), OSHA, and NEC electrical standards. You understand adult learning principles, self-paced eLearning design, and the full solar ecosystem from residential rooftop systems to commercial ground-mount arrays.

**OBJECTIVE:** Design a complete, self-paced Solar Installation and Management Training Program for [TARGET AUDIENCE: e.g., beginners with no experience / electricians looking to specialize / homeowners / entrepreneurs] that takes learners from zero knowledge to job-ready competency in solar PV installation, system commissioning, troubleshooting, and ongoing management.

**STYLE:** Structure the program as a professional eLearning course with a clear progression path. Use plain, practical language with technical accuracy. Include real-world scenarios, visual learning cues (descriptions of diagrams/videos), hands-on project checkpoints, quizzes, and safety-first messaging throughout. Tone should be encouraging, professional, and empowering.

**TASKS:** Build the full program architecture including:

1. **PROGRAM OVERVIEW**
   - Program title, tagline, and mission statement
   - Total estimated hours: [TOTAL HOURS: e.g., 40 / 80 / 120]
   - Prerequisite skills needed
   - Certification or credential outcome upon completion
   - Equipment/tools list learners need access to

2. **LEARNING PATHS** — Create [NUMBER OF LEVELS: e.g., 3] progressive levels:
   - Level 1: Solar Fundamentals (Beginner)
   - Level 2: Installation Technician (Intermediate)
   - Level 3: System Management & Business (Advanced)

3. **MODULE BREAKDOWN** — For each level, create detailed modules including:
   - Module title and learning objectives (3-5 per module)
   - Lesson topics with estimated completion time
   - Key concepts, formulas, and terminology
   - Hands-on lab or simulation activity
   - Safety protocols relevant to that module
   - Knowledge check quiz (5-10 questions per module)
   - Real-world case study or project milestone

4. **CORE CURRICULUM TOPICS** — Ensure full coverage of:
   - Solar energy fundamentals (photovoltaic effect, irradiance, energy production)
   - Solar panel types, ratings, and selection criteria
   - System components: inverters, charge controllers, batteries, mounting hardware, wiring
   - Site assessment: roof analysis, shading analysis, azimuth, tilt angle optimization
   - System sizing calculations for [SYSTEM TYPE: residential / commercial / off-grid]
   - Electrical wiring, conduit installation, string sizing, and combiner boxes
   - NEC Article 690 compliance and local permitting process
   - Grid-tied vs. off-grid vs. hybrid system configurations
   - Battery storage systems (lithium, lead-acid, flow batteries)
   - Monitoring systems and performance analytics
   - Commissioning, testing, and inspections
   - Troubleshooting and maintenance procedures
   - Net metering, incentives, tax credits (ITC), and ROI calculations
   - Safety: fall protection, arc flash, electrical safety, PPE requirements
   - Customer consultation and proposal creation
   - [ADDITIONAL TOPIC: add any region-specific or specialty topic]

5. **ASSESSMENTS & CERTIFICATION PATH**
   - Define formative assessments (quizzes per module)
   - Define summative assessments (capstone project or final exam)
   - Minimum passing score: [PASSING SCORE: e.g., 80%]
   - Capstone project description: learner designs and presents a complete solar proposal for a [PROJECT TYPE: e.g., 7kW residential rooftop system]
   - Certificate of completion criteria
   - Pathway recommendations for NABCEP Entry Level or PV Associate exam

6. **SELF-PACED DELIVERY DESIGN**
   - Recommended weekly study schedule (flexible options for [HOURS PER WEEK: e.g., 5 / 10 / 20 hours/week])
   - Progress tracking milestones
   - Community or peer learning component (forums, study groups)
   - Instructor Q&A or mentorship touchpoints
   - Resource library: recommended books, YouTube channels, NEC codebooks, NABCEP study guides
   - Platform recommendations for hosting: [PLATFORM: e.g., Teachable / Thinkific / Moodle / Custom LMS]

7. **SUPPLEMENTAL RESOURCES**
   - Glossary of 50+ solar energy terms
   - Downloadable worksheets: system sizing calculator, site assessment checklist, installation checklist, maintenance log template
   - Sample permit application walkthrough for [STATE/REGION: e.g., California / Texas / Florida]
   - Vendor and supplier directory
   - Career roadmap: job titles, salary ranges, and growth opportunities in solar

**ACTION:** Deliver the complete program architecture in a structured, organized format ready to be handed to a course development team or used directly by the program creator. Make every module actionable, every lesson purposeful, and every assessment meaningful. Prioritize safety, code compliance, and practical skill-building above all. Flag any module where hands-on practice with real equipment is strongly recommended over simulation alone.
