YouTube Thumbnail Concepts

5 high-CTR YouTube thumbnail concepts with psychology, text overlays, and A/B testing guidance.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
89 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Generate 5 high-CTR YouTube thumbnail concepts for a video about [TOPIC].

Channel style: [DESCRIBE CURRENT THUMBNAILS]
Target audience: [WHO WATCHES]
Video title: [YOUR TITLE]
Main emotion to trigger: [CURIOSITY/SHOCK/INSPIRATION/FOMO/HUMOR]

For each concept:
1. Main visual element
2. Text overlay (max 4 words)
3. Color scheme and why it grabs attention
4. Facial expression if person is shown
5. Psychological trigger used

Also provide:
- Best concept ranked #1 with full reasoning
- What to A/B test between concepts 1 and 2
- One safe option and one bold option
YouTubethumbnaildesignCTRcontent

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC], [DESCRIBE CURRENT THUMBNAILS], [WHO WATCHES], [YOUR TITLE], [CURIOSITY/SHOCK/INSPIRATION/FOMO/HUMOR].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
