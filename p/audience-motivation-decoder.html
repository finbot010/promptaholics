Decode this audience's motivations at three levels. Audience: [DESCRIPTION]. Logical motivations — the rational reasons they make decisions. Emotional motivations — the feelings driving their behavior beneath the surface. Identity motivations — the deeper story they're telling themselves about who they are. For each level: list 5 motivations with examples, show how they influence buying decisions, and write a content hook and offer angle that speaks directly to each.
