Train and Bus Route Planner

Plans a multi-city trip by train and bus with routes, times, booking tips and passes.

GeminiTravel
82 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Plan my trip by train and bus.

Route: [CITY A → CITY B → CITY C]
Dates: [DATES]
Travelers: [NUMBER, AGES]
Priorities: [CHEAPEST / FASTEST / MOST SCENIC]

For each leg: options with duration, number of changes, typical price range, the operator, and where to book. Tell me whether a rail pass is worth it for my route, which legs to book early, and luggage rules to know. Note that times and prices should be checked on the operator's site.
trainsbusespublic transport

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY A → CITY B → CITY C], [DATES], [NUMBER, AGES], [CHEAPEST / FASTEST / MOST SCENIC].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
