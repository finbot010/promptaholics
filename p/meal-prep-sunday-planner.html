Meal Prep Sunday Planner

Creates efficient Sunday meal prep plans for the whole week.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
75 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a meal prep strategist and nutritionist. Create a Sunday meal prep plan for: [PASTE DIETARY GOAL, NUMBER OF PEOPLE, BUDGET, TIME AVAILABLE, DIETARY RESTRICTIONS]. Include: shopping list organized by store section, prep sequence (what to cook first based on cook times), storage containers needed, how to assemble 5 different meals from the same base ingredients, reheating instructions for each meal, food safety and storage timing guide, and how long each item stays fresh.
