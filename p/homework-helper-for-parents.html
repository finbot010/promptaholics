Homework Helper for Parents

Explains your child's homework topic to you first, so you can guide them without just giving answers.

GeminiEducation & Learning
91 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
My child is in [GRADE] and is stuck on this homework: [PASTE OR UPLOAD A PHOTO OF THE PROBLEM]

Subject: [MATH / READING / SCIENCE / OTHER]
How their school teaches it, if I know: [e.g. a specific math method]

First, explain the concept to me as an adult in simple terms. Then give me 3-4 guiding questions I can ask my child to help them figure it out themselves, a similar practice problem with the solution, and signs that they're truly confused (and should ask the teacher) versus just tired.
homeworkparentsexplaining concepts

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [GRADE], [PASTE OR UPLOAD A PHOTO OF THE PROBLEM], [MATH / READING / SCIENCE / OTHER].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
