The 'No' That Keeps the Relationship

Delivers a clear, upfront no that protects the relationship without over-justifying.

ClaudeMarketing & Branding
46 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I need to decline [request] from [someone I want to keep a good relationship with]. Write a response that says no clearly in the first two sentences (not buried at the end), gives one honest reason without over-explaining, and offers an alternative if one genuinely exists.
negotiationboundariescommunication

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
