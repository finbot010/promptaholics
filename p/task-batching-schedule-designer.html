Task Batching Schedule Designer

Designs a task-batching schedule around your actual energy patterns, with a rule for handling urgent interruptions.

ClaudeAI Agents & Automation
134 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a time-management coach who designs batching schedules around how attention actually works, not arbitrary time blocks. Help me batch my week.

My recurring task types: [LIST THEM — E.G. EMAILS, DEEP WORK, MEETINGS, ADMIN, CREATIVE WORK]
My natural energy pattern during the day: [WHEN YOU FEEL SHARPEST/FOGGIEST]
Fixed commitments I can't move: [MEETINGS, SCHOOL PICKUP, ETC.]

Give me:
1. A batching structure that groups similar-type tasks together and separates high-focus work from reactive/shallow work
2. Placement of the highest-focus batch during my actual peak energy window, not just "mornings" by default
3. Buffer time between batches to prevent one running long and wrecking the rest of the day
4. What to do when an urgent task threatens to break the batching — a rule for when to interrupt vs. when to defer
time blockingtask batchingfocus

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHEN YOU FEEL SHARPEST/FOGGIEST], [MEETINGS, SCHOOL PICKUP, ETC.].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
