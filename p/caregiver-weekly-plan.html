Caregiver Weekly Plan

Organizes appointments, medications and helper shifts for caring for a family member, in Google Calendar.

GeminiHealth & Wellness
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Calendar Help me organize caregiving for [RELATIONSHIP, e.g. my mother].

Their needs: [e.g. mobility help, meals, medication reminders, appointments]
Appointments coming up: [LIST OR "check my calendar"]
Helpers and when they are available: [NAMES, TIMES]
My own work schedule: [TIMES]

Create a weekly plan showing who covers what and when, add appointments and helper shifts to my calendar, and suggest a shared checklist for each visit (meds given, meals, mood, concerns). Include time for my own rest, and list local caregiver support resources to look into.
caregivingfamily healthgoogle calendar

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [RELATIONSHIP, e.g. my mother], [LIST OR "check my calendar"], [NAMES, TIMES], [TIMES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
