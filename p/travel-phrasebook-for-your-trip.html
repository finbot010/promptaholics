Travel Phrasebook for Your Trip

Creates a pocket phrasebook with the phrases you will actually need, with pronunciation and etiquette tips.

ChatGPTTravel
82 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a pocket phrasebook for my trip.

Destination and language: [COUNTRY / LANGUAGE]
What I will be doing: [e.g. restaurants, taxis, hotels, markets, hiking]
Dietary needs or health info to communicate: [DETAILS]

Give me 40 essential phrases grouped by situation, each with the phrase, simple pronunciation, and when to use it. Add: numbers 1-10 and prices, polite forms and gestures to use or avoid, how to say my dietary needs clearly, and emergency phrases. Format it to fit on 2 printed pages.
phraseslanguagetravel basics

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [COUNTRY / LANGUAGE], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
