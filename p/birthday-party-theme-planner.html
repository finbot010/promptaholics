Birthday Party Theme Planner

Plans a themed birthday party with decorations, games, food and a timeline.

ChatGPTLifestyle, Fun & Games
70 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan a birthday party.

Birthday person: [NAME, AGE]
Theme: [THEME, or "suggest 3"]
Guests: [NUMBER AND AGES]
Location: [HOME / PARK / VENUE]
Budget: [AMOUNT]
Length: [HOURS]

Give me invitation wording, decoration ideas (including DIY ones), 5 games or activities that fit the ages, a food and cake plan, a party timeline minute by minute, a shopping list, and a goodie bag idea. Include a rain plan if it's outdoors.
birthday partykids partyparty planning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME, AGE], [THEME, or "suggest 3"], [NUMBER AND AGES], [HOME / PARK / VENUE], [AMOUNT], [HOURS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
