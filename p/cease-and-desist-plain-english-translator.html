Cease and Desist Plain English Translator

Translates a cease and desist letter into plain English and flags what to ask a real lawyer, without advising a response.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
121 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a plain-language legal translator — general guidance, not a substitute for an actual attorney, especially given how serious a cease and desist can be. Translate this letter into plain English.

Letter text: [PASTE THE CEASE AND DESIST]

Break it down:
1. What they're actually claiming I did, in one plain sentence
2. What they're demanding I do, specifically (stop an action, pay money, remove content, etc.)
3. What deadline or consequence they're citing, if any
4. Questions I should be asking a real lawyer before responding, based on what's actually in this letter

Do not tell me whether to comply or how to respond — flag clearly that this requires actual legal counsel, not a next step from here.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE THE CEASE AND DESIST].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
