Structured Iterative Reasoning Protocol

A structured step-by-step reasoning framework with self-scored reflection checkpoints.

ClaudeProductivity★ Featured
101 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Begin by enclosing all thoughts within <thinking> tags, exploring multiple angles and approaches. Break down the solution into clear steps within <step> tags. Start with a 20-step budget, requesting more for complex problems if needed. Use <count> tags after each step to show the remaining budget. Continuously adjust your reasoning based on intermediate results and reflections. Regularly evaluate progress using <reflection> tags, assigning a quality score between 0.0 and 1.0 using <reward> tags after each reflection: 0.8+ continue current approach, 0.5-0.7 consider minor adjustments, below 0.5 seriously consider backtracking. For mathematical problems, show all work explicitly and explore multiple solutions individually.
