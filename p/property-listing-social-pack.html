Property Listing Social Pack

Full social media content pack for any property listing across all major platforms.

ChatGPTReal Estate
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a complete social media content pack for a property listing.

Property: [ADDRESS or DESCRIPTION]
Key features: [LIST 5 FEATURES]
Price: $[PRICE] | Open house: [DATE/TIME if applicable]
Agent contact: [NAME/PHONE]
Target buyer: [DESCRIBE]

Create:
1. Instagram carousel captions (lead slide + 5 feature slides)
2. Facebook listing post (detailed, shareable)
3. Instagram Story text overlays (5 slides)
4. Tweet/X post (under 280 chars)
5. LinkedIn post (professional investor angle)
6. Email subject line to send to your database
7. SMS blast (under 160 chars)

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ADDRESS or DESCRIPTION], [LIST 5 FEATURES], [PRICE], [DATE/TIME if applicable], [NAME/PHONE], [DESCRIBE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
