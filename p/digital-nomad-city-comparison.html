Digital Nomad City Comparison

Compares cities for remote work on cost, internet, visas, time zone and coworking using current data.

GeminiTravel
90 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Compare these cities for living and working remotely: [CITY 1], [CITY 2], [CITY 3].

My passport: [COUNTRY]
Length of stay: [MONTHS]
Work hours I need to overlap: [TIME ZONE]
Budget per month: [AMOUNT]
Priorities: [e.g. fast internet, safety, community, beach, low cost]

Search current information and compare: rent for a furnished 1-bedroom, coworking prices, internet speeds, visa options for my passport and stay length, time zone overlap, safety, and community. Give a table, a score for each priority, and your recommendation. Remind me to verify visa rules on official sites.
digital nomadremote workcost of living

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY 1], [CITY 2], [CITY 3], [COUNTRY], [MONTHS], [TIME ZONE], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
