Realistic Animal Portrait

Hyper-realistic wildlife portrait with National Geographic quality detail, authentic fur/feather/scale texture, and dramatic natural lighting.

MidjourneyImage & Art
54 words · free to use 
Hyper-realistic wildlife portrait of a [ANIMAL] in its natural habitat. National Geographic level photography quality. Incredibly detailed fur, feathers or scales with subsurface scattering. Expressive eyes with authentic catchlight and depth. Natural dramatic lighting — golden hour or overcast soft light. Shot on 600mm telephoto lens with shallow depth of field. 8K resolution, masterpiece.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ANIMAL].
  3. Paste it into Midjourney and generate. Run it a few times — image tools give a different result each time.
  4. Adjust the style, lighting or aspect ratio words to steer the result.
