Customer Persona From Real Feedback

Builds customer personas from your real reviews, emails and survey answers instead of guesses.

ChatGPTBusiness & Strategy
68 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Build customer personas from real data.

Business: [WHAT YOU SELL]
Customer feedback, reviews, survey answers or support messages (anonymized):
[PASTE]

Find 2-3 distinct customer types. For each: name and short description, goals, frustrations, what made them buy, objections, words they actually use (quote them), and where to reach them. Then tell me which persona is most valuable and what to change in my marketing to speak to them.
customer personaresearchmarketing

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT YOU SELL], [PASTE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
