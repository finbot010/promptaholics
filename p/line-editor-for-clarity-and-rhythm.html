Line Editor for Clarity and Rhythm

Line-edits your draft for clarity, rhythm and wordiness, showing each change and why.

ClaudeWriting & Content
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Line-edit my writing.

Type of piece: [ESSAY / BLOG POST / NEWSLETTER / CHAPTER / REPORT]
Audience: [WHO]
My voice (describe or paste a sample I like): [DETAILS]
Draft:
[PASTE]

Edit for clarity, rhythm and concision while keeping my voice. Show the edited version, then a list of the most important changes with the reason for each (e.g. cut filler, fixed passive voice, split a long sentence). Do not change my meaning or add new ideas. End with the 3 habits in my writing to watch for.
editingclarityprose

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ESSAY / BLOG POST / NEWSLETTER / CHAPTER / REPORT], [WHO], [DETAILS], [PASTE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
