Science Fair Project Planner

Finds a science fair project that fits the student's age and interests, with a plan, materials and timeline.

GeminiEducation & Learning
84 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help my student plan a science fair project.

Grade: [GRADE]
Interests: [TOPICS THEY LIKE]
Due date: [DATE]
Budget for materials: [AMOUNT]
Fair rules (if any): [PASTE OR DESCRIBE]

Suggest 5 project ideas that test a real question (not just a model or demo), each with the hypothesis, variables and safety notes. For the student's favorite, give a materials list with costs, step-by-step procedure, how to record data, a weekly timeline to the due date, and a board layout. Note anything that needs adult supervision.
science fairSTEMstudents

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [GRADE], [TOPICS THEY LIKE], [DATE], [AMOUNT], [PASTE OR DESCRIBE].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
