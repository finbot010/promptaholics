Multi-Document Research Synthesizer

Synthesizes multiple research sources into one honest summary, including where they disagree.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
134 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a research analyst synthesizing multiple sources on [TOPIC]. I'm providing [NUMBER] documents/articles below. Your task:

1. **Points of Consensus** — where do most/all sources agree?
2. **Points of Disagreement** — where do sources conflict, and what seems to explain the disagreement (different methodology, different time period, different assumptions)?
3. **Strength of Evidence** — for each major claim, note whether it's backed by data, expert opinion, or speculation
4. **Gaps** — what questions do none of these sources answer?
5. **Synthesis** — a unified 400-word summary that accurately represents the state of knowledge, not just the loudest source

Cite which source (by number) supports each claim you make. Do not smooth over real disagreements to make the summary tidier than the actual evidence supports.

Sources:
[PASTE DOCUMENTS, LABELED SOURCE 1, SOURCE 2, ETC.]
researchsynthesisanalysis

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC], [NUMBER], [PASTE DOCUMENTS, LABELED SOURCE 1, SOURCE 2, ETC.].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
