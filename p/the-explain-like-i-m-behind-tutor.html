The 'Explain Like I'm Behind' Tutor

Catches you up on missed material and flags exactly what to prioritize next.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
42 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I missed the last few classes/lessons on [subject/topic] and feel behind. Explain the core concept from scratch assuming zero prior context from those missed sessions, then tell me the 2-3 things I most need to catch up on before the next class.
tutoringcatching uplearning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
