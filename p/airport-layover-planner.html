Airport Layover Planner

Tells you whether to stay in the airport or go out on a layover, and plans either option.

GeminiTravel
94 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me plan a layover.

Airport: [AIRPORT]
Arrive: [TIME] Depart: [TIME]
My passport: [COUNTRY]
Luggage: [CHECKED THROUGH / MUST COLLECT]
Interests: [REST / FOOD / QUICK SIGHTSEEING]

Tell me: whether I can legally leave the airport with my passport and how long security and immigration usually take there; whether I have enough time to go into the city (with transport time and cost); and either a city plan with a strict return time or the best spots inside the airport (lounges, quiet areas, food, showers). Remind me to verify transit rules on official sources.
layoverairportsconnections

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AIRPORT], [TIME], [COUNTRY], [CHECKED THROUGH / MUST COLLECT], [REST / FOOD / QUICK SIGHTSEEING].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
