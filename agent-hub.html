Connect Facebook & Instagram (one time, about 10 minutes)
  1. Make sure your Instagram is a Business or Creator account and is linked to your Facebook Page (Instagram app → Settings → Account type; Facebook Page → Settings → Linked accounts).
  2. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps → Create app. Choose the use cases for managing a Page and Instagram content. Your own account can use it right away in development mode; no app review needed to post to your own Page.
  3. Open the Graph API Explorer, pick your app, click Generate Access Token, and allow: pages_show_list pages_read_engagement pages_manage_posts instagram_basic instagram_content_publish business_management.
  4. Click the ⓘ next to the token → Open in Access Token Tool → Extend Access Token. Copy the long-lived token back into the Explorer.
  5. In the Explorer, run me/accounts. Find your Page and copy its id and its access_token (a Page token made this way doesn't expire).
  6. In Cloudflare → Workers → dawn-thunder-a558 → Settings → Variables and Secrets, add two Secrets: META_PAGE_ID (the id) and META_PAGE_TOKEN (the Page access_token). Never paste the token into a chat.
  7. Come back here and click Check again.
