Personal Knowledge Base Organizer

Designs a simple folder, tag and note system for your notes app based on how you actually work.

ClaudeAI Agents & Automation
90 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Design a personal knowledge system for my notes.

Notes app I use: [NOTION / OBSIDIAN / APPLE NOTES / GOOGLE DOCS / OTHER]
What I save: [e.g. meeting notes, ideas, research, recipes, client info]
Problems now: [e.g. cannot find things, too many folders, duplicate notes]
Time I will spend maintaining it: [MINUTES PER WEEK]

Give me: a folder or tag structure (keep it simple); naming rules for notes; 3 templates I will use most; a weekly 15-minute maintenance routine; and a plan to migrate my existing mess without starting over.
notesknowledge managementorganization

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MINUTES PER WEEK].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
