Academic Literature Review Assistant

Structures an academic literature review from your source list.

GeminiEducation & Learning
145 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me structure a literature review section for [PAPER/THESIS TOPIC] based on the sources I provide.

1. **Thematic Clusters**: Group the sources by the actual argument or finding they contribute, not just by topic keyword
2. **Chronological Development**: How has the field's understanding evolved across these sources — note any clear turning points
3. **Methodological Notes**: Where methodology differences between sources explain differing conclusions
4. **The Gap**: What specific gap in the existing literature does my research address — this needs to be precise, not "more research is needed"
5. **Draft Paragraph Structure**: A suggested paragraph-by-paragraph flow for the review section itself

Do not fabricate sources or findings — work only from what I provide, and tell me clearly if my source list has an obvious gap (e.g., missing a foundational paper in the area).

My topic and research question: [DESCRIBE]
Sources: [PASTE SOURCE SUMMARIES/ABSTRACTS]
academicresearchliterature-review

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PAPER/THESIS TOPIC], [DESCRIBE], [PASTE SOURCE SUMMARIES/ABSTRACTS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
