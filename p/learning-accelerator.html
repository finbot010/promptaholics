Learning Accelerator

Accelerated learning plan with Pareto 20%, optimal sequence, spaced repetition, ranked resources, deliberate practice, and milestone checkpoints.

ChatGPTEducation & Learning
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create an accelerated learning plan to master [SKILL] in [TIMEFRAME]. Include: the 20% of knowledge that produces 80% of results (Pareto principle applied), the optimal learning sequence from foundational to advanced, a spaced repetition schedule for retention, the specific resources — books, courses, practitioners — ranked by quality, deliberate practice exercises that build real competency, how to find a feedback loop to accelerate progress, and milestone checkpoints to verify actual learning vs false confidence.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SKILL], [TIMEFRAME].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
