Consumer Complaint Letter

Writes a firm, factual complaint letter to a company to get a refund, repair or replacement.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
94 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a consumer complaint letter.

Company: [NAME]
What I bought and when: [PRODUCT / SERVICE, DATE, PRICE]
What went wrong: [DETAILS]
What I have already tried: [CALLS, EMAILS, DATES]
What I want: [REFUND / REPAIR / REPLACEMENT / COMPENSATION]
Country/state: [LOCATION]

Write a clear letter with a timeline, the specific remedy I want, a deadline to respond, and the next step I will take if they do not (e.g. consumer protection agency, chargeback, small claims). Mention consumer rights that may apply in my location in general terms and tell me where to confirm them.
consumer rightscomplaintsrefunds

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME], [PRODUCT / SERVICE, DATE, PRICE], [DETAILS], [CALLS, EMAILS, DATES], [REFUND / REPAIR / REPLACEMENT / COMPENSATION], [LOCATION].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
