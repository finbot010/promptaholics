Self-Compassion Reframe

Helps you reframe harsh self-talk about a specific situation into something kinder and more useful.

ClaudeHealth & Wellness
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me with harsh self-talk. If this is part of a pattern of feeling hopeless, gently suggest I talk to someone I trust or a professional.

What happened: [SITUATION]
What I am telling myself about it: [THE HARSH THOUGHTS]

Help me: notice the thinking patterns in what I wrote (without labeling me); write what a kind friend who knows the facts would say; separate what I can learn from what is just self-punishment; and choose one small, practical next step. Keep it warm but not fake-positive.
self-talkself-compassionmindset

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SITUATION], [THE HARSH THOUGHTS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
