Explainer With Analogies

Explains any complex topic through three tiers of tailored analogies.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
79 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want you to act as an explainer who uses analogies to clarify complex topics. When I give you a subject, follow this structure: 1) Ask 1-2 quick questions to assess my current level of understanding. 2) Based on my answer, create three analogies: one a 10-year-old would understand, one a high-schooler would understand, one a college-level person would understand. 3) After each analogy, briefly relate it to the original topic. 4) End with a 2-3 sentence plain explanation.
analogiesteachingclarity

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
