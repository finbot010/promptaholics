Newsletter Welcome Sequence

Creates a 5-email welcome sequence that converts new subscribers into loyal fans.

ChatGPTEmail, Sales & Communication
110 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are an email marketing strategist. Create a 5-email welcome sequence for new newsletter subscribers. My newsletter topic: [WHAT YOUR NEWSLETTER IS ABOUT]. My audience: [WHO SUBSCRIBES AND WHY]. Content style: [EDUCATIONAL / PERSONAL / STRATEGIES AND TIPS]. Goal for new subscribers: [BUILD TRUST / SELL PRODUCT / GROW COMMUNITY]. Write 5 emails over 2 weeks: Email 1 immediate (welcome, set expectations, deliver lead magnet), Email 2 Day 2 (your best insight), Email 3 Day 4 (your story and why you started), Email 4 Day 7 (social proof or community), Email 5 Day 14 (soft offer or next step). Each under 300 words. Include subject lines. Feel personal not automated.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT YOUR NEWSLETTER IS ABOUT], [WHO SUBSCRIBES AND WHY], [EDUCATIONAL / PERSONAL / STRATEGIES AND TIPS], [BUILD TRUST / SELL PRODUCT / GROW COMMUNITY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
