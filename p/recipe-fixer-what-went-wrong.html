Recipe Fixer: What Went Wrong

Figures out why a recipe failed and tells you exactly what to change next time.

ClaudeFood & Cooking
68 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
My recipe did not turn out right. Help me figure out why.

Recipe:
[PASTE]
What I actually did (substitutions, oven, timing): [DETAILS]
What went wrong: [e.g. cake sank, sauce split, bread dense, meat tough]
Altitude, climate or equipment notes: [DETAILS]

Give me the most likely causes ranked, what to check, and exactly what to change next time. If the dish can still be rescued today, tell me how.
cooking problemsbakingtroubleshooting

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
