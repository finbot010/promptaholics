Study Schedule for Exam Week

Builds a realistic exam-week study schedule that balances subjects, sleep and breaks.

ChatGPTEducation & Learning
81 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Build my study schedule for exams.

Exams (subject, date, how ready I feel 1-10): [LIST]
Hours I can study per day: [HOURS]
Other commitments: [WORK, CLASSES, SPORTS]
How I study best: [PRACTICE TESTS / NOTES / GROUPS]

Create a day-by-day plan that puts more time on weak, early exams; uses active recall and practice tests instead of rereading; includes breaks, meals and 8 hours of sleep; and has a light review the night before each exam. Add a "behind schedule" plan.
exam prepstudy schedulestudents

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST], [HOURS], [WORK, CLASSES, SPORTS], [PRACTICE TESTS / NOTES / GROUPS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
