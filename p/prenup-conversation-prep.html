Prenup Conversation Prep

Helps couples prepare to talk about a prenuptial agreement calmly before seeing lawyers.

GeminiLegal & Admin
88 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
My partner and I are thinking about a prenup. We're in [COUNTRY / STATE], getting married on [DATE].

Our situation: [ASSETS, DEBTS, BUSINESSES, CHILDREN FROM BEFORE, BIG INCOME DIFFERENCES — whatever applies]
Why we're considering it: [REASON]

Give me: a gentle way to start the conversation; the topics prenups usually cover; questions for each of us to think about separately first; a list of documents to gather; why each person typically has their own lawyer; and a realistic timeline before the wedding. This is preparation, not legal advice.
prenupmarriagecouples

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [COUNTRY / STATE], [DATE], [REASON].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
