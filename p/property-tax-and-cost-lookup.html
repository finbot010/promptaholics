Property Tax and Cost Lookup

Researches property tax rates, insurance and typical ownership costs for an area before you buy.

GeminiReal Estate
89 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me estimate the real cost of owning a home in [CITY / COUNTY / STATE].

Home price: [PRICE]
Property type: [HOUSE / CONDO / TOWNHOUSE]

Search current information on: property tax rate and how it is assessed, typical homeowners insurance cost (including flood or hurricane coverage if relevant), HOA fees for similar properties, utilities, and other local costs (transfer taxes, special assessments). Give me an estimated monthly and yearly total with sources and dates, and tell me what to confirm with the county assessor and an insurance agent.
property taxhomeownership costsresearch

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY / COUNTY / STATE], [PRICE], [HOUSE / CONDO / TOWNHOUSE].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
