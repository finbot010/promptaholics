Draft a comprehensive Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for [WEBSITE/APP NAME], a [BUSINESS TYPE] that collects [DATA TYPES]. The Privacy Policy should cover: data collection, use, storage, sharing, cookies, user rights, GDPR/CCPA compliance, and contact information. The Terms should cover: acceptance, permitted use, prohibited actions, intellectual property, disclaimers, limitation of liability, and governing law. Use plain English. Note: Have a qualified lawyer review before publishing.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WEBSITE/APP NAME], [BUSINESS TYPE], [DATA TYPES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
