Focus Time Protector

Finds where your focus time is leaking in Google Calendar and proposes a protected schedule.

GeminiAI Agents & Automation
70 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Calendar Look at my next two weeks.

I need [HOURS] hours a week of deep work for: [PROJECT].
Meetings I cannot move: [LIST]

Find: my current uninterrupted blocks of 90+ minutes; meetings that fragment my days; and recurring meetings that could be shorter or less frequent. Propose a protected schedule with focus blocks, and write a short message I can send to my team explaining my new focus hours.
focusdeep workcalendar

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [HOURS], [PROJECT], [LIST].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
