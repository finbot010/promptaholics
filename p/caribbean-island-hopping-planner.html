Caribbean Island Hopping Planner

Plans a multi-island Caribbean trip with ferries or short flights, timing and budget.

ChatGPTTravel
98 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan an island-hopping trip in the Caribbean.

Islands I'm interested in: [LIST, or "suggest a good route"]
Starting from: [CITY / AIRPORT]
Days: [NUMBER]
Month of travel: [MONTH] (consider hurricane season and weather)
Style: [BEACHES / FOOD AND CULTURE / DIVING / NIGHTLIFE / QUIET]
Budget: [AMOUNT]

Give me a route with the number of nights on each island, how to get between them (ferry, short flight) and how often those usually run, a highlight per island, and a rough budget. Flag entry rules and currencies to check per island, and keep a buffer day for weather delays.
caribbeanisland hoppingferries

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST, or "suggest a good route"], [CITY / AIRPORT], [NUMBER], [MONTH], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
