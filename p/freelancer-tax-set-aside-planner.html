Freelancer Tax Set-Aside Planner

Estimates how much a freelancer should set aside for taxes and sets up a simple system to do it.

ClaudePersonal Finance & Investing
95 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me plan tax savings as a freelancer. Give me estimates and tell me exactly what to confirm with a tax professional or my tax authority.

Country / state: [LOCATION]
Expected annual freelance income: [AMOUNT]
Other income: [SALARY, ETC.]
Main business expenses: [LIST]
How I am paid: [CLIENTS, PLATFORMS, CURRENCIES]

Give me: an estimated percentage to set aside from each payment, with how you got there; deductible expense categories to track; estimated or quarterly payment deadlines to check; a simple bank-account system for setting money aside; a monthly bookkeeping routine; and the records to keep.
freelancetaxesself-employed

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LOCATION], [AMOUNT], [SALARY, ETC.], [LIST], [CLIENTS, PLATFORMS, CURRENCIES].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
