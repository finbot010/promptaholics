Audience Trust Accelerator

Generate 12 trust-building content ideas for [CREATOR/BRAND] in [NICHE]. For each idea: the title, format, trust mechanism (expertise, transparency, consistency, social proof, vulnerability, results), key message, and opening hook. Design the 12 pieces as a trust-building arc — they work individually but together build a complete trust relationship with a new follower over 30 days. Include a deployment calendar.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CREATOR/BRAND], [NICHE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
