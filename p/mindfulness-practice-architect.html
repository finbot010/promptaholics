Design a progressive mindfulness practice for someone who has [TRIED AND FAILED / NEVER TRIED / IS INTERMEDIATE]. Include: a 5-minute daily anchor practice, weekly depth practice, the common obstacles for this experience level and how to navigate them, and the markers that indicate the practice is working versus becoming another source of self-judgment.
mindfulnessmeditationmental health

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TRIED AND FAILED / NEVER TRIED / IS INTERMEDIATE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
