Website Copy Complete Rewrite

A full conversion-focused website copy rewrite using proven direct response principles.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
97 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Rewrite the copy for my [PAGE TYPE: homepage/about/services/landing page] using conversion copywriting principles.

Current copy: [PASTE YOUR CURRENT COPY]
Target visitor: [DESCRIBE WHO LANDS HERE]
Main action I want them to take: [CTA]
Biggest hesitation they arrive with: [OBJECTION]
My unfair advantage over competitors: [DIFFERENTIATOR]

Rewrite with:
- Hero section: outcome-led headline + clarifying subheadline
- Trust signals woven naturally (not just a logo bar)
- Every claim backed by specificity (numbers, names, results)
- No passive voice, no jargon, no 'we are passionate about'
- Clear visual hierarchy suggestions [H1] [H2] [CTA]
- Mobile-first sentence length
websitecopywritinghomepageconversionUX

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PAGE TYPE: homepage/about/services/landing page], [PASTE YOUR CURRENT COPY], [DESCRIBE WHO LANDS HERE], [CTA], [OBJECTION], [DIFFERENTIATOR], [H1], [H2] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
