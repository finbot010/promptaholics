Garage Sale Prep and Pricing Guide

Plans a garage sale with sorting, pricing, signs, setup and what to do with leftovers.

ChatGPTLifestyle, Fun & Games
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm having a garage sale on [DATE].

What I'm selling: [CATEGORIES — e.g. kids' clothes, furniture, tools, electronics, books]
Goal: [MAKE MONEY / CLEAR SPACE / BOTH]
Location type: [HOUSE / APARTMENT COMPLEX / COMMUNITY SALE]

Give me: a sorting plan for the week before; a pricing cheat sheet by category; how to advertise (online listings, signs, wording); table setup tips that boost sales; how to handle haggling and early birds; a cash and digital payment setup; and a plan for donating or listing what's left.
garage saleyard saledecluttering

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DATE], [MAKE MONEY / CLEAR SPACE / BOTH], [HOUSE / APARTMENT COMPLEX / COMMUNITY SALE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
