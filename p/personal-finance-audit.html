Personal Finance Audit

A no-nonsense personal finance review that acts like having your own CFO.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
97 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Act as a personal CFO and conduct a complete financial audit for me.

My monthly income: [INCOME]
Fixed expenses: [LIST]
Variable spending: [ESTIMATE]
Current savings: [AMOUNT]
Debt: [DESCRIBE]
Financial goal: [GOAL + TIMELINE]

Provide:
1. Where my money is actually going vs where it should go
2. The 3 biggest leaks I need to plug immediately
3. A realistic savings plan with monthly targets
4. How to allocate raises or windfalls
5. The one financial move that would have the biggest impact in 90 days
6. Investing basics tailored to my situation

Be blunt. Do not sugarcoat.
personal financemoneybudgetingwealth

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [INCOME], [LIST], [ESTIMATE], [AMOUNT], [DESCRIBE], [GOAL + TIMELINE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
