Weekend Getaway Within Driving Distance

Finds weekend getaway destinations within your driving time, with a plan for each.

GeminiTravel
71 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Suggest weekend getaways within [HOURS] hours' drive of [CITY].

Dates: [DATES]
Travelers: [WHO]
Vibe: [NATURE / SMALL TOWN / BEACH / FOOD AND WINE / CITY BREAK]
Budget: [AMOUNT]

Give me 5 destinations with drive time, why they fit, what to do, where to stay (price range), and the weather forecast for those dates. Pick the best one and give a simple Saturday-Sunday plan with drive times between stops.
weekend triproad tripgoogle maps

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [HOURS], [CITY], [DATES], [WHO], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
