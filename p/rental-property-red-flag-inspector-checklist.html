Rental Property Red Flag Inspector Checklist

Builds a category-by-category red-flag checklist for evaluating a rental property before buying.

ChatGPTReal Estate
142 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a real estate investor who's been burned by properties that looked fine on paper. Build me a red-flag checklist for evaluating this property.

Property type: [SINGLE FAMILY / MULTI-UNIT / CONDO / ETC.]
Location context: [NEIGHBORHOOD TYPE, MARKET]
What I know so far: [LISTING DETAILS, AGE, ANY DISCLOSED ISSUES]

Give me a checklist organized by category:
1. Structural/mechanical red flags to look for during a walkthrough (not just "check the roof" — specific signs of specific problems)
2. Financial red flags in the numbers (rent roll, expenses, cap rate) that suggest the seller is hiding something
3. Neighborhood/market red flags that wouldn't show up in the listing
4. Questions to ask the seller/agent that tend to reveal problems when answered evasively

Flag which of these is worth paying for a professional inspection over, versus what I can reasonably assess myself.
real estaterental propertydue diligence

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SINGLE FAMILY / MULTI-UNIT / CONDO / ETC.], [NEIGHBORHOOD TYPE, MARKET], [LISTING DETAILS, AGE, ANY DISCLOSED ISSUES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
