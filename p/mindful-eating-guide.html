Mindful Eating Guide

Personalized mindful eating guide addressing emotional triggers, hunger signals, and a 7-day challenge.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
141 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a personalized mindful eating guide to improve my relationship with food.

Current eating patterns: [DESCRIBE — emotional eating / rushed meals / night snacking / restricting]
Goal: [LOSE WEIGHT / STOP BINGE EATING / REDUCE ANXIETY AROUND FOOD / BETTER ENERGY]
Food relationship history: [ANY RELEVANT CONTEXT]
Biggest trigger: [STRESS / BOREDOM / SOCIAL SITUATIONS / HABITS]
Time typically available for meals: [RUSHED / 15-20 MIN / RELAXED]

Provide:
1. The core principles of mindful eating (without diet culture)
2. A hunger scale — how to read real hunger vs emotional hunger
3. Pre-meal ritual (2 minutes) to shift into mindful mode
4. The SLOW method for meals
5. Identifying and interrupting emotional eating triggers
6. How to eat satisfyingly without overeating
7. A 7-day mindful eating challenge
8. What to do after an episode of emotional or mindless eating

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ANY RELEVANT CONTEXT], [STRESS / BOREDOM / SOCIAL SITUATIONS / HABITS], [RUSHED / 15-20 MIN / RELAXED].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
