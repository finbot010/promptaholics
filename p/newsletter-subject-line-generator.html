Newsletter Subject Line Generator

Generates 20 email subject lines with preview text and timing recommendations.

You are an email marketing copywriter. Generate 20 subject lines for a newsletter about: [PASTE NEWSLETTER TOPIC AND AUDIENCE]. Use these 10 proven formulas (2 per formula): Curiosity gap ('The mistake 90% of [audience] make'), Number list ('7 things I wish I knew about [topic]'), How-to ('How to [desired outcome] without [pain]'), Social proof ('Why [X people/companies] switched to [thing]'), Controversy ('Unpopular opinion: [contrarian take]'). For each subject line add: preview text, predicted open rate (high/medium/low), and best day/time to send.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE NEWSLETTER TOPIC AND AUDIENCE], [X people/companies].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
