Fixer-Upper Renovation Budget

Builds a renovation budget and priority list for a fixer-upper.

ChatGPTReal Estate
96 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm looking at a fixer-upper.

Property: [TYPE, SIZE, YEAR BUILT]
Issues I noticed: [e.g. old roof, dated kitchen, water stain, single-pane windows]
What I want it to become: [MOVE-IN READY / RENTAL / FLIP / DREAM HOME]
Location: [AREA] (costs vary a lot)
Budget for repairs: [AMOUNT]

Give me: a priority order (safety and structure first, then systems, then looks); a low-mid-high cost range per item for my area with a note to get local quotes; a 15% contingency; what I can DIY vs what needs a licensed pro; and the inspections to order before buying.
renovationbudgetfixer upper

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TYPE, SIZE, YEAR BUILT], [MOVE-IN READY / RENTAL / FLIP / DREAM HOME], [AREA], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
