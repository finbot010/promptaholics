Weekly Review System Builder

Builds complete weekly review systems for sustained productivity.

ChatGPTAI Agents & Automation
58 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a productivity coach. Build a complete weekly review system for: [PASTE ROLE AND TOOLS USED]. Create: a 30-45 minute review protocol with timed sections (capture/clarify/organize/reflect/plan), specific questions for each section, trigger list for what to review, metrics dashboard to update, next week planning template, and energy and focus assessment. Include a shorter 10-minute daily review version.
productivityweekly reviewGTDreflection

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE ROLE AND TOOLS USED].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
