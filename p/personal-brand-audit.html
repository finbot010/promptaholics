Personal Brand Audit

Full personal brand audit with gap analysis, platform consistency review, and 30-day upgrade plan.

ChatGPTMore Prompts
145 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Conduct a comprehensive personal brand audit for me.

My name: [NAME]
My profession/niche: [WHAT YOU DO]
Goal for personal brand: [JOB OPPORTUNITIES / CLIENT ATTRACTION / SPEAKING / THOUGHT LEADERSHIP]
Current online presence:
- LinkedIn: [DESCRIBE PROFILE STATE]
- Twitter/X: [FOLLOWING / CONTENT FREQUENCY]
- Instagram: [HANDLE AND NICHE]
- Website: [URL OR NONE]
- Google search results: [WHAT COMES UP FOR YOUR NAME]

Audit and provide:
1. Current brand perception (what your digital presence says about you)
2. Gap analysis (where you want to be vs where you are)
3. Top 3 highest-impact fixes to make this week
4. Content pillars (3-4 themes to own in your space)
5. Consistency audit — name, photo, bio across platforms
6. SEO optimization for your name
7. 30-day action plan to upgrade your brand
8. How to get Google to surface the right results for your name
personal brandauditonline presencereputationcareer

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME], [WHAT YOU DO], [DESCRIBE PROFILE STATE], [FOLLOWING / CONTENT FREQUENCY], [HANDLE AND NICHE], [URL OR NONE], [WHAT COMES UP FOR YOUR NAME].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
