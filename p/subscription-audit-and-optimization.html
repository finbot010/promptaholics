Subscription Audit and Optimization

Audits subscriptions and creates optimization plans to cut costs.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
69 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a personal finance optimizer. Help me audit and optimize my subscriptions: [PASTE LIST OF SUBSCRIPTIONS WITH MONTHLY COSTS]. For each subscription analyze: usage frequency (daily/weekly/monthly/rarely), whether a free alternative exists, whether I can share it (family plan), whether annual payment saves money, and whether I actually need it. Create: keep/cancel/downgrade recommendations, estimated monthly savings, and a negotiation script for services I want to keep at lower cost.
subscriptionsbudgetingpersonal financeoptimization

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE LIST OF SUBSCRIPTIONS WITH MONTHLY COSTS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
