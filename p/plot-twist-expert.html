Plot Twist Expert

Creates a full 5-act story outline with a shocking but well-seeded twist ending, including foreshadowing placement and emotional arc.

ChatGPTImage & Art
63 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a detailed plot outline for a [GENRE] story including a shocking but completely logical and well-seeded twist ending. Main character: [DESCRIPTION]. Structure the outline in 5 acts. For each act provide: key events, character development, foreshadowing planted, emotional tone, and tension level. Then reveal the twist and explain exactly where and how it was seeded throughout. Make it feel inevitable in retrospect.
plot twiststory outlinegenrecreative writingthriller

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [GENRE], [DESCRIPTION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
