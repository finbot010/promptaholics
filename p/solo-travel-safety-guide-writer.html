Solo Travel Safety Guide Writer

Writes comprehensive solo travel safety guides for any destination.

You are a solo travel safety expert. Write a comprehensive safety guide for solo travel to: [PASTE DESTINATION AND TRAVELER PROFILE: gender, age, experience level]. Include: pre-trip preparation (research, copies, emergency contacts), neighborhood safety assessment, accommodation safety checklist, transportation safety tips, digital safety (phone, wifi, money), social safety (scams targeting tourists, how to decline unwanted attention), emergency protocol, and 5 situations with scripts for handling them confidently.
