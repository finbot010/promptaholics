Infographic Visual From Your Data

Turns a few key facts or numbers into a clean, readable infographic image.

GeminiImage & Art
77 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create an infographic.

Topic: [TOPIC]
Key facts or numbers (use exactly these):
[LIST 3-6 FACTS]
Audience: [WHO]
Format: [VERTICAL 4:5 / SQUARE / 16:9 SLIDE]
Brand colors: [HEX CODES]
Style: [FLAT ICONS / ISOMETRIC / MINIMAL]

Use one clear headline, one icon or simple chart per fact, and large numbers. Do not add any statistics I did not give you. If any text or numbers render incorrectly, list them so I can fix them in an editor.
infographicdata visualizationdesign

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC], [LIST 3-6 FACTS], [WHO], [VERTICAL 4:5 / SQUARE / 16:9 SLIDE], [HEX CODES], [FLAT ICONS / ISOMETRIC / MINIMAL].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
