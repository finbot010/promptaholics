- Full Name: [FULL_NAME_AS_PER_PASSPORT]
- Date of Birth: [DOB]
- Passport Number: [PASSPORT_NUMBER]
- PAN Number: [PAN_NUMBER]
- Nature of Business/Practice: [BUSINESS_TYPE_OR_SPECIALIZATION]
- Business/Clinic/Firm Name: [ENTITY_NAME]
- Business Address: [ADDRESS]
- Annual Turnover/Income: INR [AMOUNT]
- Years in Business/Practice: [YEARS]
- Number of Employees (if applicable): [COUNT]
- GST Number (if applicable): [GST_NUMBER]
- Professional License Number (if applicable): [LICENSE_NUMBER]

Travel Details:
- Primary Purpose: [BUSINESS_MEETINGS/TOURISM/CONFERENCE/TRADE_FAIR/FAMILY_VISIT]
- B1 Component: [BUSINESS_MEETING_DETAILS or NA]
- B2 Component: [TOURISM_PLACES or NA]
- Travel Duration: [FROM_DATE] to [TO_DATE]
- USA Business Contact (if B1): [COMPANY_NAME, ADDRESS, CONTACT_PERSON]
- Accommodation: [HOTEL_NAME/HOST_DETAILS]

Provide complete visa form filling guide:
1. DS-160 occupation section: exact fields for businessman vs each professional category

5. Professional income proof documents: ITR 3 years, bank statements, business registration

Format as a comprehensive professional visa guide with sections clearly labeled.
