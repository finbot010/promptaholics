Last-Minute Trip Rescue

Helps you handle a cancelled or delayed flight: rebooking options, your rights and what to ask for.

GeminiTravel
99 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
My travel plans just went wrong. Help me fix them fast.

What happened: [CANCELLED / DELAYED / MISSED CONNECTION / OVERBOOKED]
Flight details: [AIRLINE, FLIGHT NUMBER, ROUTE, DATE]
Where I am now: [AIRPORT]
Where I need to be and by when: [DESTINATION, DEADLINE]

Search for: alternative flights today and tomorrow (same airline and others); my passenger rights for this route (for example, compensation rules that may apply), with official sources; what to ask the airline for (rebooking, meals, hotel); and a short script for the agent or phone call. Tell me what to save as evidence for a claim.
flight delaytravel disruptionrebooking

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AIRLINE, FLIGHT NUMBER, ROUTE, DATE], [AIRPORT], [DESTINATION, DEADLINE].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
