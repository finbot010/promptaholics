Podcast Episode Planner

Complete podcast episode planning from hook to show notes — saves hours of prep time.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
93 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan a complete podcast episode about [TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE].

Episode length: [DURATION]
Format: [SOLO/INTERVIEW/PANEL]

Create:
1. Episode title (5 variations, A/B testable)
2. Hook script — first 60 seconds to prevent drop-off
3. Episode outline with timestamps and talking points
4. 10 interview questions if applicable (start easy, build to controversial)
5. Transition phrases between segments
6. Call to action scripts (2 options: soft and direct)
7. Show notes template with SEO-optimized description
8. 5 social media clip ideas with exact timestamps to pull
9. Email subject line to promote this episode
podcastcontent planningaudioshow notesinterview

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC], [TARGET AUDIENCE], [DURATION], [SOLO/INTERVIEW/PANEL].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
