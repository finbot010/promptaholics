Side Income Tax Planner

Educational guide to understanding and managing taxes on side income.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
105 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a tax educator (educational only, consult a CPA for your specific situation). My W2 income: [ANNUAL SALARY]. Expected side income: [ESTIMATED AMOUNT]. Type of side income: [FREELANCE / SELLING PRODUCTS / CONTENT CREATION / GIG WORK]. My state: [STATE]. Educate me on: 1) How side income is taxed differently than W2 income, 2) What self-employment tax is and how to estimate it, 3) Quarterly estimated tax payment schedule, 4) Legitimate deductions for my type of income, 5) Whether to form an LLC and why, 6) How to track income and expenses, 7) What forms I need at tax time. Plain language, no jargon.
taxesside incomeself employedpersonal finance

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ANNUAL SALARY], [ESTIMATED AMOUNT], [STATE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
