Company Research Before an Interview

Researches a company's recent news, products, culture and interviewers so you walk in prepared.

GeminiCareer & Resume
79 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Research [COMPANY NAME] before my interview for [ROLE].

Find: what the company does and how it makes money; news and announcements from the last 6 months; main products and competitors; culture and values (from their site and employee reviews); and recent posts from my interviewers if public: [NAMES]. Then give me: 3 smart questions to ask based on what you found, how to connect my experience to their current priorities, and anything that might come up as a challenge.
interview prepcompany researchjob search

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [COMPANY NAME], [ROLE], [NAMES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
