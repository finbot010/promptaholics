Partnership Outreach Email Writer

Writes partnership outreach emails for businesses of any size relationship.

ChatGPTEmail, Sales & Communication
77 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a business development strategist. Write 3 partnership outreach emails for: [PASTE YOUR BUSINESS, POTENTIAL PARTNER TYPE, MUTUAL BENEFIT]. Email 1: Peer-level (similar size businesses, mutual promotion). Email 2: Upward (approaching a larger brand, lead with what's in it for them). Email 3: Downward (approaching a smaller brand to offer exposure). Each email: under 150 words, specific compliment on their work, clear value proposition for them first, easy next step. Include subject lines and timing recommendations.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
