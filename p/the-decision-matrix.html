The Decision Matrix

7-framework decision analysis — weighted pros/cons, 10/10/10 rule, pre-mortem, regret test, and a clear verdict.

ChatGPTMore Prompts★ Featured
141 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I need to make a decision about [DECISION — career move, major purchase, relocation, business pivot, relationship, investment]. Help me think through this rigorously using multiple frameworks: (1) Weighted Pros & Cons — list every pro and con, then assign importance weights (1-10) to calculate a weighted score, (2) 10/10/10 Rule — how will I feel about this in 10 minutes, 10 months, and 10 years?, (3) Pre-Mortem — imagine it went horribly wrong — what happened? Now imagine it went perfectly — what happened?, (4) Opportunity Cost — what am I giving up? What doors close?, (5) The Regret Test — at age 80, which choice would I regret NOT making?, (6) Reversibility Check — how easily can I undo this?, (7) Final Verdict — a clear recommendation with confidence level and the single most important factor driving the decision.
