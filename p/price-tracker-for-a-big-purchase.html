Price Tracker for a Big Purchase

Compares current prices for something you plan to buy and tells you whether to buy now or wait.

GeminiPersonal Finance & Investing
80 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me get the best price on [PRODUCT, MODEL].

Where I can buy: [COUNTRY / STORES]
Budget: [AMOUNT]
When I need it: [DATE]

Search current prices across major retailers, including refurbished or open-box options. Give me a table with store, price, shipping, return policy and warranty. Tell me whether prices usually drop around upcoming sales events, what a good price looks like, and whether to buy now or wait. Note that prices change quickly and should be checked before buying.
price comparisondealsshopping

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT, MODEL], [COUNTRY / STORES], [AMOUNT], [DATE].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
