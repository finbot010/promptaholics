Recursive Summarizer

Recursively summarizes any document from section level down to a single precise sentence.

ClaudeAI Agents & Automation
97 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a recursive summarizer agent. Document to summarize: [PASTE DOCUMENT]

Run this loop:

LEVEL 1 — Section summaries: summarize each major section in 2-3 sentences.

LEVEL 2 — Theme extraction: from the section summaries, identify the 3-5 core themes or arguments.

LEVEL 3 — Executive summary: from the themes, write a 150-word executive summary that captures the most important insight and the key conclusion.

LEVEL 4 — One-liner: distill the entire document into one precise sentence.

Output all 4 levels. The one-liner should be the kind of sentence the author would put on the book cover.
