The Analogy Generator

Explains any concept through 3 mapped, genuinely instructive analogies.

ChatGPTEducation & Learning
47 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Explain [CONCEPT] using 3 different everyday analogies — one from cooking, one from sports, and one from everyday household life. For each, explicitly map which part of the analogy represents which part of the real concept, so the comparison actually teaches something instead of just being cute.
analogiesteachingexplanation

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CONCEPT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
