The Emergency Fund Target Calculator

Calculates a personalized emergency fund target and savings pace based on real expenses.

ClaudePersonal Finance & Investing
45 words · free to use

My monthly essential expenses (rent, utilities, food, insurance, minimum debt payments) total roughly [amount]. Help me figure out a reasonable emergency fund target range for my situation — I'm [employment type, e.g. salaried/freelance/dual-income] — and a realistic monthly savings amount to reach it in [timeframe].
