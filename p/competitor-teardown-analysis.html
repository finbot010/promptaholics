Competitor Teardown Analysis

A structured competitive breakdown that ends in one usable positioning angle.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
48 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Analyze [COMPETITOR] as a competitor to my business, [MY BUSINESS/PRODUCT]. Break it down into: what they clearly do better than me, what I do better than them, where we're just different (not better/worse), and one specific positioning angle I could use in marketing that they can't easily copy.
competitor analysisstrategypositioning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [COMPETITOR], [MY BUSINESS/PRODUCT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
