Logo Concept Explorer

Generates logo concepts in several directions so you can pick a style before hiring a designer.

GeminiImage & Art
96 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Generate logo concepts for my brand.

Brand name: [NAME]
What the business does: [DESCRIPTION]
Personality: [3 WORDS, e.g. friendly, bold, premium]
Colors I like or must use: [COLORS]
Styles to explore: wordmark, simple icon + wordmark, emblem/badge, abstract mark

Create 4 concepts, one per style, on a plain white background, flat vector style, no mockups. Spell the brand name exactly as written. Then describe each concept in one line and say which would work best at small sizes (favicon, app icon). Note that final logos should be redrawn as vector files and checked for trademark conflicts.
logobrandingdesign concepts

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME], [DESCRIPTION], [3 WORDS, e.g. friendly, bold, premium], [COLORS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
