Freezer Meal Prep Day Plan

Plans a freezer cooking day that stocks a month of meals, with labels and reheating steps.

ClaudeFood & Cooking
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan a freezer meal prep day.

Number of meals I want: [NUMBER] meals for [NUMBER] people
Time I have: [HOURS]
Freezer space: [SMALL / MEDIUM / CHEST FREEZER]
Likes and restrictions: [DETAILS]
Equipment: [SLOW COOKER, INSTANT POT, OVEN, FREEZER BAGS, FOIL PANS]

Pick recipes that freeze well and share ingredients. Give me a shopping list, a cooking order so I'm never waiting, how to package each meal flat and labeled (name, date, reheating steps), how long each keeps frozen, and which foods to avoid freezing.
freezer mealsbatch cookingmeal prep

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER], [HOURS], [SMALL / MEDIUM / CHEST FREEZER], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
