Podcast Show Notes Writer

Full podcast show notes with SEO descriptions, timestamps, pull quotes, and social media teasers.

81 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write comprehensive show notes for a podcast episode.

Podcast name: [NAME]
Episode title: [TITLE]
Episode number: [NUMBER]
Guest (if any): [NAME + ONE LINE BIO]
Main topic: [WHAT WAS DISCUSSED]
Key points: [LIST 5-8 TAKEAWAYS]
Timestamps: [PASTE OR DESCRIBE]
Links mentioned: [LIST]

Create:
1. SEO-optimized description (150-200 words)
2. Bullet-point timestamps
3. Key quotes (3-5 pull quotes for social)
4. Resources and links section
5. 5 SEO keyword phrases
6. Social media teaser (Twitter/X and LinkedIn)

Tone: [MATCH THE PODCAST VOICE]
podcastshow notesSEOcontentaudio

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME], [TITLE], [NUMBER], [NAME + ONE LINE BIO], [WHAT WAS DISCUSSED], [LIST 5-8 TAKEAWAYS], [PASTE OR DESCRIBE], [LIST] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
