Niche Content Strategy

Finds your unique content niche, differentiator, and first 10 content pieces to establish authority.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me find and dominate a profitable content niche.

Current content/topic: [WHAT YOU CURRENTLY POST]
Skills and experience: [WHAT YOU KNOW WELL]
Passions: [WHAT YOU COULD TALK ABOUT FOREVER]
Current audience: [SIZE]
Monetization goal: [HOW YOU WANT TO EARN]
Creators you admire: [LIST 3-5]

Provide:
1. Analysis of current positioning
2. Three niche-down options ranked by opportunity
3. My unique differentiator in one sentence
4. First 10 content pieces to establish authority
5. Direct competitors and the gap I fill
6. Monetization path for this niche
nichecontent strategypositioningcreatoraudience

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT YOU CURRENTLY POST], [WHAT YOU KNOW WELL], [WHAT YOU COULD TALK ABOUT FOREVER], [SIZE], [HOW YOU WANT TO EARN], [LIST 3-5].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
