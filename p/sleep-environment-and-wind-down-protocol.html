Include: ideal sleep environment setup (temperature, light, sound), a wind-down routine starting 90 minutes before bed, what to avoid in the 6 hours before sleep, morning routine that reinforces circadian rhythm, supplements with evidence-base (and safety notes), cognitive techniques for racing thoughts, and when these issues warrant seeing a sleep specialist. Science-backed recommendations only.
