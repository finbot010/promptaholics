Real Estate Agent Interview Questions

Prepares questions to compare real estate agents and spot red flags before you sign with one.

ClaudeReal Estate
77 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me choose a real estate agent.

I am: [BUYING / SELLING / BOTH]
Property type and area: [DETAILS]
Price range: [RANGE]
What matters to me: [e.g. local knowledge, communication, negotiation, marketing]

Give me: 15 interview questions grouped by experience, strategy, communication and fees; what good and weak answers sound like; red flags; how commissions and contract terms usually work and what is negotiable (tell me to check local rules); and a scorecard to compare 3 agents.
real estate agenthiringselling a home

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BUYING / SELLING / BOTH], [DETAILS], [RANGE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
