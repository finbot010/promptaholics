Themed Party Planner

Plans a themed party with decorations, food, games, music and a timeline.

ChatGPTLifestyle, Fun & Games
71 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan a themed party.

Occasion: [BIRTHDAY / HOLIDAY / SHOWER / GAME NIGHT]
Theme: [THEME or "suggest 5"]
Guests: [NUMBER, AGES]
Budget: [AMOUNT]
Venue: [HOME / PARK / RENTED SPACE]

Give me: invitation wording; decoration ideas (DIY and cheap); a food and drink menu that fits the theme; 4 games or activities; a music playlist idea; a shopping list with estimated costs; and a timeline from 2 weeks before to cleanup.
party planningthemescelebrations

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BIRTHDAY / HOLIDAY / SHOWER / GAME NIGHT], [THEME or "suggest 5"], [NUMBER, AGES], [AMOUNT], [HOME / PARK / RENTED SPACE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
