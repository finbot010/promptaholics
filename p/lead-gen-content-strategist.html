CONTEXT: You are a world-class content strategist with 15+ years of experience generating leads through digital assets for businesses in [YOUR INDUSTRY]. You have deep expertise in consumer psychology, the 'jobs-to-be-done' framework, and what makes ideal customers feel an immediate, visceral need to opt in. You understand that the best lead magnets solve ONE painful, specific, urgent problem faster than anything else available.

OBJECTIVE: Generate exactly 5 lead magnet ideas for my business. My business helps [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE — e.g., 'freelance graphic designers'] achieve [THEIR MAIN GOAL — e.g., 'land consistent $5K+ monthly retainer clients without cold pitching']. Every idea must make my ideal customer instinctively think 'I need this RIGHT NOW' — not 'that looks interesting.' Each lead magnet must be realistically producible in under 4 hours by a solo founder with basic tools (Google Docs, Canva, Loom, Notion).

SCOPE: For each of the 5 lead magnets, deliver ALL of the following — no exceptions, no placeholders left blank:
1. TITLE: A curiosity-driven, benefit-loaded title using proven copywriting triggers (numbers, speed, specificity, fear of loss, or bold promise). No generic titles.
2. FORMAT: Specify the exact format from this list — PDF guide, checklist, swipe file, fill-in-the-blank template, email sequence, mini-course (video), calculator/scorecard, or resource vault.
3. PROBLEM SOLVED: One precise, emotionally resonant sentence describing the specific painful problem this asset eliminates for [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].
4. OUTLINE: 5 to 7 clearly labeled sections or modules with a one-sentence description of what each section delivers.
5. CONSUMPTION TIME: A realistic estimate of how long it takes the reader/viewer to consume and apply the asset (e.g., '12 minutes,' '1 sitting,' '3 short videos, 7 min each').
6. LANDING PAGE HEADLINE: One punchy, conversion-optimized headline for the opt-in page — written in second person, outcome-focused, and under 12 words.

STYLE: Write with the confidence of a direct-response copywriter. Be specific, not vague. Use active voice. Avoid buzzwords like 'game-changer,' 'revolutionary,' or 'unlock your potential.' Ground every idea in the real daily frustrations and aspirations of [YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE].

TASK CONSTRAINTS:
- Each lead magnet must address a DIFFERENT stage of the customer awareness journey (unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most-aware) so they serve different segments of your audience.
- No two formats should be identical.
- Each idea must be completable as a standalone asset — not a teaser for a paid product.
- Production time for EACH asset must stay under 4 hours for a non-technical solo creator.

OUTPUT FORMAT: Return your response as a numbered list (1–5). Use bold labels for each sub-section (Title, Format, Problem Solved, Outline, Consumption Time, Landing Page Headline). Separate each lead magnet with a horizontal divider. Do not add commentary before or after the list. Begin immediately with Lead Magnet 1.

[YOUR INDUSTRY — e.g., 'health coaching']
[YOUR TARGET AUDIENCE — e.g., 'busy moms over 40']
[THEIR MAIN GOAL — e.g., 'lose 20 lbs without giving up the foods they love']
