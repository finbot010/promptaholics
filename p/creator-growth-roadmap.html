Build a 90-day growth roadmap for [CREATOR] in [NICHE] at [FOLLOWER COUNT] on [PLATFORM]. Month 1 — Foundation: audit, optimize profile, define content pillars, set up systems. Month 2 — Momentum: daily posting, test formats, first collab. Month 3 — Scale: double down on top format, build email list, launch first offer. Include weekly milestones, key metrics, and the single most important action each week. End with success criteria for day 90.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CREATOR], [NICHE], [FOLLOWER COUNT], [PLATFORM].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
