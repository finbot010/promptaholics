Recipe From What Is in My Fridge

Creates 3 recipes using only what you already have, ranked by effort, so nothing goes to waste.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
81 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want to cook with what I already have.

Ingredients in my fridge and pantry: [LIST]
Must use up soon: [ITEMS]
Equipment: [STOVE / OVEN / AIR FRYER / MICROWAVE ONLY]
Time: [MINUTES]
Dietary needs: [NONE / DETAILS]

Give me 3 recipes ranked from easiest to most impressive, using only my ingredients plus basic staples (salt, oil, pepper). For each: ingredient amounts, numbered steps, total time, and one swap if I am missing something. Tell me how to store leftovers safely.
recipesleftoversno waste

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST], [ITEMS], [STOVE / OVEN / AIR FRYER / MICROWAVE ONLY], [MINUTES], [NONE / DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
