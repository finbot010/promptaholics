Home Inspection Report Translator

Turns a long home inspection report into prioritized issues, rough costs and negotiation points.

ClaudeReal Estate
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Translate this home inspection report into decisions I can make. I will get contractor quotes for anything serious.

Location: [CITY/REGION]
My budget for repairs after closing: [AMOUNT]
Inspection report:
[PASTE]

Give me: a priority table (Safety / Major / Minor / Cosmetic) with each issue, what it means in plain English, and a rough cost range to verify locally; issues that need a specialist (roof, foundation, electrical, sewer); what to ask the seller to fix, credit, or reduce the price for; and questions for the inspector.
home inspectionbuyersrepairs

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY/REGION], [AMOUNT], [PASTE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
