Cultural Recipe Story and Method

Explains a traditional dish's background and teaches the method, including regional variations.

ClaudeFood & Cooking
78 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Teach me to cook [DISH] from [REGION / COUNTRY].

My kitchen and skill level: [DETAILS]
Ingredients that may be hard to find where I live: [LOCATION]

Give me: a short, accurate background on the dish and how it is traditionally eaten; a reliable recipe with steps and why each step matters; regional variations; respectful substitutions for hard-to-find ingredients (and which ones change the dish too much); and common mistakes. Note anything about the dish's history that is debated.
traditional recipesfood historycooking

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DISH], [REGION / COUNTRY], [DETAILS], [LOCATION].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
