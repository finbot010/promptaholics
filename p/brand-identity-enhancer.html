Rewrite this brand identity to be clearer, stronger, and more emotionally aligned. Current identity: [DESCRIBE — include positioning, tone, visual style, audience]. Enhance by: sharpening the core belief the brand stands for, making the audience more specific, clarifying what the brand is NOT (equally important), strengthening the emotional promise, and making the visual and verbal identity work together as a unified system. Provide an enhanced brand identity card — one page that captures everything needed to build consistently on this brand.
