Seasonal Produce Recipe Ideas

Tells you what is in season where you live right now and gives simple recipes for each.

GeminiFood & Cooking
67 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
What fruits and vegetables are in season right now in [REGION / COUNTRY]?

List the top 10 with how to pick a good one and how to store it. Then give me 5 simple recipes that use several of them, each under [MINUTES] minutes, plus one idea for preserving extras (freezing, pickling, sauce). Suggest farmers markets near [CITY] if you can find them, with days and hours.
seasonalproducefarmers market

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [REGION / COUNTRY], [MINUTES], [CITY].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
