Morning Routine Designer

A science-backed morning routine built around your specific goals and schedule — not a generic 5am cold shower plan.

ChatGPTMore Prompts
81 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Design my ideal morning routine.

Wake-up time: [TIME]
Time available before work/obligations: [DURATION]
Main goal: [ENERGY/FOCUS/CALM/FITNESS/CREATIVITY]
Current struggles: [WHAT IS NOT WORKING]
Must-haves: [NON-NEGOTIABLES]
Things I hate: [WHAT TO AVOID]

Create:
1. Minute-by-minute schedule
2. The science behind each element you included
3. A 5-minute emergency version for chaotic mornings
4. The one habit that anchors the whole routine
5. How to make this sustainable long-term (not just day 1 motivation)
6. What to track to know it is actually working
morning routineproductivityhabitswellnessdaily routine

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TIME], [DURATION], [ENERGY/FOCUS/CALM/FITNESS/CREATIVITY], [WHAT IS NOT WORKING], [NON-NEGOTIABLES], [WHAT TO AVOID].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
