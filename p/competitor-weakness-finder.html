Competitor Weakness Finder

Finds specific, exploitable competitor weaknesses instead of generic SWOT filler.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
140 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a competitive strategy analyst. I'm going to describe my competitor and I need you to find the gaps I can actually exploit — not generic "they could improve customer service" advice.

Competitor: [COMPETITOR NAME/DESCRIPTION]
Their main product: [WHAT THEY SELL]
What they're known for: [THEIR REPUTATION/STRENGTH]
My business: [WHAT I DO]

Give me:
1. **Three specific weaknesses** in their offer, pricing, or positioning — not vague, something I could point to in a sales conversation
2. **One thing they're clearly avoiding** (a customer segment, a use case, a price point) and why that might be a real opportunity, not just an oversight
3. **A one-sentence positioning line** I could use that directly contrasts with their weakest point, without naming them
4. A gut-check: is this weakness worth building a strategy around, or is it too minor to matter?
competitor analysisstrategybusiness

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [COMPETITOR NAME/DESCRIPTION], [WHAT THEY SELL], [THEIR REPUTATION/STRENGTH], [WHAT I DO].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
