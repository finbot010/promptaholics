Allergy-Safe Recipe Adapter

Adapts a recipe to remove an allergen and checks which store-bought ingredients often hide it.

GeminiFood & Cooking
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Adapt this recipe to be free of [ALLERGEN(S)].

Recipe:
[PASTE OR LINK]
Who it is for: [e.g. child with a severe nut allergy]
Store or country: [WHERE I SHOP]

Give me: the adapted recipe with safe substitutions and amounts; ingredients that commonly contain hidden versions of the allergen (sauces, spice blends, baked goods) and what to look for on labels; cross-contamination tips for my kitchen; and how the substitutions change taste or texture. For severe allergies, remind me to read every label and follow the allergist's advice.
food allergiessafe cookingsubstitutions

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ALLERGEN(S)], [PASTE OR LINK], [WHERE I SHOP].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
