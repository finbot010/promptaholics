The Song Crafter

Complete song lyrics with full song structure, sensory imagery, earworm hook, BPM, and key suggestion.

ChatGPTImage & Art
99 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write complete song lyrics for a [GENRE — pop / hip-hop / country / R&B / rock / indie] track about [THEME/EMOTION]. Structure: Verse 1 (set the scene), Pre-Chorus (build tension), Chorus (the anthem hook — singable and repeatable), Verse 2 (deepen the story), Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Bridge (emotional peak — surprise twist), Final Chorus (slight variation for impact), Outro. Use [RHYME SCHEME — ABAB / AABB / free verse]. Include vivid sensory imagery — make people feel it, not just hear it. The hook should be stuck in someone's head after one listen. Suggest a tempo (BPM) and key.
songwritinglyricsmusiccreativecomposition

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [THEME/EMOTION], [RHYME SCHEME — ABAB / AABB / free verse].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
