Rental Property ROI Calculator

Full rental property investment analysis — cash flow, ROI, cap rate, and 10-year projections.

Analyze the return on investment for this rental property.

Purchase price: $[PRICE] | Down payment: [%]
Interest rate: [%] | Loan term: [YEARS]
Expected monthly rent: $[RENT]
Property tax (annual): $[TAX]
Insurance (annual): $[INSURANCE]
HOA (monthly): $[HOA or 0]
Maintenance (monthly): $[AMOUNT]
Property management: [%] or self-managed
Vacancy rate: [%]

Calculate and explain:
1. Monthly mortgage payment
2. Total monthly expenses
3. Monthly cash flow
4. Cash-on-cash return
5. Cap rate
6. Break-even analysis
7. 5 and 10-year projections
8. Verdict: good deal or pass?

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRICE], [YEARS], [RENT], [TAX], [INSURANCE], [HOA or 0], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
