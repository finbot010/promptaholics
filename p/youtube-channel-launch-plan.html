YouTube Channel Launch Plan

Plans the first 90 days of a new YouTube channel: niche, first 12 videos and a publishing rhythm.

ChatGPTVideo & Film
84 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan the launch of my YouTube channel.

Topic and audience: [DETAILS]
What I can make: [FORMATS, TIME PER WEEK, GEAR]
Why people would watch me: [EXPERTISE / PERSONALITY / ANGLE]
Goal for 90 days: [e.g. 1,000 subscribers, a consistent habit, leads for my business]

Give me: a sharp channel positioning; the first 12 video ideas in publishing order (mix of searchable and shareable); a weekly production routine; channel name and banner ideas; what to measure each week; and what to change if videos underperform.
youtubechannel launchgrowth

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DETAILS], [FORMATS, TIME PER WEEK, GEAR], [EXPERTISE / PERSONALITY / ANGLE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
