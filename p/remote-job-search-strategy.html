Remote Job Search Strategy

Finds where remote jobs in your field are posted now and builds a weekly search plan.

GeminiCareer & Resume
83 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me find a remote job.

Role and field: [DETAILS]
Experience level: [LEVEL]
Time zone and countries I can legally work for: [DETAILS]
Salary target: [AMOUNT]

Search and tell me: the job boards and communities where remote roles in my field are posted now; companies known for hiring remotely in my field; keywords to search; red flags of fake remote job scams; and a weekly plan (applications, networking, portfolio work). Note how to check that a remote role accepts people in my location.
remote jobsjob boardsjob search

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DETAILS], [LEVEL], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
