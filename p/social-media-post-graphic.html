Social Media Post Graphic

Designs a scroll-stopping social media graphic with your headline, brand colors and the right size.

GeminiImage & Art
89 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a social media graphic.

Platform and size: [INSTAGRAM POST 4:5 / STORY 9:16 / LINKEDIN 1.91:1 / X 16:9]
Headline text (spell exactly): "[HEADLINE]"
Supporting text (optional): "[SUBTEXT]"
Brand colors: [HEX CODES]
Style: [MINIMAL / BOLD TYPOGRAPHY / PHOTO BACKGROUND / ILLUSTRATED]
Mood: [ENERGETIC / CALM / PREMIUM / PLAYFUL]

Keep text large and readable on a phone, with strong contrast and plenty of empty space. Make 3 versions with different layouts. If any text renders incorrectly, tell me so I can add it in an editor instead.
social mediagraphicsinstagram

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [HEADLINE], [SUBTEXT], [HEX CODES], [ENERGETIC / CALM / PREMIUM / PLAYFUL].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
