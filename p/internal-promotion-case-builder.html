Internal Promotion Case Builder

Builds a written case for your promotion with evidence, impact and a plan for the conversation.

ClaudeCareer & Resume
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me build a case for a promotion.

Current role and level: [DETAILS]
Role I want: [TITLE / LEVEL]
What that next level is expected to do (from career framework or job description): [PASTE]
My evidence: [PROJECTS, RESULTS, FEEDBACK, EXTRA RESPONSIBILITIES]
My manager's style: [DETAILS]

Give me: a one-page promotion case mapping my evidence to each expectation; gaps and how to address them; how to start the conversation; answers to "not yet" and "there is no budget"; and a 90-day plan if the answer is not yet.
promotioncareer growthnegotiation

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DETAILS], [TITLE / LEVEL], [PASTE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
