Customer Support Macro Library

Creates ready-to-use support reply templates for your most common questions, in your brand voice.

ChatGPTEmail, Sales & Communication
88 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a library of support reply templates (macros).

Business: [WHAT YOU SELL]
Brand voice: [e.g. warm and casual / professional]
Most common requests: [e.g. where is my order, refund request, login problem, how do I cancel]
Policies: [REFUND / SHIPPING / CANCELLATION RULES]

For each request: a template with [placeholders] for personal details, a short version for chat, and a note on when not to use it. Keep each human and specific. Add 3 general macros: asking for more info, escalating to a specialist, and closing a ticket.
supporttemplateshelp desk

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT YOU SELL], [REFUND / SHIPPING / CANCELLATION RULES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
