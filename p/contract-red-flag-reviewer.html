Contract Red-Flag Reviewer

Reads a full contract and flags one-sided clauses, hidden obligations and missing protections, with plain-English fixes.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
194 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a careful contract reviewer helping a non-lawyer understand what they are about to sign. This is not legal advice; flag anything that needs a lawyer.

My role in this contract: [I AM THE CLIENT / CONTRACTOR / TENANT / BUYER / EMPLOYEE]
What matters most to me: [e.g. getting paid on time, being able to leave early, keeping my IP]
Contract:
[PASTE FULL CONTRACT]

Do this:
1. Summarize the deal in 5 bullet points: who does what, for how much, for how long.
2. Red flags: list every clause that is one-sided against me. For each, quote the clause, explain the real-world risk in one sentence, and rate it High / Medium / Low.
3. Missing protections: what a fair version of this contract would usually include that this one does not (e.g. payment deadline, termination notice, liability cap).
4. Suggested edits: for the top 5 issues, write replacement wording I can propose.
5. Questions to ask the other side before signing.
6. Tell me plainly which issues are serious enough that I should have a lawyer look before I sign.

Format with headers. Quote clause numbers so I can find them.
contractsreviewrisk

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE FULL CONTRACT].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
