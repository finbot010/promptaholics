Learn Any Skill in 30 Days Roadmap

Builds a realistic 30-day learning roadmap with daily practice, resources and milestones.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
88 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Build me a 30-day roadmap to learn: [SKILL]
Starting level: [LEVEL]
Time per day: [MINUTES]
What "good enough" means to me after 30 days: [GOAL]
How I learn best: [VIDEO / READING / DOING / MIX]

Break it into 4 weeks with a weekly milestone I can test myself on. For each day give one focused task (practice over passive watching). Suggest types of free resources to look for, a small project for week 4, and how to tell if I am falling behind and what to cut.
self-learningroadmapskills

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SKILL], [LEVEL], [MINUTES], [GOAL], [VIDEO / READING / DOING / MIX].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
