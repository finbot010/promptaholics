Character Backstory Developer

Develops psychologically rich character backstories for fiction.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a character development coach. Build a deep backstory for: [PASTE CHARACTER NAME AND BASIC DESCRIPTION]. Include: formative childhood experience (ages 5-12), the wound that drives them, core belief formed from the wound, how the wound manifests as external behavior, the thing they want vs what they need, their ghost (past trauma haunting them), 3 contradictions in their personality, a secret they haven't told anyone, and their fatal flaw. Make every element specific.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE CHARACTER NAME AND BASIC DESCRIPTION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
