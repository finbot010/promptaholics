Honeymoon or Anniversary Trip Planner

Plans a romantic trip with destination ideas, a day-by-day plan and special touches within your budget.

ChatGPTTravel
78 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan a romantic trip.

Occasion: [HONEYMOON / ANNIVERSARY]
Dates and length: [DATES]
Budget total: [AMOUNT]
We love: [BEACH / ADVENTURE / FOOD / CULTURE / RELAXING]
We do not want: [e.g. long flights, crowds, all-inclusive resorts]
Departing from: [CITY]

Suggest 4 destinations that fit, with why. For our favorite: a day-by-day plan balancing activities and downtime, 3 special-moment ideas (sunset spot, a memorable dinner, a surprise), where to splurge and where to save, and what to book early.
honeymoonanniversaryromantic trip

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [HONEYMOON / ANNIVERSARY], [DATES], [AMOUNT], [BEACH / ADVENTURE / FOOD / CULTURE / RELAXING], [CITY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
