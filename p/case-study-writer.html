Case Study Writer

Story-driven case study that turns client results into compelling social proof and marketing content.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
119 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a compelling case study about a client result or project success.

Client/project: [NAME OR DESCRIPTION]
Industry: [THEIR INDUSTRY]
The problem they came to you with: [DESCRIBE]
What you did: [YOUR SOLUTION OR PROCESS]
The results achieved: [SPECIFIC NUMBERS AND OUTCOMES]
Timeline: [HOW LONG IT TOOK]
Client quote (if available): [PASTE QUOTE]

Write it in this format:
1. Hook headline (the result in one punchy line)
2. The Challenge (make the reader feel the pain)
3. The Solution (what you did, step by step)
4. The Results (lead with numbers, be specific)
5. Client quote or reaction
6. Key takeaway for the reader
7. CTA (work with us / download / read more)

Tone: Storytelling-first. Confident but not boastful.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME OR DESCRIPTION], [THEIR INDUSTRY], [DESCRIBE], [YOUR SOLUTION OR PROCESS], [SPECIFIC NUMBERS AND OUTCOMES], [HOW LONG IT TOOK], [PASTE QUOTE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
