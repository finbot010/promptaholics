Character Voice Consistency Checker

Checks whether each character's dialogue sounds distinct and consistent across your chapters.

ClaudeImage & Art
69 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Check my characters' voices.

Character notes (age, background, personality): [LIST]
Chapters or scenes:
[PASTE]

For each main character: describe how they actually sound in these pages (vocabulary, sentence length, humor, what they avoid saying); flag lines that sound like another character or out of character; and suggest rewrites for the 5 weakest lines. Then give me a one-paragraph voice guide per character to keep next to me while writing.
charactersdialoguefiction editing

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST], [PASTE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
