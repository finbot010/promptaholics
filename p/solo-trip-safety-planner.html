Solo Trip Safety Planner

Builds a destination-specific solo travel safety plan without generic fear-based advice.

ChatGPTTravel
126 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a seasoned solo traveler who takes safety seriously without being paranoid or ruining the trip. Build me a safety plan.

Destination: [WHERE YOU'RE GOING]
Trip length: [DURATION]
My experience level with solo travel: [FIRST TIME / EXPERIENCED]
Specific concerns I have: [ANY SPECIFIC WORRIES]

Give me:
1. Destination-specific safety considerations that are actually relevant here, not generic "stay aware of your surroundings" advice
2. A check-in system with someone at home — specific frequency and what info they should have
3. What to do in the first few hours after arriving to establish situational awareness (not sightsee immediately)
4. Red flags specific to this destination that indicate a situation to leave immediately

Keep this practical, not fear-mongering — the goal is preparedness, not anxiety.
solo travelsafetytrip planning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHERE YOU'RE GOING], [DURATION], [FIRST TIME / EXPERIENCED], [ANY SPECIFIC WORRIES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
