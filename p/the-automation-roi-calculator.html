The Automation ROI Calculator

An honest time-saved-vs-setup-cost estimate before you automate anything.

ChatGPTAI Agents & Automation
48 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm considering automating this task: "[DESCRIBE THE TASK]", which currently takes me about [TIME] per [FREQUENCY, e.g. 'week']. Estimated setup time/cost for automating it is [ESTIMATE OR 'help me estimate']. Walk me through whether this is actually worth automating, with real numbers, not just "automation is always good."
automationROIproductivity

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE THE TASK], [TIME], [FREQUENCY, e.g. 'week'], [ESTIMATE OR 'help me estimate'].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
