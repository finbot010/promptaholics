Journaling Prompts for Reflection

Gives a month of journaling prompts tailored to what you are working through.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
72 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Give me 30 days of journaling prompts.

What I want to reflect on: [e.g. a career change, a breakup, confidence, gratitude, burnout]
Time per day: [MINUTES]
Style I like: [QUESTIONS / SENTENCE STARTERS / LISTS]

Make each week build on the last (noticing, understanding, deciding, acting). Include one prompt per day, a weekly review prompt, and 3 quick prompts for days I do not feel like writing. Keep them specific, not generic.
journalingreflectionself-awareness

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MINUTES], [QUESTIONS / SENTENCE STARTERS / LISTS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
