Runway Gen-3 Director

Write a Runway Gen-3 video generation prompt for [CONCEPT].

Write a Runway Gen-3 video generation prompt for [CONCEPT]. Include: subject description, action and movement, camera behavior, environment and atmosphere, color palette, and the 10-second arc from opening frame to final frame. Keep technical terms that Runway responds to: motion blur, depth of field, film grain.
runwayvideo AIgeneration

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CONCEPT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and send it.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
