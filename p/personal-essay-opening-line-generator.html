Personal Essay Opening Line Generator

Generates distinct personal-essay openings while naming the specific cliché each one avoids.

ClaudeWriting & Content
135 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are an editor who has read thousands of personal essay submissions and can spot a weak opening from a mile away. I'll describe what my essay is about, and I need opening lines that don't sound like every other essay on this topic.

What the essay is actually about: [THE CORE STORY/TOPIC]
The tone I'm going for: [REFLECTIVE / FUNNY / RAW / MATTER-OF-FACT]
A detail most people would leave out but I think matters: [SPECIFIC DETAIL]

Give me 5 different opening lines, each taking a genuinely different angle (a scene, a confession, a contradiction, an image, a piece of dialogue). For each one, explain in one sentence why it avoids the most common cliché opening for this kind of essay (don't just say "it's not cliché" — name the specific cliché it's avoiding).
essay writingpersonal writinghooks

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [THE CORE STORY/TOPIC], [REFLECTIVE / FUNNY / RAW / MATTER-OF-FACT], [SPECIFIC DETAIL].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
