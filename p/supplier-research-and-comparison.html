Supplier Research and Comparison

Researches and compares suppliers or manufacturers for your product with current pricing and terms.

GeminiBusiness & Strategy
70 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Find and compare suppliers for my business.

What I need: [PRODUCT / MATERIAL / SERVICE]
Quantity and frequency: [DETAILS]
Location preference: [LOCAL / COUNTRY / ANY]
Must-haves: [CERTIFICATIONS, MINIMUM ORDER, SHIPPING TIME]

Search and compare 6 suppliers: location, minimum order, price range if public, lead time, reviews or reputation, and contact. Then give me questions to ask each supplier, red flags to watch for, and a message to request quotes.
supplierssourcingcomparison

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT / MATERIAL / SERVICE], [DETAILS], [LOCAL / COUNTRY / ANY], [CERTIFICATIONS, MINIMUM ORDER, SHIPPING TIME].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
