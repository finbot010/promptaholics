Partnership Pitch Deck

Professional partnership proposal that shows strategic fit and a clear win-win structure.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
115 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a partnership proposal for [YOUR COMPANY] to partner with [TARGET COMPANY].

Your company: [DESCRIBE]
Target partner: [DESCRIBE THEIR BUSINESS]
Why this partnership makes sense: [THE STRATEGIC FIT]
What you bring to the table: [YOUR VALUE]
What you want from them: [THEIR VALUE]
Proposed structure: [CO-MARKETING / REVENUE SHARE / INTEGRATION / REFERRAL]
Expected outcome for both: [WIN-WIN RESULT]

Write:
1. Executive summary (150 words)
2. The opportunity (why now, why us, why them)
3. Proposed partnership structure (specific and concrete)
4. What each party does
5. Success metrics (how we measure it)
6. Next steps (specific — a call, a pilot, a document)

Tone: Peer-to-peer. Confident. Show you've done your homework on their business.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR COMPANY], [TARGET COMPANY], [DESCRIBE], [DESCRIBE THEIR BUSINESS], [THE STRATEGIC FIT], [YOUR VALUE], [THEIR VALUE], [WIN-WIN RESULT] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
