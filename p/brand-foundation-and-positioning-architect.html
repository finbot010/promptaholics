Act as a senior brand strategist at a global branding agency with 20+ years of experience. Your task is to develop a complete brand positioning framework for [BUSINESS NAME], a company operating in the [INDUSTRY] industry.

SITUATION: [BUSINESS NAME] is entering or evolving within the [INDUSTRY] market and needs a differentiated brand position.
COMPLICATION: The market is saturated with [NUMBER] competitors and audiences are skeptical of generic brand promises.
QUESTION: How do we carve a unique, defensible, and emotionally resonant position?
ANSWER: Develop the following in brand guidelines format:

1. BRAND POSITIONING STATEMENT
- Use the template: For [target audience] who [need/want], [BUSINESS NAME] is the [category] that [key benefit] because [reason to believe].
- Include primary, secondary, and aspirational positioning layers.

2. MISSION STATEMENT (3 versions)
- Functional version (what you do)
- Emotional version (how you make people feel)
- Purpose-driven version (why you exist beyond profit)

3. BRAND VISION (5-year and 10-year)

4. CORE VALUES (5 values with behavioral definitions)
- Each value must include: the value name, a one-line definition, and 2 real-world behavioral examples of how this value shows up.

5. BRAND PROMISE
- One sentence your brand commits to delivering every single time.

6. COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION MAP
- List [NUMBER] direct competitors
- Map their positioning on two axes: [AXIS 1] vs [AXIS 2]
- Identify the white space [BUSINESS NAME] owns

Format all output as sections of a professional Brand Guidelines Document with headers, subheaders, and clear rationale for each decision.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BUSINESS NAME], [INDUSTRY], [NUMBER], [AXIS 1], [AXIS 2].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
