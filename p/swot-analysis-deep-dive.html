SWOT Analysis Deep Dive

Conducts deep SWOT analysis with TOWS matrix and priority actions.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
73 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a business strategist. Conduct a comprehensive SWOT analysis for: [PASTE BUSINESS DESCRIPTION]. Go beyond surface level — list 5 items per quadrant with evidence or reasoning for each. Then create a TOWS matrix showing: how to use Strengths to exploit Opportunities (SO), how Strengths can defend against Threats (ST), how to use Opportunities to overcome Weaknesses (WO), and how to minimize Weaknesses and avoid Threats (WT). End with 3 priority actions.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE BUSINESS DESCRIPTION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
