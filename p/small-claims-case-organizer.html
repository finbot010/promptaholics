Small Claims Case Organizer

Organizes the facts, evidence and timeline for a small claims case and drafts a clear statement of claim.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
126 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me organize a small claims case. I understand this is not legal advice and I will check my local court's rules.

Location: [CITY/STATE/COUNTRY]
Who I am claiming against: [NAME/BUSINESS]
Amount: [AMOUNT]
What happened, in my own words:
[DESCRIBE]
Evidence I have: [CONTRACTS, MESSAGES, RECEIPTS, PHOTOS, WITNESSES]

Give me:
1. A clean timeline of events with dates.
2. The core claim in 3 sentences, as a judge would want to hear it.
3. Evidence list: what each item proves, and gaps I should fill.
4. The other side's likely arguments and how my evidence answers them.
5. A draft statement of claim.
6. A 2-minute opening statement I can practice out loud.
7. Questions to look up on my local court's website (filing fee, deadlines, limits).
small claimscourtevidence

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY/STATE/COUNTRY], [NAME/BUSINESS], [AMOUNT], [DESCRIBE], [CONTRACTS, MESSAGES, RECEIPTS, PHOTOS, WITNESSES].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
