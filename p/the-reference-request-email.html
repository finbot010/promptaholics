The Reference Request Email

Asks a former colleague for a reference without it feeling out of the blue.

ChatGPTCareer & Resume
48 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want to ask [FORMER COLLEAGUE/MANAGER], who I haven't spoken to in [TIME SINCE LAST CONTACT], to be a reference for a job application. Write a short, warm email that reconnects naturally before making the ask, and makes it easy for them to say yes or decline gracefully.
referencesjob searchemail

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [FORMER COLLEAGUE/MANAGER], [TIME SINCE LAST CONTACT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
