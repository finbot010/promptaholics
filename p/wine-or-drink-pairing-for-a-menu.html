Wine or Drink Pairing for a Menu

Suggests wine, beer or non-alcoholic pairings for your menu, with options you can find locally.

GeminiFood & Cooking
70 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Suggest drink pairings for my menu.

Menu: [DISHES]
Guests: [NUMBER, ANY WHO DO NOT DRINK]
Budget per bottle: [AMOUNT]
Where I shop: [STORE OR CITY]
Preferences: [WINE / BEER / COCKTAILS / NON-ALCOHOLIC]

For each course: 2 pairing options with why they work, a non-alcoholic option, and a typical price range. Search for options likely available at my store. Add serving temperatures and how much to buy for my group.
pairingwinenon-alcoholic drinks

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DISHES], [NUMBER, ANY WHO DO NOT DRINK], [AMOUNT], [STORE OR CITY], [WINE / BEER / COCKTAILS / NON-ALCOHOLIC].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
