Digital Detox Plan

A realistic 30-day digital detox plan that reduces screen time without going cold turkey.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
98 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me create a sustainable digital detox plan.

Current screen time: [HOURS/DAY]
Biggest habits: [SOCIAL MEDIA/STREAMING/GAMING/NEWS/DOOM SCROLLING]
Platforms hardest to quit: [LIST]
Why I want to detox: [ANXIETY/SLEEP/FOCUS/RELATIONSHIPS]
Work requires screens: [YES/NO — describe]
Goal: [REDUCE BY X HOURS/FULL BREAK/SPECIFIC PLATFORM]

Create:
1. Digital audit — which habits cost me most
2. Realistic 30-day reduction plan (not cold turkey)
3. Phone-free zones and times for my home
4. Replacement activities for each habit
5. How to manage FOMO and withdrawal
6. App settings and tools to make this easier
7. What to do with the time I reclaim
digital detoxscreen timesocial mediawellnessmindfulness

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [HOURS/DAY], [SOCIAL MEDIA/STREAMING/GAMING/NEWS/DOOM SCROLLING], [LIST], [ANXIETY/SLEEP/FOCUS/RELATIONSHIPS], [YES/NO — describe], [REDUCE BY X HOURS/FULL BREAK/SPECIFIC PLATFORM].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
