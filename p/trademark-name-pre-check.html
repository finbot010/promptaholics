Trademark Name Pre-Check

Walks you through checking whether a business or product name may run into trademark problems.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
88 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm thinking of naming my [BUSINESS / PRODUCT / APP] "[NAME]". It's in [INDUSTRY], selling in [COUNTRIES], mostly [ONLINE / LOCAL].

Help me pre-check it: explain what makes a name strong or weak as a trademark (generic, descriptive, suggestive, invented); list where to search for conflicts (the official trademark database for my country, business registries, domains, social handles, app stores); what "likely to cause confusion" generally means; and 5 alternative names that are more distinctive. Recommend when it's worth paying a trademark attorney to do a full search.
trademarkbrand namebusiness name

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BUSINESS / PRODUCT / APP], [NAME], [INDUSTRY], [COUNTRIES], [ONLINE / LOCAL].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
