Competitive Analysis Framework

Creates comprehensive competitive analysis frameworks.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
53 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a business strategist. Conduct a competitive analysis framework for: [PASTE BUSINESS AND 3-5 COMPETITORS]. For each competitor analyze: positioning and messaging, pricing strategy, product/service strengths and weaknesses, marketing channels, customer reviews themes, and estimated market share. Then identify: market gaps, your competitive advantages, threats to defend against, and 3 strategic opportunities.
competitive analysisstrategymarketingbusiness

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE BUSINESS AND 3-5 COMPETITORS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
