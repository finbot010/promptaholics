Brand Storytelling Framework

A complete brand story in three formats using the Hero's Journey framework — how the best brands emotionally connect.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
95 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me craft a compelling brand origin story for [BRAND NAME].

Founder background: [BRIEF BIO]
The problem that started it all: [ORIGIN MOMENT]
What existed before: [OLD SOLUTIONS AND WHY THEY FAILED]
The breakthrough: [WHAT YOU FIGURED OUT]
Who you serve: [CUSTOMER]
Mission: [WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND PROFIT]

Write three versions:
1. The 30-second elevator pitch version
2. The 2-minute investor/media version
3. The long-form About page version (500 words)

Each should follow the Hero's Journey framework where the customer is the hero and the brand is the guide — not the other way around.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BRAND NAME], [BRIEF BIO], [ORIGIN MOMENT], [OLD SOLUTIONS AND WHY THEY FAILED], [WHAT YOU FIGURED OUT], [CUSTOMER], [WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND PROFIT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
