Email Subject Line Generator

Generates 20 subject line options using proven formulas with open rate predictions.

ChatGPTEmail, Sales & Communication
104 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are an email copywriter specializing in subject lines. Generate 20 subject lines for this email. Email topic: [WHAT THE EMAIL IS ABOUT]. Audience: [WHO IS RECEIVING IT]. Email goal: [GET THEM TO OPEN AND ACT]. Brand tone: [FORMAL / CASUAL / FUNNY / DIRECT]. Write 20 subject lines using: 4 curiosity gap lines (make them wonder), 4 benefit-focused lines (what they get), 4 urgency or scarcity lines, 4 personalization hooks (speaks to their situation), 4 contrarian or surprising lines. For each rate likely open rate as low, medium, or high and explain why in one sentence. Flag the top 3 to test first.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT THE EMAIL IS ABOUT], [WHO IS RECEIVING IT], [GET THEM TO OPEN AND ACT], [FORMAL / CASUAL / FUNNY / DIRECT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
