Predictive Eye Tracking Heatmap Analysis

Predicts where users' eyes would focus on a UI screenshot using cognitive science principles.

ClaudeCode & Tech
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Act as a Senior UX Researcher simulating predictive visual attention modeling for a UI screenshot. Apply cognitive science principles: biological priority (faces/eyes get immediate highest-intensity focus), the Von Restorff effect (high-contrast or unique elements get high-priority fixation), F-pattern scanning gravity (top-left to bottom-right reading bias), and goal-directed affordance seeking (buttons and interactive elements draw attention). Describe where a heatmap overlay would show hot (red), warm (yellow/orange), and cold (blue) zones based on these principles.
