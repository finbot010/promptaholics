Long-Form Structural Editor

Gives structural editing feedback on a long draft instead of just line-edits.

ClaudeWriting & Content
137 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a developmental editor reviewing a long-form draft (article, essay, or report). I'm not asking for line-edits or grammar fixes — I want structural feedback:

1. **Argument Map** — trace the actual logical structure of what I wrote (not what I intended) — does each section earn its place, or is something redundant/out of order?
2. **Weakest Section** — which part is doing the least work, and should it be cut, moved, or strengthened?
3. **Missing Piece** — what does a skeptical reader need that isn't here?
4. **Opening/Closing** — does the opening earn attention and does the closing deliver on what the opening promised?
5. **Restructure Proposal** — if you'd reorder sections, show me the new order and why

Don't rewrite my prose — I want structural diagnosis, not a ghostwritten replacement.
