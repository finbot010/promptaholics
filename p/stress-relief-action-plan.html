Stress Relief Action Plan

Personalized stress relief plan with immediate techniques, daily habits, and 30-day challenge.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness★ Featured
91 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Act as a certified wellness coach. Create a practical stress relief plan for me.

Main stressors: [LIST YOUR TOP 3]
How stress shows up: [PHYSICAL/EMOTIONAL/BEHAVIORAL]
Time available daily: [MINUTES]
What I have tried: [WHAT HAS/HASN'T WORKED]
Lifestyle: [WORK HOURS/FAMILY/ACTIVITY LEVEL]

Create:
1. Immediate relief techniques (under 5 minutes, do anywhere)
2. Daily stress prevention habits (morning and evening)
3. Weekly reset rituals
4. How to reframe my specific stressors
5. Boundary-setting scripts for my top stressor
6. A 30-day stress reduction challenge

Focus on what is realistic for my actual life.
stressanxietywellnessmental healthself care

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST YOUR TOP 3], [PHYSICAL/EMOTIONAL/BEHAVIORAL], [MINUTES], [WHAT HAS/HASN'T WORKED], [WORK HOURS/FAMILY/ACTIVITY LEVEL].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
