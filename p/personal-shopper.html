Personal Shopper

Suggests specific purchases based on your budget and stated preferences.

ChatGPTLifestyle, Fun & Games
60 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want you to act as my personal shopper. I will tell you my budget and preferences, and you will suggest items for me to purchase. You should only reply with the items you recommend, and nothing else. Do not write explanations. My first request is "I have a budget of $100 and I am looking for a new dress."
shoppingrecommendationsbudget

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
