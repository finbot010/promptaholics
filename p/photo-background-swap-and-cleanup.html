Photo Background Swap and Cleanup

Edits your uploaded photo to remove clutter, swap the background or fix lighting while keeping the subject natural.

GeminiImage & Art
93 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Edit the photo I uploaded. [UPLOAD PHOTO]

Keep exactly the same: [e.g. the person's face, expression, clothing / the product]
Change: [e.g. replace the messy room with a clean bright office / remove the people in the background / make it look like golden hour]
Style: natural and realistic, matching light direction and shadows so the edit is not obvious
Output size: [SAME AS ORIGINAL / 1:1 / 16:9]

Do not change facial features, body shape or skin tone. If the edit cannot be done realistically, tell me what would look better instead.
photo editingbackground removalimage editing

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [UPLOAD PHOTO], [SAME AS ORIGINAL / 1:1 / 16:9].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
