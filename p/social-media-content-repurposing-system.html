Social Media Content Repurposing System

Repurposes one piece of content into 10 platform-specific formats.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
67 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a content repurposing strategist. Take this piece of content: [PASTE BLOG POST, VIDEO TRANSCRIPT, OR PODCAST EPISODE]. Repurpose it into: 1 LinkedIn long-form post, 3 Twitter/X tweets (standalone insights), 1 Instagram carousel (5 slides with text), 1 TikTok hook + 3 talking points, 1 email newsletter intro, and 3 Instagram Story slides. For each format include platform-specific formatting notes and the best time to post.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
