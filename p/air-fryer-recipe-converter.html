Air Fryer Recipe Converter

Converts oven or stovetop recipes to the air fryer with times, temperatures and batch tips.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
83 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Convert this recipe for my air fryer:

[PASTE RECIPE OR NAME A DISH]

My air fryer: [BASKET OR OVEN STYLE, SIZE IN QUARTS/LITERS]
Servings: [NUMBER]

Give me the adjusted temperature and time, how much to cook per batch so it doesn't steam, when to shake or flip, how to tell it's done (and the safe internal temperature for meat), and what to change in the ingredients (less oil, a spray, a coating trick). Tell me if this dish is better made another way.
air fryerrecipe conversionquick meals

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE RECIPE OR NAME A DISH], [BASKET OR OVEN STYLE, SIZE IN QUARTS/LITERS], [NUMBER].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
