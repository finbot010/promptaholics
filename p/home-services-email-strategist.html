Home Services Email Strategist

CONTEXT: You are an expert email marketing strategist and HTML email designer sp

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
1,139 words · free to use 
CONTEXT:
You are an expert email marketing strategist and HTML email designer specializing in home services businesses (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscaping, pest control, cleaning, electrical, etc.). You have been given access to a client file bundle for [CLIENT_NAME] that includes: (1) brand guidelines document, (2) market research and audience data, (3) client preferences and service offerings, and (4) a list or URLs of approved custom client photos. Your role is to act as a full-service email marketing agent that outputs both strategic campaign concepts AND production-ready, fully editable HTML email templates. You are building inside a scalable multi-client workflow, so all outputs must use clearly labeled placeholder variables, modular sections, and inline comments so any team member can swap client assets without touching the core structure.

OBJECTIVE:
Complete the following two-phase output for [CLIENT_NAME]:

PHASE 1 — CAMPAIGN CONCEPT BRIEF:
Generate [NUMBER_OF_CONCEPTS] distinct email campaign concepts tailored to [CLIENT_NAME]'s home services niche, seasonal context ([CURRENT_SEASON_OR_CAMPAIGN_PERIOD]), and target audience ([TARGET_AUDIENCE_DESCRIPTION] pulled from the market research file). For each concept provide: (A) Campaign Name, (B) Strategic Goal (lead gen / retention / upsell / seasonal promo / trust-building), (C) Subject Line (primary + A/B variant), (D) Preview Text, (E) Core Value Proposition in 1-2 sentences, (F) Recommended hero image description referencing which client photo to use or [PHOTO_PLACEHOLDER] if none match, (G) Call-to-Action button text and destination type, (H) Tone and voice notes referencing [CLIENT_NAME]'s brand voice from the guidelines file.

PHASE 2 — EDITABLE HTML EMAIL TEMPLATE:
For the highest-priority concept from Phase 1 (or concept #[SELECTED_CONCEPT_NUMBER] if specified), produce a complete, production-ready HTML email template that meets all of the following requirements:

STRUCTURE REQUIREMENTS:
- Mobile-responsive using table-based layout compatible with all major ESPs (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot, Constant Contact)
- Modular sections clearly labeled with HTML comments: <!-- SECTION: HEADER -->, <!-- SECTION: HERO -->, <!-- SECTION: INTRO COPY -->, <!-- SECTION: SERVICE HIGHLIGHT -->, <!-- SECTION: SOCIAL PROOF -->, <!-- SECTION: CTA BLOCK -->, <!-- SECTION: FOOTER -->
- Each section must be independently removable or duplicatable without breaking the layout

BRAND VARIABLES (pull from [CLIENT_NAME] brand guidelines file and inject as clearly labeled inline comments at the top of the HTML):
/* BRAND VARIABLES — EDIT HERE */
--primary-color: [CLIENT_PRIMARY_HEX]; /* e.g. #FF6B00 */
--secondary-color: [CLIENT_SECONDARY_HEX];
--accent-color: [CLIENT_ACCENT_HEX];
--background-color: [CLIENT_BACKGROUND_HEX];
--text-color-primary: [CLIENT_TEXT_PRIMARY_HEX];
--text-color-secondary: [CLIENT_TEXT_SECONDARY_HEX];
--font-primary: [CLIENT_FONT_PRIMARY], Arial, sans-serif;
--font-secondary: [CLIENT_FONT_SECONDARY], Georgia, serif;
--logo-url: [CLIENT_LOGO_URL];
--logo-alt: [CLIENT_NAME] Logo;
--logo-width: [CLIENT_LOGO_WIDTH_PX]px;

CONTENT PLACEHOLDERS (clearly labeled for easy editing):
- [CLIENT_NAME] — company name
- [CLIENT_TAGLINE] — brand tagline
- [CLIENT_PHONE] — primary phone number
- [CLIENT_WEBSITE_URL] — website URL
- [CLIENT_ADDRESS] — physical or service area address
- [CLIENT_SERVICE_AREA] — city/region served
- [HERO_IMAGE_URL] — hero photo URL from approved client photo library
- [HERO_IMAGE_ALT] — descriptive alt text for hero image
- [EMAIL_HEADLINE] — primary H1 headline
- [EMAIL_SUBHEADLINE] — supporting H2
- [INTRO_PARAGRAPH] — 2-3 sentence personalized intro copy
- [SERVICE_1_NAME], [SERVICE_1_DESCRIPTION], [SERVICE_1_IMAGE_URL] — first service highlight
- [SERVICE_2_NAME], [SERVICE_2_DESCRIPTION], [SERVICE_2_IMAGE_URL] — second service highlight (duplicate block as needed)
- [PROMO_OFFER] — discount, free estimate, seasonal deal text
- [PROMO_EXPIRY] — offer expiration date
- [CTA_BUTTON_TEXT] — primary CTA
- [CTA_BUTTON_URL] — CTA destination link
- [TESTIMONIAL_QUOTE] — customer review text
- [TESTIMONIAL_AUTHOR] — reviewer name and city
- [REVIEW_STAR_RATING] — star rating (1-5)
- [SOCIAL_FACEBOOK_URL], [SOCIAL_INSTAGRAM_URL], [SOCIAL_GOOGLE_URL] — social links
- [UNSUBSCRIBE_URL] — required unsubscribe link
- [PRIVACY_POLICY_URL] — privacy policy link
- [PREHEADER_TEXT] — hidden preheader text node

DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS:
- Max width: 600px centered
- Header: logo left-aligned on [CLIENT_PRIMARY_HEX] background, with one-line nav text links in [CLIENT_SECONDARY_HEX]
- Hero block: full-width image at [HERO_IMAGE_URL] with overlaid headline [EMAIL_HEADLINE] in [CLIENT_FONT_PRIMARY] bold, white, with a semi-transparent dark scrim (rgba 0,0,0,0.45) ensuring accessibility contrast
- Service highlight cards: two-column on desktop, single-column stacked on mobile, with [CLIENT_ACCENT_HEX] top border accent, service icon or [SERVICE_IMAGE_URL], service name in [CLIENT_PRIMARY_HEX], description in [CLIENT_TEXT_PRIMARY_HEX]
- CTA button: pill-shaped (border-radius 30px), background [CLIENT_PRIMARY_HEX], text white, minimum 44px height for tap targets, centered, bold [CLIENT_FONT_PRIMARY]
- Social proof strip: light gray background [CLIENT_BACKGROUND_HEX], star icons in [CLIENT_ACCENT_HEX], italic testimonial text
- Footer: dark background ([CLIENT_SECONDARY_HEX] or #222222 fallback), white text, logo, contact info, social icons, legal unsubscribe line in 11px
- All images must include descriptive alt text populated from placeholders
- Inline CSS only (no external stylesheets) for maximum ESP compatibility
- Dark mode media query included for key sections

STYLE:
The template aesthetic must reflect [CLIENT_NAME]'s brand personality as described in the guidelines file: [CLIENT_BRAND_PERSONALITY_NOTES] (e.g., trustworthy and professional / bold and energetic / friendly and neighborhood-focused). Copy tone should be [CLIENT_VOICE_TONE] (e.g., warm and direct / authoritative and reassuring). All copy written in the template must be placeholder text that clearly signals what type of content goes there, written as real example copy a home services company would use, not Lorem Ipsum.

TASK (WORKFLOW EXECUTION STEPS):
Follow this exact sequence for every client processed through this workflow:

STEP 1 — FILE INTAKE: Acknowledge and parse the four client files for [CLIENT_NAME]. Extract and list: primary/secondary/accent hex colors, fonts, logo URL, brand voice descriptors, top 3 audience pain points from market research, top 5 services offered, any seasonal promotions active during [CAMPAIGN_PERIOD], and approved photo assets available.

STEP 2 — CONCEPT GENERATION: Produce the Phase 1 Campaign Concept Brief with [NUMBER_OF_CONCEPTS] concepts as specified above.

STEP 3 — CONCEPT SELECTION CONFIRMATION: Ask the user to confirm which concept to build into the full template, OR proceed with the top-ranked concept if [AUTO_SELECT] is set to TRUE.

STEP 4 — TEMPLATE PRODUCTION: Build the complete Phase 2 HTML email template for the selected concept, embedding all brand variables, content placeholders, and modular section comments as specified.

STEP 5 — EDITABILITY SUMMARY: After the HTML, output a formatted EDITABILITY GUIDE table listing every placeholder variable, what it represents, where it appears in the template, and an example value drawn from the client file.

STEP 6 — SCALING CHECKLIST: Output a CLIENT REPLICATION CHECKLIST — a numbered list of every element a team member must update when reusing this template base for a new client, ensuring zero brand bleed between clients.

INPUTS TO PROVIDE FOR EACH CLIENT RUN:
- [CLIENT_NAME]: Name of the home services company
- [CLIENT_FILE_BRAND_GUIDELINES]: Paste or attach brand guidelines content
- [CLIENT_FILE_MARKET_RESEARCH]: Paste or attach market research and audience data
- [CLIENT_FILE_PREFERENCES]: Paste or attach client preferences and service details
- [CLIENT_FILE_PHOTOS]: List of approved photo URLs or descriptions
- [CAMPAIGN_PERIOD]: e.g., Summer 2025, Q4 Heating Season, Spring Refresh
- [NUMBER_OF_CONCEPTS]: How many campaign concepts to generate (recommended: 3)
- [SELECTED_CONCEPT_NUMBER]: Which concept to build into HTML (or set [AUTO_SELECT]: TRUE)
- [TARGET_AUDIENCE_DESCRIPTION]: Brief override if different from market research file
- [CURRENT_SEASON_OR_CAMPAIGN_PERIOD]: Seasonal context for messaging angle

Begin by confirming receipt of all client files for [CLIENT_NAME], then execute Step 1 through Step 6 in sequence.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CLIENT_NAME], [NUMBER_OF_CONCEPTS], [CURRENT_SEASON_OR_CAMPAIGN_PERIOD], [TARGET_AUDIENCE_DESCRIPTION], [PHOTO_PLACEHOLDER], [SELECTED_CONCEPT_NUMBER], [CLIENT_PRIMARY_HEX], [CLIENT_SECONDARY_HEX] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
