The Objection Pre-Empter

Surfaces and defuses likely objections before the prospect ever raises them.

ClaudeEmail, Sales & Communication
46 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
List the 3 most likely objections a prospect would raise to buying [product/service] at [price point], and for each one, write a single sentence that addresses it proactively — the kind of line you'd want in a proposal so the objection never comes up out loud.
salesobjection handlingpersuasion

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
