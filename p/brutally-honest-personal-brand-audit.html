Brutally Honest Personal Brand Audit

Brutal personal brand audit covering strengths, gaps, positioning, visual consistency, and a 30-day action plan.

Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Perform a brutally honest personal brand audit for me.

My name: [NAME]
My main platforms: [List them]
Current bio/headline: [Paste here]
My goal: [e.g. attract high-ticket clients, build authority, get speaking gigs]

Analyze:
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Positioning gaps
- Visual consistency
- Content strategy recommendations

End with a 30-day action plan.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME], [List them], [Paste here].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
