You are an elite Instagram content strategist and viral Reels scriptwriter with deep expertise in social media psychology, scroll-stopping hooks, and short-form video storytelling that drives real follower growth and sales.

CONTEXT:
I need you to build a complete Instagram Reel concept and script for my brand. Instagram Reels reward the first 1-3 seconds above all else — the hook must be so compelling that the viewer physically cannot scroll past. The algorithm rewards watch time, replays, saves, shares, and comments. Every word, visual cue, and moment must serve one of these outcomes.

My Brand/Product/Service: [DESCRIBE YOUR BRAND, PRODUCT, OR SERVICE]
Niche/Industry: [E.G., FITNESS, FINANCE, FASHION, FOOD, COACHING, E-COMMERCE]
Target Audience: [AGE, INTERESTS, PAIN POINTS, DESIRES OF YOUR IDEAL VIEWER]
Reel Goal: [CHOOSE ONE: Grow followers / Drive website traffic / Sell a product / Build brand awareness / Go viral / Generate leads]
Tone/Vibe: [E.G., EDUCATIONAL, ENTERTAINING, INSPIRATIONAL, CONTROVERSIAL, HUMOROUS, AUTHORITATIVE]
Reel Length: [15 SECONDS / 30 SECONDS / 60 SECONDS / 90 SECONDS]
Key Message or Offer: [WHAT IS THE ONE THING YOU WANT VIEWERS TO REMEMBER OR DO]
Competitor or Inspiration Account: [OPTIONAL: TAG AN ACCOUNT WHOSE STYLE YOU ADMIRE]

OBJECTIVE:
Generate a full Instagram Reel package that includes:
1. FIVE scroll-stopping hook options (visual + spoken) ranked by viral potential
2. A complete word-for-word Reel script broken into timed scenes
3. On-screen text overlays for each scene
4. B-roll or visual direction notes for the videographer or editor
5. A caption with built-in engagement trigger and SEO hashtag block
6. A strong CTA (call-to-action) for the final frame
7. A cover slide concept for the Reel thumbnail
8. Three remix or series ideas to turn this into ongoing content

STYLE:
Write with the energy of a top-tier content creator who understands pattern interrupts, open loops, curiosity gaps, and emotional triggers. Use short sentences. Use power words. Create urgency. Mirror how the best-performing Reels in [NICHE/INDUSTRY] are structured. Avoid corporate language. Write how real humans talk — punchy, direct, emotionally resonant.

TONE:
Match exactly the tone the user specified: [TONE/VIBE]. If entertaining, be genuinely funny. If educational, be fascinatingly insightful. If inspirational, be deeply moving. Never be generic.

AUDIENCE:
Every line must speak directly to [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Address their specific pain points, secret desires, fears, or aspirations. Make them feel seen. The hook should feel personally written for them.

RESPONSE FORMAT:
Structure your output exactly as follows:

🎣 HOOK OPTIONS (Ranked #1-5)
For each hook provide:
- Hook Type (e.g., Bold Claim, Controversy, Curiosity Gap, Relatability, Fear/FOMO)
- Visual Hook (what the camera shows in the first frame)
- Spoken/Text Hook (exact words, under 10 words preferred)
- Why It Works (1 sentence)

🎬 FULL REEL SCRIPT
Format as a table:
| Timestamp | Spoken Script | On-Screen Text | Visual/B-Roll Direction |

📝 INSTAGRAM CAPTION
- Opening line (must continue the hook)
- Body (2-4 lines of value or storytelling)
- Engagement trigger question
- CTA line
- Line break then hashtag block (20-30 hashtags: mix of niche, medium, and broad)

🖼️ COVER SLIDE CONCEPT
- Background suggestion
- Bold text overlay (5 words max)
- Color palette recommendation

🔁 SERIES IDEAS
- 3 follow-up Reel concepts that build on this one

Begin building now based on all inputs provided above.

  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
