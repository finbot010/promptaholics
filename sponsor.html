What we accept

Our visitors trust this page because we're picky. Paying doesn't change that.

We say yes to

  • Real AI products for writing, images, video, audio, code or productivity
  • A named company or founder, an About page and a privacy policy
  • Clear pricing with a free tier or free trial
  • A tool our visitors can sign up for and use today

We say no to

  • Link swaps, backlink requests and SEO-only sites
  • Single-effect wrappers of features other tools already offer
  • Adult, gambling, crypto-signal or "get rich" tools
  • Tools that scrape accounts, ask for social logins or break platform rules
  • Anything misleading about what it does or what it costs

Questions

Will this give me a backlink for SEO?

No. Paid links are marked rel="sponsored", as Google requires. You're paying for visibility to people choosing an AI tool, not for search ranking.

Can I get listed for free?

Yes, if your tool earns a spot on merit. Use the Suggestion Box on Creation Station. We add free tools only when we'd recommend them ourselves, and we don't do link swaps.

How do I cancel?

Billing is monthly through Stripe. Cancel anytime from the link in your Stripe receipt, or email us. Your card stays up until the end of the period you paid for.

What if you remove my listing?

We only remove a listing if the tool breaks the rules above, for example by changing what it does or adding misleading pricing. If that happens, we refund the unused part of your month.

What do you need from me?

Your tool name, website and a one-line pitch. We use your site's logo, or you can send a square logo (at least 128×128) after approval.
