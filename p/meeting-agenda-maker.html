Meeting Agenda Maker

Ruthlessly efficient meeting agendas that drive decisions.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
50 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a structured agenda for a [DURATION] meeting about [TOPIC] with [ATTENDEES].

Include:
- Pre-read materials
- Measurable meeting objectives
- Timed blocks with owners
- Decision points (not discussion)
- Parking lot section
- Action items: owner + deadline

Goal: zero wasted minutes. Everyone leaves with a next step.
productivitymeetingagendateam

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DURATION], [TOPIC], [ATTENDEES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
