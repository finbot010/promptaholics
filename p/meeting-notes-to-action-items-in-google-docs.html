Meeting Notes to Action Items in Google Docs

Turns meeting notes in a Google Doc into decisions, owners, deadlines and a follow-up email.

GeminiAI Agents & Automation
80 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Drive Open my meeting notes in "[DOC NAME]" and turn them into:

1. Decisions made (one line each).
2. Action items in a table: task | owner | deadline | status.
3. Open questions and who should answer them.
4. Risks or blockers mentioned.
5. A follow-up email to attendees: [NAMES OR "everyone in the doc"], under 150 words, with the action items.
If an owner or deadline is unclear in the notes, mark it "unassigned" rather than guessing.
meetingsaction itemsgoogle docs

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DOC NAME], [NAMES OR "everyone in the doc"].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
