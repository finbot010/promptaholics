Travel Insurance Policy Explainer

Explains what a travel insurance policy actually covers and the gaps to watch for.

ClaudeTravel
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Explain this travel insurance policy in plain English:

[PASTE THE POLICY WORDING OR SUMMARY]

My trip: [DESTINATION, DATES, ACTIVITIES — e.g. scuba, skiing, rental car]
My concerns: [e.g. pre-existing condition, cancellation, lost bags, medical evacuation]

Tell me: what's covered and up to how much; the main exclusions; what counts as a valid reason to cancel; how claims work and what proof I'd need; and any gap for my specific trip. End with questions to ask the insurer before buying. Only use what's in the text I pasted.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE THE POLICY WORDING OR SUMMARY].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
