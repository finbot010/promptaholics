Twitter/X Thread Writer

Viral Twitter/X threads with proven hooks, one-insight-per-tweet structure, and strong CTAs.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a viral X/Twitter thread about [TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE].

Thread length: [10/15/20] tweets

Structure:
Tweet 1: The hook — bold claim, contrarian take, or surprising fact
Tweets 2-N: One insight per tweet. Short paragraphs. White space.
Second-to-last: Summarize the key lesson
Last tweet: CTA + follow prompt

Rules:
- Max 280 chars per tweet
- No hashtag spam (max 2)
- Each tweet works standalone
- Build tension throughout
- Use numbers and specifics

Tone: [EXPERT/PERSONAL STORY/DATA-DRIVEN/CONTRARIAN]
Your credibility: [WHY TRUST YOU ON THIS]

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC], [TARGET AUDIENCE], [10/15/20], [EXPERT/PERSONAL STORY/DATA-DRIVEN/CONTRARIAN], [WHY TRUST YOU ON THIS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
