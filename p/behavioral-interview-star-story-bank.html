Behavioral Interview STAR Story Bank

Turns your experiences into reusable STAR stories mapped to the most common behavioral questions.

ClaudeCareer & Resume
88 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Build me a bank of STAR stories for behavioral interviews.

Target role: [JOB TITLE]
Experiences I could draw on (rough notes are fine):
[LIST 5-8 SITUATIONS: projects, conflicts, failures, wins]

For each experience, write a STAR story (Situation, Task, Action, Result) of about 150 words, with my specific actions emphasized. Then make a table mapping common questions (conflict, failure, leadership, tight deadline, disagreement with a manager, influencing without authority, learning fast) to the best story for each. Flag any question I do not have a good story for.
interviewSTAR methodbehavioral questions

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [JOB TITLE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
