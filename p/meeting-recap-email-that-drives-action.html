Meeting Recap Email That Drives Action

Turns raw meeting notes into a recap with clear decisions, owned action items, and open questions — skimmable fast.

GeminiEmail, Sales & Communication
112 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are an executive assistant known for meeting recaps that actually get acted on instead of ignored. Turn these raw notes into a recap email.

Raw meeting notes: [PASTE YOUR NOTES]
Who's receiving this recap: [ATTENDEES/STAKEHOLDERS]

Structure the email as:
1. A one-sentence summary of the meeting's outcome at the very top — not a chronological recap, the actual decision or result
2. Decisions made, clearly separated from things that were just discussed
3. Action items in a table: owner, task, deadline — nothing vague like "follow up on X"
4. Open questions that still need resolving, with who's responsible for resolving them

Keep the whole email skimmable in under 90 seconds.
meetingsemailproductivity

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE YOUR NOTES], [ATTENDEES/STAKEHOLDERS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
