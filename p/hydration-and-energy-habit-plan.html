Hydration and Energy Habit Plan

Builds simple daily habits for hydration, meals and movement to avoid the afternoon energy crash.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
81 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me fix my afternoon energy crash with simple habits. No supplements, and tell me if symptoms are worth checking with a doctor.

Typical day (wake, meals, caffeine, water, movement, sleep): [DETAILS]
When I crash: [TIME]
Work type: [DESK / ACTIVE / SHIFTS]

Give me: the most likely habits causing the crash; a simple day plan with water, meals, light and movement; caffeine timing; a 5-minute reset for when the crash hits; and a 2-week tracking plan to see what helps.
hydrationenergydaily habits

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DETAILS], [TIME], [DESK / ACTIVE / SHIFTS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
