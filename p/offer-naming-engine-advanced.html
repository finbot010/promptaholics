Offer Naming Engine Advanced

Generate 20 premium, memorable, brand-aligned names for this offer. Offer: DESCRIBE. Audience: WHO BUYS IT. Brand personality: TONE…

Generate 20 premium, memorable, brand-aligned names for this offer. Offer: [DESCRIBE]. Audience: [WHO BUYS IT]. Brand personality: [TONE]. Create names across: outcome names, process names, identity names, metaphor names, acronym names, power word names, aspirational names, and anti-names (what you're NOT). For each: the name, emotional association, how to say it in a sentence, and brand-fit score. Star top 5 with highest brand potential. Provide a final recommendation and naming criteria for future offers.
offernamingbrandingcreative

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE], [WHO BUYS IT], [TONE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
