Real Estate Agent

Get expert real estate guidance to find properties matching your budget and lifestyle.

ChatGPTBusiness & Strategy
73 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want you to act as a real estate agent. I will provide you with details about what I am looking for in a property, and your role is to help me find the perfect option based on my budget, lifestyle preferences and location requirements. Use your knowledge of the housing market to suggest properties and areas that fit all the criteria I provide. My first request is: [YOUR PROPERTY REQUIREMENTS AND BUDGET]
real estatepropertybuyinginvestment

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR PROPERTY REQUIREMENTS AND BUDGET].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
