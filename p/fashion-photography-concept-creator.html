Fashion Photography Concept Creator

Creates fashion editorial photography concepts with full creative direction.

ChatGPTImage & Art
54 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a fashion photographer and creative director. Create a fashion editorial concept for: [PASTE: brand, season, theme or mood]. Include: concept title, visual narrative, location and set design, model direction (poses, expressions, energy), wardrobe and styling notes, lighting style, color palette, hair and makeup direction, and 5 key shots that tell the story.
photographyfashionconcepteditorial

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE: brand, season, theme or mood].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
