TikTok Script Formula

Second-by-second TikTok scripts with hooks, overlays, and captions optimized for the algorithm.

ChatGPTWriting & Content★ Featured
62 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a [15/30/60]-second TikTok script about [TOPIC] for [NICHE/AUDIENCE].

Format every second:
[0-3s] HOOK: Scroll-stopping first line
[3-8s] SETUP: Context in one sentence
[8-20s] VALUE: The tip, story, or reveal
[20-25s] PAYOFF: The satisfying conclusion
[25-30s] CTA: Follow, comment, or duet prompt

Include:
- On-screen text overlay suggestions
- Trending sound/music mood
- Caption (under 100 chars) with 3-5 hashtags

Style: [EDUCATIONAL/ENTERTAINING/STORYTELLING/POV]

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [15/30/60], [TOPIC], [NICHE/AUDIENCE], [0-3s], [3-8s], [8-20s], [20-25s], [25-30s] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
