House Hunting Scorecard

Builds a scorecard to rate and compare homes you visit, weighted by what matters to you.

GeminiReal Estate
75 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Make me a house hunting scorecard.

Must-haves: [e.g. 3 bedrooms, yard, under 30 min commute]
Nice-to-haves: [LIST]
Deal-breakers: [LIST]
Who's deciding: [ME / PARTNER / FAMILY]

Build a weighted scoring sheet (Google Sheets layout with formulas) covering location, layout, condition, light, noise, storage, outdoor space, costs and gut feeling. Add a printable checklist of things to inspect at a viewing (water pressure, signs of damp, outlets, roof, windows) and questions to ask the seller's agent.
house huntinghome buyingcomparison

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST], [ME / PARTNER / FAMILY].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
