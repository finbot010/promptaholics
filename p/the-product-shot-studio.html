The Product Shot Studio

High-end product photography with 3-point studio lighting, premium styling, and e-commerce quality.

MidjourneyImage & Art
97 words · free to use 
Create a premium product photograph of [PRODUCT]. Studio lighting setup: 3-point lighting with a soft key light, rim light for edge definition, and a subtle fill. Surface: [marble / dark wood / gradient backdrop / lifestyle setting]. Style: [Apple-level minimalism / luxury editorial / lifestyle action shot / flat lay]. Add subtle environmental elements that tell a brand story: [props related to product use]. Shallow depth of field, f/1.8 bokeh effect. Color palette: [specify 2-3 colors]. The image should look like it belongs in a high-end magazine ad or a premium e-commerce listing. 4K, ultra-sharp product detail.
product photographycommercialadvertisinge-commercebranding

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT].
  3. Paste it into Midjourney and generate. Run it a few times — image tools give a different result each time.
  4. Adjust the style, lighting or aspect ratio words to steer the result.
