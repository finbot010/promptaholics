Clay-Crafted City World

Generates a whimsical, hand-sculpted clay-style miniature city scene.

ChatGPTImage & Art
59 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Generate a whimsical miniature world featuring [landmark] crafted entirely from colorful modeling clay. Every element should appear hand-sculpted with visible fingerprints and organic clay textures. Use a playful, childlike style with vibrant colors. The handmade quality should be evident in every surface. Add the city name at the top in a bold, friendly rounded font matching the clay aesthetic.
