Audience Objection Crusher

List and crush the top objections for this offer. Offer: [DESCRIBE YOUR OFFER]. For each objection provide: 1. The objection stated as the prospect would actually say it 2. The root fear or belief behind the objection 3. A reframe that shifts their perspective 4. A direct response (what to say or write) 5. A social proof example or story that addresses it 6. A preemptive version — how to address it before they raise it. Cover at minimum: price, time, trust, skepticism, bad past experience, and 'I need to think about it.' End with a 10-question FAQ that handles all objections in one section.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE YOUR OFFER].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
