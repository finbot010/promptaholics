Interior Room Makeover Visualizer

Reimagines a photo of your room in a new style so you can see a redesign before buying anything.

GeminiImage & Art
83 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Redesign the room in my photo. [UPLOAD PHOTO OF ROOM]

Room type: [LIVING ROOM / BEDROOM / KITCHEN / OFFICE]
Style I want: [e.g. Japandi, mid-century modern, coastal, industrial, cozy minimal]
Keep: [e.g. the window layout, flooring, fireplace, sofa]
Budget feel: [BUDGET / MID-RANGE / HIGH-END]
Colors: [PREFERENCES]

Keep the room's real proportions, walls and windows. Show 2 versions of the makeover. Then list the main items in each design (furniture, lighting, rug, decor, paint color) so I can shop for similar pieces.
interior designroom makeoverhome decor

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [UPLOAD PHOTO OF ROOM], [LIVING ROOM / BEDROOM / KITCHEN / OFFICE], [BUDGET / MID-RANGE / HIGH-END], [PREFERENCES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
