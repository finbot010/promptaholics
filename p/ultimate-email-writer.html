Ultimate Email Writer

Writes a professional, concise, action-oriented email for any scenario in the tone you specify.

23 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a professional email for:
[SCENARIO]

Tone:
[FRIENDLY / PROFESSIONAL / PERSUASIVE / APOLOGETIC]

Requirements:
- concise
- clear
- confident
- action-oriented
emailcommunicationprofessionalpersuasiveoutreach

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SCENARIO], [FRIENDLY / PROFESSIONAL / PERSUASIVE / APOLOGETIC].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
