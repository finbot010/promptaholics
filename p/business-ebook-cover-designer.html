CONTEXT: You are a senior graphic designer at a top-tier branding agency specializing in high-converting digital product covers for business and strategy niches. Your work is regularly featured in design showcases and consistently drives strong click-through rates on platforms like Amazon KDP, Gumroad, and LinkedIn. You understand the psychology of color blocking, visual hierarchy, and thumbnail optimization.

OBJECTIVE: Design a flat, modern, high-converting ebook cover layout for a business strategy guide titled '[EBOOK TITLE]' by '[AUTHOR NAME]'. The design must be visually arresting at full size AND at a 150px thumbnail, using bold color blocking and geometric precision to communicate authority, clarity, and forward momentum.

STYLE: Flat design. Zero gradients, zero shadows, zero textures, zero photographic elements. All shapes are crisp, geometric, and intentional. The aesthetic references brands like McKinsey, Stripe, and Linear — clean, confident, and corporate-forward with one bold visual punch.

TASK: Produce a detailed design specification and layout description for the ebook cover using the following mandatory parameters:

1. CANVAS: Standard ebook cover ratio 6×9 inches at 300dpi (1800×2700px). Portrait orientation.

2. BACKGROUND STRUCTURE – DIAGONAL SPLIT:
   - A single sharp, razor-crisp diagonal line divides the canvas from the upper-left area to the lower-right area at approximately a 35-degree angle.
   - TOP-LEFT HALF: Filled with solid corporate navy blue. Exact hex: #0A1F44. No variation, no noise, perfectly flat.
   - BOTTOM-RIGHT HALF: Filled with pure white. Exact hex: #FFFFFF. Perfectly flat.
   - The diagonal edge must be pixel-perfect with zero anti-aliasing blur — hard geometric edge only.

3. GEOMETRIC ARROW GRAPHIC:
   - A large abstract arrow composed of 2–3 bold geometric shapes (e.g., a wide parallelogram body + a sharp right-triangle arrowhead, OR two overlapping angular polygons forming a rightward/upward-pointing arrow).
   - Color: Vibrant orange. Exact hex: #FF6B00.
   - The arrow OVERLAYS the diagonal split — it must cross both the navy and white halves simultaneously, functioning as the visual anchor that bridges both zones.
   - Scale: The arrow should occupy approximately 40–55% of the total canvas width, centered slightly left-of-center horizontally, and vertically centered across the diagonal split line.
   - Style: Completely flat. No drop shadow. No bevel. Pure solid orange geometry.
   - Direction: Arrow points toward the upper-right, implying upward growth and strategic direction.

4. TYPOGRAPHY LAYOUT:
   - EBOOK TITLE '[EBOOK TITLE]': Placed in the navy blue zone (upper half). Font: Bold sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat ExtraBold or Inter Black). Size: Large — approximately 10–13% of canvas height per line. Color: Pure white (#FFFFFF). Left-aligned or centered. Maximum 2 lines. Letter-spacing: slightly tight (-20 to -40 tracking). All caps preferred.
   - SUBTITLE '[SUBTITLE]' (if applicable): Directly below the title in the navy zone. Same font family, Regular or Medium weight. Size: 40–50% of title font size. Color: Orange (#FF6B00) or white. Single line preferred.
   - AUTHOR NAME '[AUTHOR NAME]': Placed in the white zone (lower half). Font: Same sans-serif family, Medium weight. Size: Small-to-medium — approximately 4–5% of canvas height. Color: Navy blue (#0A1F44). Left-aligned or centered, mirroring title alignment.
   - Optional: A thin 3–4px horizontal rule line in orange (#FF6B00) separating the author name from a one-line descriptor tag such as '[TAGLINE OR CREDENTIAL]'.

5. COLOR PALETTE — STRICT RULES:
   - Navy Blue: #0A1F44 — backgrounds, text on white zones
   - Pure White: #FFFFFF — backgrounds, text on navy zones
   - Vibrant Orange: #FF6B00 — arrow graphic, accent lines, subtitle highlights
   - NO additional colors permitted. No grays, no off-whites, no tints or shades of these colors.

6. THUMBNAIL LEGIBILITY TEST:
   - At 150×225px (thumbnail size), the diagonal split must remain clearly visible, the orange arrow must be immediately recognizable as a directional shape, and the title text must remain legible (minimum 2 words visible).
   - Ensure title font size and weight are heavy enough to survive aggressive downscaling.

7. NEGATIVE SPACE RULES:
   - Maintain generous padding on all four edges: minimum 5% of canvas width on left/right, minimum 4% on top/bottom.
   - Do not crowd the diagonal edge with text — keep a clear buffer of at least 80px around the split line on both sides.

OUTPUT DELIVERABLE: Provide (A) a precise written layout specification describing exact element positions as percentages of canvas dimensions, (B) a layer-by-layer construction order for a designer using Figma, Adobe Illustrator, or Canva Pro, and (C) a list of 3 micro-refinements a senior designer would make to push this from good to exceptional (e.g., optical alignment tweaks, arrow anchor adjustments, text weight swap suggestions).

RESOURCES REFERENCED: [EBOOK TITLE], [SUBTITLE], [AUTHOR NAME], [TAGLINE OR CREDENTIAL], target platform [TARGET PLATFORM e.g. Amazon KDP / Gumroad / LinkedIn], and any [BRAND FONT] if the author has an existing font preference.
