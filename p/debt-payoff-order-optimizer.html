Debt Payoff Order Optimizer

Runs the actual interest-savings math to recommend a debt payoff order, not a one-size-fits-all method.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
133 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a financial planner who runs the actual numbers instead of defaulting to "just do the debt snowball." Help me figure out the smartest payoff order.

My debts (balance, interest rate, minimum payment): [LIST EACH ONE]
Extra amount I can put toward debt each month beyond minimums: [AMOUNT]
Whether I'm more motivated by quick wins or by saving the most money: [PREFERENCE]

Give me:
1. The mathematically optimal payoff order (highest interest first) and roughly how much interest it saves vs. paying in a different order
2. The "quick win" order (smallest balance first) and how much longer/more expensive that approach is
3. A recommendation for MY specific situation given my stated motivation style — don't just default to one method
4. A rough payoff timeline for whichever order I go with
debt payoffpersonal financebudgeting

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST EACH ONE], [AMOUNT], [PREFERENCE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
