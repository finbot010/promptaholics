Content Calendar Builder

Complete 30-day content calendar with daily platform, format, topic, goal, hook, and hashtags across content pillars — plus 4 hero pieces.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding★ Featured
81 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Build a detailed 30-day content calendar for [BRAND / NICHE] across [PLATFORMS — Instagram / LinkedIn / TikTok / Twitter]. For each day provide: the platform, content format (reel / carousel / static / story / thread), a specific topic and angle, the primary goal (awareness / engagement / conversion / retention), a sample hook or opening line, and relevant hashtags. Vary content pillars across the month: educational, entertaining, inspiring, promotional, and community-driven. Include 4 hero content pieces for maximum reach.
content calendarsocial mediaplanningstrategy30-day

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BRAND / NICHE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
