Returning to Work After Parental Leave

Plans a smooth return to work after parental leave, from the first conversations to the first month back.

GeminiCareer & Resume
90 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm going back to work after [LENGTH] of parental leave on [DATE].

Role: [JOB]
Childcare plan: [DETAILS]
What I want to ask for: [e.g. phased return, flexible hours, remote days, lactation breaks]
Worries: [e.g. catching up, being overlooked, guilt, logistics]

Give me: a message to my manager 2-3 weeks before returning to agree a plan; a catch-up checklist (projects, changes, people to meet); a realistic schedule for the first 2 weeks; a script for asking for flexibility; and ways to handle mornings and sick days. Keep it practical and reassuring.
parental leavereturn to workwork-life

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LENGTH], [DATE], [JOB], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
