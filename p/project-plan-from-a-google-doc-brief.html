Project Plan From a Google Doc Brief

Turns a project brief in Google Drive into milestones, tasks, timeline and risks.

GeminiAI Agents & Automation
69 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Drive Read the project brief "[DOC NAME]" and build a project plan.

Team: [PEOPLE AND ROLES]
Deadline: [DATE]
Hours per week available: [HOURS]

Give me: milestones with dates working back from the deadline; tasks under each milestone with owner and estimate; dependencies; a simple timeline I can paste into Google Sheets; the 5 biggest risks with how to reduce them; and what to cut if we fall behind.
project planninggoogle docstimeline

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DOC NAME], [PEOPLE AND ROLES], [DATE], [HOURS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
