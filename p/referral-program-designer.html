Referral Program Designer

Complete referral program with reward structure, launch email, mechanics, and implementation tools.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
82 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Design a referral program for my business.

Business: [DESCRIBE]
Current customers: [APPROXIMATE NUMBER]
Product price: $[PRICE]
Customer LTV: $[LIFETIME VALUE]
Tech available: [SHOPIFY/STRIPE/CUSTOM]

Design:
1. Reward structure (what referrers get, what new customers get)
2. How to calculate sustainable reward amounts
3. Referral mechanics (link/code/email)
4. Launch email to existing customers (write it)
5. How to promote without being annoying
6. The referral page copy
7. Tools to implement (free and paid)
8. What the best referral programs in my industry do
referralword of mouthgrowthviralmarketing

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE], [APPROXIMATE NUMBER], [PRICE], [LIFETIME VALUE], [SHOPIFY/STRIPE/CUSTOM].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
