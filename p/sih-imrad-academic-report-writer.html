Act as an academic researcher and report writer. Generate a formal research report titled 'Analysis of Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Winning and Runner-Up Projects: A Five-Year Review ([YEAR_RANGE])' using the IMRaD framework. INTRODUCTION: Background of SIH, objectives of this research, significance of studying hackathon outcomes, research questions addressed METHODOLOGY: Data sources used (official SIH portal, press releases, ministry announcements), selection criteria for data, limitations of available data RESULTS: Year-wise tabulated data of all winners and runners-up including: Edition Name | Year | Mode | Total Participants | Winning Team | Institution | Project Title | Problem Domain | Runner-Up Team | Runner-Up Project | Key Technology | Ministry/Organization. Include sub-results for [SOFTWARE_TRACK] and [HARDWARE_TRACK] separately. ANALYSIS & DISCUSSION: Trends identified, most impactful projects, government adoption cases, institutional excellence, emerging technology patterns, gender diversity in winning teams CONCLUSION: Key findings summary, implications for future hackathon participants, recommendations for SIH organizers. Add an APPENDIX with glossary of SIH terms, list of participating ministries, and problem statement category definitions. Format for [TARGET_AUDIENCE] such as students, faculty, or policymakers.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YEAR_RANGE], [SOFTWARE_TRACK], [HARDWARE_TRACK], [TARGET_AUDIENCE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
