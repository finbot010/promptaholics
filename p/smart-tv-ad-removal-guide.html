Smart TV Ad Removal Guide

Role: You are a senior smart TV network engineer and cybersecurity expert with 1

Role: You are a senior smart TV network engineer and cybersecurity expert with 10+ years of experience in consumer electronics, DNS-level ad blocking, firmware customization, and home network configuration.

Instructions: Provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide to completely remove or block ALL advertisements from a [SMART_TV_BRAND_AND_MODEL] running [TV_OPERATING_SYSTEM e.g. Tizen, WebOS, Android TV, Roku OS, Fire TV OS]. Cover every possible layer of ad removal including but not limited to:
1. Built-in TV settings — disable ACR (Automatic Content Recognition), targeted ads, interest-based ads, and any native ad banners in the home screen UI.
2. DNS-level blocking — how to configure the TV to use Pi-hole, NextDNS, AdGuard Home, or a public ad-blocking DNS like AdGuard DNS or Quad9 directly from the TV network settings.
3. Router-level blocking — how to assign the TV a static IP and apply network-wide DNS ad blocking so ads are blocked even if the TV resets its DNS.
4. VPN or proxy methods — if applicable, how to route TV traffic through an ad-blocking VPN.
5. Hosts file or ADB methods — for Android TV or Fire TV, provide ADB sideloading commands to install ad blockers like AdGuard or Blokada.
6. Third-party launcher replacement — if available for the TV model, recommend launchers that remove ad-laden home screens.
7. Physical or VLAN network isolation — advanced method to sandbox the TV and block telemetry and ad servers at the firewall level.

Specifics: The user has [TECHNICAL_SKILL_LEVEL e.g. beginner/intermediate/advanced] technical knowledge. They have access to [AVAILABLE_TOOLS e.g. a Raspberry Pi, their router admin panel, a Windows/Mac/Linux computer]. Their network router is [ROUTER_BRAND_AND_MODEL]. They use the TV primarily for [PRIMARY_USE_CASE e.g. streaming Netflix/YouTube/cable, gaming]. They do NOT want to void their warranty if possible, but are open to [RISK_TOLERANCE e.g. low-risk settings changes only / moderate ADB methods / full advanced modifications].

Expected Output: Return a clearly numbered, tiered action plan starting from the easiest and safest methods to the most advanced. For each method provide: exact setting names and menu paths, specific commands or DNS addresses to enter, any risks or side effects, and estimated effectiveness percentage for ad removal. End with a recommended combination strategy for maximum ad removal based on the user's profile. Include a list of known ad-serving domains specific to [SMART_TV_BRAND_AND_MODEL] that should be blacklisted.

Notes: Prioritize methods that survive TV firmware updates and factory resets. Flag any method that may break streaming DRM or cause app malfunctions. Provide fallback options if a primary method fails.
