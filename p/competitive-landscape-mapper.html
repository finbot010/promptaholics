Competitive Landscape Mapper

Maps the competitive landscape and finds your actual defensible angle.

GeminiMarketing & Branding
134 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Using current, real information about the market, help me map the competitive landscape for [PRODUCT/COMPANY/CATEGORY].

1. **Direct Competitors**: Companies solving the same problem the same way
2. **Indirect Competitors**: Companies solving the same problem differently, or solving an adjacent problem that could expand into this space
3. **Positioning Map**: For each competitor, their core positioning claim and who they're actually targeting
4. **Gaps**: Underserved segments or unaddressed pain points across the current landscape
5. **Our Realistic Angle**: Given the gaps you found, what's a defensible position — not "be the best at everything" but a specific wedge

Be honest if a market looks saturated or a proposed angle looks weak — don't manufacture a gap that isn't really there.

What we're building: [DESCRIBE YOUR PRODUCT/COMPANY]
Who we think our customer is: [DESCRIBE TARGET CUSTOMER]

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT/COMPANY/CATEGORY], [DESCRIBE YOUR PRODUCT/COMPANY], [DESCRIBE TARGET CUSTOMER].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
