Property Investment Analyzer

Analyzes real estate investment opportunities with key metrics.

ChatGPTReal Estate
42 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a real estate investment analyst. Analyze this property opportunity: [PASTE DETAILS: price, rent, expenses, location]. Calculate: gross yield, net yield, cash-on-cash return, cap rate, break-even occupancy, 5-year projection. Flag risks and opportunities. Recommend: buy, negotiate, or pass — with reasoning.
real estateinvestmentanalysisROI

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE DETAILS: price, rent, expenses, location].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
