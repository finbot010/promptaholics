Video Script From a YouTube Reference

Studies the structure of a video you like and writes an original script for your topic using that structure.

GeminiVideo & Film
77 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@YouTube Watch this video for its structure only: [LINK]

My video topic: [TOPIC]
Length: [MINUTES]
My style: [e.g. talking head, voiceover with b-roll, tutorial]

1. Break down the reference video's structure: hook, sections, pacing, how it keeps attention, and call to action, with timestamps.
2. Write an original script for my topic using a similar structure (not the same words or examples), with on-screen text and b-roll notes.
3. Give 5 title options and 3 thumbnail concepts.
youtubescriptstructure

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LINK], [TOPIC], [MINUTES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
