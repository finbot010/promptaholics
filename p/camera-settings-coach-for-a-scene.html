Camera Settings Coach for a Scene

Explains the right camera settings for a specific scene and why, so you learn while you shoot.

GeminiImage & Art
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Tell me what camera settings to use.

Camera and lens: [MODEL, LENS]
Scene: [e.g. kids playing indoors, fireworks, waterfall, concert, night street]
Look I want: [SHARP / MOTION BLUR / BLURRY BACKGROUND / BRIGHT AND AIRY]
Gear available: [TRIPOD / FLASH / NONE]

Give me starting settings (mode, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, focus mode, white balance), why each one, what to change if the photo is too dark, blurry or noisy, and a simple practice exercise. Look up any model-specific feature names I should use.
camera settingsexposurelearning photography

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MODEL, LENS], [TRIPOD / FLASH / NONE].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
