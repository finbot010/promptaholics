Cold Email Sequence Writer

Writes complete 5-email cold outreach sequences with subject lines.

ChatGPTEmail, Sales & Communication
71 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a cold email copywriter and sales strategist. Write a 5-email cold outreach sequence for: [PASTE: product/service, target prospect, pain point]. Email 1: Pattern interrupt hook + value prop (under 100 words). Email 2: Case study or social proof (3 days later). Email 3: Different angle/objection addressed (5 days later). Email 4: Scarcity or insight (7 days later). Email 5: Graceful breakup (10 days later). Include subject lines for each.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
