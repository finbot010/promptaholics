Educational YouTube Playlist Curator

Finds and orders the best free YouTube videos into a course for any subject.

GeminiEducation & Learning
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@YouTube Build me a free video course on [SUBJECT].

Learner level: [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE]
Age: [ADULT / TEEN / CHILD]
Time available: [HOURS PER WEEK] for [WEEKS]

Find the best videos from reputable educators and arrange them into a logical order by week. For each video: title, channel, length, and what it teaches. Add a short practice task after each week and a final project. Avoid videos that are outdated or mainly selling something.
youtubecurriculumself-study

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SUBJECT], [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE], [ADULT / TEEN / CHILD], [HOURS PER WEEK], [WEEKS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
