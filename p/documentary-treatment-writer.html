Documentary Treatment Writer

Writes professional documentary treatments for pitching.

You are a documentary filmmaker. Write a compelling treatment for a documentary about: [PASTE SUBJECT]. Include: logline (1 sentence), synopsis (3 paragraphs), main characters/subjects, narrative arc, key scenes, visual approach, tone, target audience, and why this story matters now.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE SUBJECT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
