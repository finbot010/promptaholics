Cold Email to a Local Business

Researches a local business and writes a personalized cold email offering your service.

GeminiEmail, Sales & Communication
83 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Research [BUSINESS NAME] in [CITY] (website, Google reviews, social media) and write a cold email offering my service.

My service: [WHAT YOU OFFER]
Proof: [RESULTS FOR SIMILAR BUSINESSES]

Find one specific, genuine reason my service could help them (for example from their reviews or website). Write an email under 120 words with a specific subject line, the reason, one proof point, and a low-pressure ask. Then a follow-up for 5 days later. Do not invent facts about the business; quote what you found.
cold emaillocal businessoutreach

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BUSINESS NAME], [CITY], [WHAT YOU OFFER], [RESULTS FOR SIMILAR BUSINESSES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
