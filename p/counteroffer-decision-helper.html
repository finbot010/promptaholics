Counteroffer Decision Helper

Helps you decide whether to accept a counteroffer from your current employer or take the new job.

ClaudeCareer & Resume
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
My current employer made a counteroffer after I resigned. Help me think it through.

Current job: [ROLE, PAY, WHAT I LIKE, WHAT I DON'T]
Why I started looking: [HONEST REASONS]
New offer: [ROLE, PAY, BENEFITS, WHAT EXCITES ME]
Counteroffer: [WHAT THEY'RE OFFERING NOW]

Ask me 5 questions that get to the real issue. Then compare the options in a table (pay, growth, the reasons I left, risk), explain the common risks of accepting a counteroffer, and help me write a gracious reply for whichever choice I make.
counterofferjob offerdecision

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ROLE, PAY, WHAT I LIKE, WHAT I DON'T], [HONEST REASONS], [ROLE, PAY, BENEFITS, WHAT EXCITES ME], [WHAT THEY'RE OFFERING NOW].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
