Real Estate Lead Magnet Ideas

Generates lead magnets and content ideas that attract buyers and sellers in your area.

ClaudeReal Estate
78 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm a real estate agent in [CITY / AREA] focusing on [BUYERS / SELLERS / INVESTORS / FIRST-TIME BUYERS / LUXURY].

Give me 8 lead magnet ideas (guides, checklists, calculators, reports) that my ideal client would trade an email for. For each: the title, what's inside, the best channel to promote it, and the follow-up email that should come after the download. Then give me a 4-week content calendar for social media built around the top two ideas.
lead generationrealtor marketingcontent

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY / AREA].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
