Claude Synthesis Engine

I am going to give you [NUMBER] sources on [TOPIC]: [PASTE].

ClaudeWriting & Content
46 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I am going to give you [NUMBER] sources on [TOPIC]: [PASTE]. Synthesize them into a single coherent view. Identify where they agree, where they conflict, and which conflicts are empirical (facts) versus interpretive (values or framing). Produce the synthesis a genuinely expert reader would find credible.
claudesynthesisresearch

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER], [TOPIC], [PASTE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
