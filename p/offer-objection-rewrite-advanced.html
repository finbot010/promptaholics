Rewrite these objections and responses to be more persuasive and emotionally aligned. Objections: [LIST]. For each: rewrite the acknowledgment to be more empathetic, reframe it as evidence of the right buyer not the wrong one, rewrite the response with a story that dissolves the objection emotionally, and write the preemptive version for a sales page. Make each feel like a conversation with the most trusted advisor the prospect has ever had.
