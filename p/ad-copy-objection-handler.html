Ad Copy Objection Handler

Builds ad copy that names and directly addresses the real purchase objection, not generic feature copy.

ClaudeMarketing & Branding
126 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a direct-response copywriter who builds ad copy around handling the real objection standing between someone and a purchase, not generic feature-benefit copy. Write ad copy for:

Product/service: [WHAT YOU'RE ADVERTISING]
Target audience: [WHO YOU'RE TARGETING]
The #1 objection stopping people from buying: [THE REAL HESITATION — PRICE, TRUST, "WILL THIS WORK FOR ME," ETC.]

Write:
1. A headline that names the objection directly rather than avoiding it
2. Body copy that addresses the objection head-on with a specific reason it doesn't apply (proof, guarantee, specific mechanism) — not just reassurance
3. A call-to-action that reduces the perceived risk of taking the next step
4. One alternate headline angle that approaches the same objection from a completely different direction, in case the first doesn't resonate
advertisingcopywritingconversion

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT YOU'RE ADVERTISING], [WHO YOU'RE TARGETING].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
