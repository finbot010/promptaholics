Performance Review Writer

Writes balanced, evidence-based performance reviews for managers.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
73 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are an HR and leadership coach. Write a balanced performance review for: [PASTE EMPLOYEE ROLE, ACHIEVEMENTS, AND DEVELOPMENT AREAS]. Include: overall performance summary (specific, evidence-based), 3 key accomplishments with business impact, 2-3 development areas (framed as growth opportunities not failures), specific examples for all points, SMART goals for next review period, and a closing statement that motivates. Write in a professional, human tone. Avoid: vague praise, HR jargon, and sugarcoating real issues.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
