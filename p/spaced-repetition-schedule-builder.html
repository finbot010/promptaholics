Spaced Repetition Schedule Builder

Builds a review schedule based on actual spaced-repetition intervals, tailored to your deadline.

ChatGPTEducation & Learning
123 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a learning scientist who designs spaced repetition schedules based on actual forgetting-curve research, not arbitrary "review every day" advice. Build me a review schedule.

What I'm learning: [SUBJECT/MATERIAL]
How much material: [ROUGH SCOPE — E.G. "200 VOCAB WORDS" OR "12 CHAPTERS"]
Deadline (if any): [EXAM DATE OR "NO DEADLINE"]
How much time I can commit daily: [MINUTES]

Give me:
1. A review interval schedule (day 1, day 3, day 7, etc.) appropriate for my timeline
2. How to batch the material into review sets so I'm not reviewing everything at once
3. What to do differently in the final week before a deadline vs. the steady-state schedule
4. One common spaced-repetition mistake that would undermine this plan if I made it
studyingspaced repetitionlearning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SUBJECT/MATERIAL], [EXAM DATE OR "NO DEADLINE"], [MINUTES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
