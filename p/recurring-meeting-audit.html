Recurring Meeting Audit

Audits your recurring meetings in Google Calendar and suggests which to cut, shorten or turn into async updates.

GeminiAI Agents & Automation
64 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Calendar List all my recurring meetings.

For each: title, how often, length, number of attendees, and total hours per month it costs me. Then suggest which to cancel, shorten, make less frequent, or replace with an async update (written update or shared doc), based on these priorities: [YOUR PRIORITIES]. Draft a short, polite message to the organizer for each change I might propose.
meetingscalendar audittime management

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR PRIORITIES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
