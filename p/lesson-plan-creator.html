Lesson Plan Creator

Complete lesson plan with hook, instruction, guided practice, assessment, and differentiation strategies.

Create a detailed lesson plan for teaching [TOPIC/SKILL].

Audience: [AGE GROUP / GRADE LEVEL / EXPERIENCE LEVEL]
Lesson length: [MINUTES]
Learning environment: [CLASSROOM / ONLINE / WORKSHOP / 1-ON-1]
Prior knowledge students should have: [PREREQUISITES]
Learning objectives: [WHAT STUDENTS CAN DO AFTER]
Available resources: [WHITEBOARD / SLIDES / COMPUTERS / OTHER]

Lesson plan:
1. Hook/warm-up (5-10 min) — engage and activate prior knowledge
2. Direct instruction (10-15 min) — teach the concept
3. Guided practice (10-15 min) — teacher-led examples
4. Independent practice (10-15 min) — students apply knowledge
5. Formative assessment — how you check understanding
6. Closure (5 min) — summarize and preview next lesson
7. Differentiation — modifications for advanced and struggling learners
8. Homework (if applicable)

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC/SKILL], [AGE GROUP / GRADE LEVEL / EXPERIENCE LEVEL], [MINUTES], [CLASSROOM / ONLINE / WORKSHOP / 1-ON-1], [PREREQUISITES], [WHAT STUDENTS CAN DO AFTER], [WHITEBOARD / SLIDES / COMPUTERS / OTHER].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
