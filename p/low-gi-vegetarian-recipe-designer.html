**Context:** You are assisting a registered dietitian who specializes in evidence-based nutrition planning. The dietitian needs a complete, practical vegetarian recipe that meets specific clinical nutritional targets for a client meal plan.

**Objective:** Design ONE detailed vegetarian recipe serving exactly 2 people, with approximately 500 calories per serving (±50 calories acceptable) and a low glycemic index (GI score below 55 or low GL overall). The recipe must be nutritionally balanced, satisfying, and easy to prepare at home.

**Style:** Professional yet approachable. Use clear culinary language suitable for both dietitians and home cooks. Be precise with measurements.

**Tone:** Informative, confident, and practical.

**Audience:** A registered dietitian reviewing and potentially prescribing this recipe to clients managing blood sugar, weight, or metabolic health.

**Response Format:** Please structure your response with the following clearly labeled sections:
1. **Recipe Name** – Creative and descriptive
2. **Why It Fits** – Brief clinical rationale explaining how it meets the low-GI and calorie targets
3. **Ingredients List** – Exact quantities for 2 servings, including the estimated GI category (Low/Medium/High) for key ingredients
4. **Step-by-Step Instructions** – Clear, numbered cooking steps with timing
5. **Nutritional Breakdown (per serving):** Calories, Protein (g), Total Carbohydrates (g), Dietary Fiber (g), Net Carbs (g), Total Fat (g), Saturated Fat (g), Glycemic Index estimate, Glycemic Load estimate
6. **Dietitian Notes** – Any substitution options, allergen flags, or tips to further lower GI if needed
7. **Meal Pairing Suggestions** – Optional low-GI complements (beverages, sides) to complete the meal
