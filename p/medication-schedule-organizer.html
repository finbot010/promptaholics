Medication Schedule Organizer

Turns a list of prescriptions into a clear daily schedule and a checklist of questions for the pharmacist.

ClaudeHealth & Wellness
103 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Organize this medication list into a daily schedule. Use only the instructions on the labels or from my doctor; do not change doses.

Medications (name, dose, how often, with or without food, special instructions from the label): [LIST]
Daily routine (wake, meals, bed): [TIMES]
Who manages it: [ME / CAREGIVER FOR SOMEONE]

Create: a time-by-time daily schedule table; a weekly checklist I can print; reminders for any medication with special instructions; and a list of questions to confirm with the pharmacist (interactions, timing conflicts, what to do if a dose is missed). Do not give medical advice beyond what is on the labels.
medicationsschedulecaregiver

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST], [TIMES], [ME / CAREGIVER FOR SOMEONE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
