Cooking Technique Video Coach

Finds the best YouTube videos for a cooking technique and summarizes the key steps and mistakes.

GeminiFood & Cooking
73 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@YouTube Find the 3 best videos teaching how to [TECHNIQUE, e.g. sear a steak, make fresh pasta, temper chocolate].

My level: [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE]

For each video: link, length and why it is good. Then combine them into one clear set of steps, the 5 most common mistakes and how to avoid them, how to tell when it is done right (look, sound, temperature), and a simple practice recipe for my first try.
youtubecooking skillstechnique

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
