Grant Proposal Writer

Complete grant proposals with all required sections aligned to grantor priorities and language.

ChatGPTMore Prompts
79 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me write a grant proposal for [ORGANIZATION/PROJECT].

Organization: [NAME AND BRIEF DESCRIPTION]
Project name: [TITLE]
Funding needed: $[AMOUNT]
Grantor: [WHO YOU ARE APPLYING TO]
Grantor priorities: [WHAT THEY FUND]
Target beneficiaries: [WHO IS HELPED]
Timeline: [PROJECT DURATION]

Write:
1. Executive summary (250 words)
2. Statement of need (with data)
3. Project description (goals, activities, timeline)
4. Evaluation plan (how you'll measure success)
5. Organizational capacity
6. Budget narrative
7. Sustainability plan

Tone: Professional, evidence-based, aligned with grantor language.
grantnonprofitfundingproposalwriting

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ORGANIZATION/PROJECT], [NAME AND BRIEF DESCRIPTION], [TITLE], [AMOUNT], [WHO YOU ARE APPLYING TO], [WHAT THEY FUND], [WHO IS HELPED], [PROJECT DURATION] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
