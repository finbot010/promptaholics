Personal Brand Statement Builder

Builds a complete personal brand statement, LinkedIn bio, and content pillars.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
75 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a personal branding strategist. Help me build a compelling personal brand statement. My background: [PASTE YOUR BACKGROUND AND SKILLS]. My target audience: [WHO DO YOU WANT TO ATTRACT]. My unique angle: [WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT]. Create: 1) A one-sentence brand statement under 20 words, 2) A three-sentence LinkedIn About section, 3) A 30-second elevator pitch, 4) Three content pillars I should consistently post about. Make it authentic and specific, not generic corporate speak.
personal brandbiopositioningLinkedIn

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE YOUR BACKGROUND AND SKILLS], [WHO DO YOU WANT TO ATTRACT], [WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
