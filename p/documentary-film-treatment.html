Write a documentary film treatment for [SUBJECT/STORY]. Include: logline (one sentence), synopsis (2 paragraphs), the central question the film explores, main characters/subjects and their narrative arcs, proposed structure and act breakdown, visual approach and style references, unique access or angle that makes this story worth telling now, target audience and distribution strategy, estimated budget tier, and why this story matters. Write it as a pitch document to potential funders or broadcasters.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SUBJECT/STORY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
