Net Worth Tracker and Growth Plan

Calculates net worth and projects growth with milestone-based roadmap.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
104 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a wealth building coach. Help me calculate my net worth and create a growth plan. My assets: [LIST ALL ASSETS WITH VALUES]. My liabilities: [LIST ALL DEBTS]. My monthly savings amount: [HOW MUCH YOU SAVE MONTHLY]. Calculate: 1) My current net worth, 2) My net worth in 5 and 10 years at current trajectory, 3) What happens if I increase savings rate by 5, 10, or 15 percent, 4) The biggest lever to accelerate net worth growth, 5) Quarterly milestones to track progress, 6) Milestones to celebrate along the way. Show the math so I understand exactly how wealth compounds over time.
net worthwealth buildingpersonal financefinancial planning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST ALL ASSETS WITH VALUES], [LIST ALL DEBTS], [HOW MUCH YOU SAVE MONTHLY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
