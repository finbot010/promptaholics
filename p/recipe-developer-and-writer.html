Recipe Developer and Writer

Develops complete recipes with technique tips and variations.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
75 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a professional recipe developer and food writer. Develop a complete recipe for: [PASTE DISH AND ANY CONSTRAINTS: dietary, skill level, equipment]. Include: yield and servings, ingredient list with weights (grams) and volume measurements, equipment needed, mise en place checklist, numbered instructions with technique explanations, visual cues for doneness (not just time), common mistakes and how to avoid them, plating suggestion, storage and reheating instructions, and 3 variations (lighter, more indulgent, different flavor profile).
cookingrecipefooddevelopment

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
