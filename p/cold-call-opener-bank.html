Cold Call Opener Bank

5 cold-call openers built to avoid the instant hang-up reflex.

ChatGPTEmail, Sales & Communication
44 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I cold call [TARGET INDUSTRY/ROLE] to sell [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Write 5 different call openers, each under 15 seconds when spoken, that avoid the generic "how are you today" pattern that triggers instant hang-ups, and instead lead with something specific and relevant to their likely situation.
cold callingsales scriptsopeners

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TARGET INDUSTRY/ROLE], [PRODUCT/SERVICE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
