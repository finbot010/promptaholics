Meeting Summarizer Agent

Expert meeting analyst produces executive summary, discussion points, action items table with owners and deadlines, unresolved questions, and next meeting agenda.

ChatGPTAI Agents & Automation
91 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Act as an expert meeting analyst. Here are the raw notes or transcript from a meeting: [PASTE NOTES / TRANSCRIPT]. Produce a professional meeting summary including: a 3-sentence executive summary of what was decided, a bulleted list of key discussion points with brief context, a clear ACTION ITEMS table with: the specific task, the owner, the deadline, and the success criteria, any unresolved questions or items tabled for next meeting, and the recommended next meeting agenda based on what was discussed. Format for easy sharing with stakeholders who were not present.
meetingsummarynotesaction itemsagentproductivity

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE NOTES / TRANSCRIPT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
