The ELI5 Translator

Same explanation at two levels side by side — five-year-old and expert.

Explain [TOPIC] two ways, side by side: first, like you're explaining it to a curious 5-year-old using only simple words and a concrete example; then, the same idea explained precisely for someone with real background knowledge in the field. Label each clearly.

How to use this prompt
