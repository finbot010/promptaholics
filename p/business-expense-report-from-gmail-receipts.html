Business Expense Report From Gmail Receipts

Pulls business receipts from Gmail into a categorized expense list ready for your bookkeeping.

GeminiBusiness & Strategy
72 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Gmail Find receipts and invoices for my business from [START DATE] to [END DATE].

Business type: [DESCRIBE]
Categories I use: [e.g. software, advertising, equipment, travel, contractors]

Give me a table: date, vendor, amount, currency, category, and whether the receipt is attached. Total by category, flag possible personal purchases and duplicates, and list vendors I paid but could not find a receipt for. Format it so I can paste it into Google Sheets.
expensesreceiptsbookkeeping

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [START DATE], [END DATE], [DESCRIBE].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
