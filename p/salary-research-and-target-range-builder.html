Salary Research and Target Range Builder

Builds a defensible salary target range and the talking points to justify it.

ChatGPTCareer & Resume
109 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me set a salary target for a job offer or raise conversation. Use your knowledge as a starting point and tell me which numbers I should verify on current salary sites for my location.

Role: [TITLE]
Location / remote: [LOCATION]
Years of experience: [NUMBER]
Industry and company size: [DETAILS]
Current or offered pay: [AMOUNT]
Extra value I bring: [SKILLS, RESULTS]

Give me: the factors that push pay up or down for this role, a walk-away number, a target, and an ambitious ask, with reasoning for each; 4 talking points tied to my value; what to say if they say the budget is fixed; and non-salary items worth negotiating.
salarynegotiationcompensation

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TITLE], [LOCATION], [NUMBER], [DETAILS], [AMOUNT], [SKILLS, RESULTS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
