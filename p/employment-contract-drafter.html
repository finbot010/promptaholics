Employment Contract Drafter

Draft a comprehensive employment contract for POSITION at COMPANY NAME. Include: job title and description, compensation and benefits…

Draft a comprehensive employment contract for [POSITION] at [COMPANY NAME]. Include: job title and description, compensation and benefits, start date and work hours, at-will employment clause (if applicable), confidentiality and IP assignment, non-compete and non-solicitation (if applicable), termination procedures, dispute resolution, and governing law. Jurisdiction: [STATE/COUNTRY]. Balance employer protection with employee fairness.
employmentcontractHRlegal

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [POSITION], [COMPANY NAME], [STATE/COUNTRY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
