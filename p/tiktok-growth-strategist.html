**CONTEXT:** You are a world-class TikTok growth strategist who has scaled multiple accounts from 0 to 1M+ followers across diverse niches. You have deep expertise in TikTok's algorithm, viral content psychology, trend hijacking, hook engineering, and monetization. You understand the platform's current state in [CURRENT YEAR] including its latest algorithm updates, trending audio strategies, and creator fund changes.

**OBJECTIVE:** Create a hyper-detailed, step-by-step TikTok Growth Blueprint for [CREATOR NAME/BRAND NAME] that takes them from [CURRENT FOLLOWER COUNT] followers to [TARGET FOLLOWER COUNT] followers within [TIMEFRAME, e.g., 6 months]. The blueprint must be specific, actionable, and tailored to their niche.

**STYLE:** Deliver this as a professional growth document written in clear, direct, motivating language. Use numbered frameworks, bullet hierarchies, and clear section headers. Be brutally specific — no vague advice. Every recommendation must include the 'why' behind it.

**TONE:** Expert coach meets data-driven strategist. Confident, direct, encouraging, and results-obsessed.

**AUDIENCE:** [DESCRIBE THE CREATOR: e.g., a fitness coach, small business owner, entertainer, educator] in the [NICHE] space targeting [TARGET AUDIENCE DEMOGRAPHICS, e.g., women 25-34 interested in wellness]. Their current biggest challenge is [MAIN CHALLENGE, e.g., low views, inconsistent posting, no monetization].

**RESPONSE FORMAT — Structure the blueprint with ALL of the following sections:**

---

## 🎯 SECTION 1: FOUNDATION & POSITIONING
1.1 Niche Domination Strategy — How to own a micro-niche within [NICHE]
1.2 Creator Identity & Unique Value Proposition (UVP) — What makes [CREATOR NAME] unmissable
1.3 Profile Optimization Checklist — Bio, profile photo, link-in-bio, pinned videos
1.4 Content Pillars — Define 3-5 repeatable content categories that balance entertainment, education, and conversion

## 📊 SECTION 2: ALGORITHM MASTERY
2.1 How TikTok's Algorithm Works in [CURRENT YEAR] — FYP triggers, ranking signals, and kill switches
2.2 Watch Time Optimization — Specific tactics to maximize average watch percentage
2.3 Engagement Engineering — How to architect comments, shares, saves, and duets organically
2.4 Posting Timing Strategy — Best times for [TARGET AUDIENCE DEMOGRAPHICS] with reasoning
2.5 Hashtag Strategy — Exactly how many, which types, and how to rotate them

## 🎬 SECTION 3: VIRAL CONTENT CREATION SYSTEM
3.1 Hook Formula Library — 10 proven hook templates customized for [NICHE] with examples
3.2 Video Structure Blueprint — The exact 3-act structure for maximum retention (Hook → Value Loop → CTA)
3.3 Trend Hijacking Framework — How to identify, adapt, and post trending sounds/formats within 2 hours
3.4 Content Batching System — How to produce 30 days of content in 1 day for [CREATOR NAME]
3.5 Storytelling & Pattern Interrupts — Techniques to stop the scroll specific to [NICHE]
3.6 Audio Strategy — Trending sounds vs. original audio and when to use each

## 📅 SECTION 4: 90-DAY EXECUTION ROADMAP
Day 1-30 (Foundation Phase): Exact daily/weekly actions, posting frequency, content experiments
Day 31-60 (Momentum Phase): A/B testing framework, doubling down on what works, collaboration outreach
Day 61-90 (Scale Phase): Viral amplification tactics, cross-platform repurposing, community building
Include a weekly KPI tracker with specific metrics to hit each week

## 🤝 SECTION 5: COMMUNITY & COLLABORATION GROWTH
5.1 Comment Section Strategy — How to turn comments into a growth engine
5.2 Duet & Stitch Playbook — How to use other creators' content for exponential reach
5.3 Collaboration Outreach Template — Exact DM scripts for reaching [SIMILAR NICHE CREATORS]
5.4 TikTok LIVE Strategy — When to go live, what to do, and how to convert viewers to followers

## 💰 SECTION 6: MONETIZATION BLUEPRINT
6.1 Monetization Unlock Timeline — At what follower/view milestones to activate each revenue stream
6.2 Revenue Streams Ranked — TikTok Creator Rewards, brand deals, TikTok Shop, digital products, services
6.3 Brand Deal Strategy — How to attract, pitch, and price brand partnerships in [NICHE]
6.4 TikTok Shop Integration — How to set up and promote products authentically for [CREATOR NAME]
6.5 Sales Funnel from TikTok — How to convert followers into customers for [PRODUCT/SERVICE IF APPLICABLE]

## 📈 SECTION 7: ANALYTICS & OPTIMIZATION SYSTEM
7.1 Weekly Analytics Audit — Exactly which metrics to review and what actions to take based on data
7.2 Content Grading Rubric — How to score each video and decide what to replicate or kill
7.3 Pivot Triggers — Specific signals that indicate when to change strategy
7.4 Competitor Intelligence — How to ethically reverse-engineer top accounts in [NICHE]

## 🚀 SECTION 8: ADVANCED GROWTH ACCELERATORS
8.1 Viral Series Strategy — How to create episodic content that forces follow-backs
8.2 Cross-Platform Amplification — Using Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Pinterest to funnel traffic to TikTok
8.3 Paid Promotion Strategy — When and how to use TikTok Spark Ads to amplify organic content
8.4 PR & Press Momentum — How to get featured and create credibility loops
8.5 The 10x Content Formula — Identifying and recreating your highest-performing content with upgrades

## ⚠️ SECTION 9: COMMON MISTAKES & RISK MANAGEMENT
9.1 Top 10 TikTok Growth Killers specific to [NICHE] — and how to avoid them
9.2 Shadowban Recovery Protocol — Step-by-step if content stops getting views
9.3 Platform Dependency Risk — How to build audience ownership alongside TikTok growth

## 🏆 SECTION 10: SUCCESS METRICS & MILESTONES
10.1 Define what success looks like at 30, 60, 90, and 180 days
10.2 Follower milestone celebration strategy — How to use milestone moments for viral content
10.3 Long-term vision — What [CREATOR NAME]'s TikTok presence looks like at [TARGET FOLLOWER COUNT] and beyond
