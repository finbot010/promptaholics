Brand Voice & Tone Guide

A comprehensive brand voice guide that keeps all content consistent — used by agencies and in-house teams alike.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
94 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a complete brand voice and tone guide for [BRAND NAME] in [INDUSTRY].

Target audience: [DESCRIBE]
Brand personality: [3 ADJECTIVES]
Inspiration brands: [2-3 BRANDS WE ADMIRE]

Include:
1. Voice characteristics (4 traits with do/do not examples for each)
2. Tone shifts (how voice changes: website vs social vs customer support vs email)
3. Vocabulary guide: 10 words we use, 10 words we never use
4. Writing style rules (sentence length, punctuation, formatting)
5. 5 before/after rewrites showing the brand voice in action
6. A one-paragraph brand voice summary anyone on the team can memorize
brandingvoicetonecopywritingbrand

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BRAND NAME], [INDUSTRY], [DESCRIBE], [3 ADJECTIVES], [2-3 BRANDS WE ADMIRE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
