Cyberpunk World Builder

Design a fully immersive cyberpunk world centered around [CITY NAME]. Include political systems, mega-corporations, underground culture, black markets, technology level, architecture style, transportation, social hierarchy, AI influence, and environmental atmosphere. Create 3 unique factions with their own goals, aesthetics, and conflicts. Format as a world-building bible.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY NAME].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
