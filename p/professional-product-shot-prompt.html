Professional Product Shot Prompt

Apple/Nike-level product photographer creates hyper-realistic studio shots with 3 angles and negative prompts.

Lighting: Soft dramatic key light, subtle rim lighting, gentle reflections
Background: Pure white / lifestyle context / floating
