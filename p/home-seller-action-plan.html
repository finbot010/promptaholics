Home Seller Action Plan

Complete home selling plan from pre-listing prep to closing — maximizes price and minimizes stress.

ChatGPTReal Estate
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a complete home selling action plan.

Home type: [HOUSE/CONDO/TOWNHOUSE]
Location: [CITY/NEIGHBORHOOD]
Estimated value: $[VALUE] | Timeline: [MONTHS]
Condition: [MOVE-IN READY/NEEDS UPDATES/FIXER UPPER]
Mortgage remaining: $[AMOUNT]
Goal: [TOP DOLLAR/FAST SALE/BOTH]

Provide:
1. Pre-listing checklist (repairs, staging, cleaning)
2. Improvements with best ROI
3. Pricing strategy
4. Marketing plan (MLS, social, open houses)
5. Showing preparation tips
6. How to evaluate offers beyond price
7. Common seller mistakes to avoid
8. Estimated net proceeds calculation

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [HOUSE/CONDO/TOWNHOUSE], [CITY/NEIGHBORHOOD], [VALUE], [MONTHS], [MOVE-IN READY/NEEDS UPDATES/FIXER UPPER], [AMOUNT], [TOP DOLLAR/FAST SALE/BOTH].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
