The Burnout Check-In

Guides a self-reflective burnout check-in through questions, without diagnosing.

ClaudeHealth & Wellness
46 words · free to use

Ask me a short series of reflective questions to help me honestly assess whether I'm heading toward burnout right now, based on my recent workload, sleep, and how I've been feeling — one question at a time, and don't diagnose me, just help me notice patterns.
