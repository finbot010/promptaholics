Life Vision Board Planner

Complete life vision plan with vision statement, area goals, vision board concepts, and quarterly milestones.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
144 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me create a meaningful life vision and action plan.

Time horizon: [1 YEAR / 3 YEARS / 5 YEARS / 10 YEARS]
Areas of life to focus on: [CAREER / RELATIONSHIPS / HEALTH / FINANCES / TRAVEL / CREATIVITY / SPIRITUALITY]
Where I am now: [HONEST CURRENT STATE IN EACH AREA]
Where I want to be: [DESCRIBE YOUR IDEAL LIFE]
What's holding me back: [YOUR HONEST BLOCKERS]
Core values: [LIST 3-5 THINGS MOST IMPORTANT TO YOU]

Create:
1. Life vision statement (one paragraph — your ideal life described as if already real)
2. Goals for each life area (specific, measurable, time-bound)
3. Vision board categories and image concepts to find
4. The 3 keystone habits that would unlock everything else
5. Quarterly milestone breakdown
6. Morning review ritual to stay connected to the vision
7. How to handle setbacks without abandoning the plan

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [1 YEAR / 3 YEARS / 5 YEARS / 10 YEARS], [HONEST CURRENT STATE IN EACH AREA], [DESCRIBE YOUR IDEAL LIFE], [YOUR HONEST BLOCKERS], [LIST 3-5 THINGS MOST IMPORTANT TO YOU].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
