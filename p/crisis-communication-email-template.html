Crisis Communication Email Template

Creates crisis communication email templates for common business emergencies.

ChatGPTEmail, Sales & Communication
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a crisis communications specialist. Write crisis communication templates for: [PASTE BUSINESS TYPE AND CRISIS SCENARIO: data breach/product recall/service outage/PR issue]. Create: immediate acknowledgment email (within 1 hour), full explanation email (within 24 hours), resolution and prevention email (within 72 hours), customer compensation offer template, and media statement. For each: lead with empathy before explanation, be specific about what happened, own the mistake, state concrete next steps with timeline, and avoid legal jargon.
