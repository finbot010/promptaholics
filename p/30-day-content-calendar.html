30-Day Content Calendar

A complete 30-day content calendar with daily topics, hooks, CTAs, and posting times.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Build a 30-day content calendar for [BRAND/CREATOR] in the [NICHE] space.

Platforms: [INSTAGRAM/TIKTOK/YOUTUBE/LINKEDIN/ALL]
Posting frequency: [DAILY/3X WEEK/5X WEEK]
Content pillars: [LIST 3-5 THEMES]
Target audience: [DESCRIBE]
Goal: [GROW/SELL/EDUCATE/ENTERTAIN]

For each day:
- Content type (Reel, carousel, story, long-form)
- Topic/angle
- Hook (first line or first 3 seconds)
- Primary CTA
- Best posting time

Also include:
- 4 evergreen ideas to reuse monthly
- 3 trending formats to try
- Weekly theme structure

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BRAND/CREATOR], [NICHE], [INSTAGRAM/TIKTOK/YOUTUBE/LINKEDIN/ALL], [DAILY/3X WEEK/5X WEEK], [LIST 3-5 THEMES], [DESCRIBE], [GROW/SELL/EDUCATE/ENTERTAIN].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
