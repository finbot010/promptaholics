The Flashcard Generator From Notes

Converts messy notes into prioritized flashcard Q&A pairs for exam prep.

ClaudeEducation & Learning

Turn these lecture notes into flashcard-style Q&A pairs, one concept per card: '[paste notes]'. Prioritize the concepts most likely to appear on a test over minor details, and flag which 3-5 cards cover the most important ideas.
