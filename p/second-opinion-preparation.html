Second Opinion Preparation

Organizes your diagnosis, records and questions so a second-opinion appointment is as useful as possible.

ClaudeHealth & Wellness
95 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me prepare for a second opinion. I am not asking you to give the second opinion.

Diagnosis or recommendation I received: [DETAILS]
Treatment options offered: [LIST]
Tests and records I have: [LIST]
What I am unsure or worried about: [CONCERNS]

Give me: a one-page summary to share with the new doctor; a list of records to request and how to request them; 10 questions to ask (about the diagnosis, alternatives, risks, what happens if I wait); how to compare the two opinions; and how to bring it up with my current doctor without awkwardness.
second opinionmedical decisionspatient advocacy

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DETAILS], [LIST], [CONCERNS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
