Sales Page Copywriter

Professional direct-response sales page using proven copywriting frameworks used by 6 and 7-figure businesses.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
100 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a high-converting sales page for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [IDEAL CUSTOMER].

Product: [DESCRIBE]
Price: [PRICE]
Biggest objection: [MAIN HESITATION]
Key transformation: [BEFORE STATE → AFTER STATE]

Use this structure:
- Headline: Outcome-focused, specific, stops the scroll
- Subheadline: Addresses the main objection
- Lead: Agitate the problem they have right now
- Body: Features as proof of benefits, not feature lists
- Social proof: [Add placeholder for testimonials]
- Objection handling: Address top 3 objections directly
- Guarantee: Risk reversal statement
- CTA: Specific and urgent, not 'Buy Now'
- P.S.: One final emotional nudge

Tone: Confident, direct, human. No hype.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT/SERVICE], [IDEAL CUSTOMER], [DESCRIBE], [PRICE], [MAIN HESITATION], [BEFORE STATE → AFTER STATE], [Add placeholder for testimonials].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
