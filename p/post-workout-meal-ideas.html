Post-Workout Meal Ideas

Suggests quick meals and snacks after exercise that fit your workout, schedule and tastes.

GeminiFood & Cooking
95 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Give me post-workout meal ideas.

Workout type: [e.g. weightlifting, running, yoga, team sports]
When I train: [MORNING / LUNCH / EVENING]
Time to cook afterwards: [MINUTES]
Foods I like or avoid: [DETAILS]
General goal: [e.g. build strength, more energy, eat more balanced]

Give me 10 ideas — 4 quick snacks, 4 full meals, 2 make-ahead options — with ingredients and why each is useful after this type of exercise (protein, carbs, hydration). Keep it food-first and practical. If I have a medical condition or specific targets, remind me to check with a doctor or dietitian.
post-workoutproteinhealthy meals

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MORNING / LUNCH / EVENING], [MINUTES], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
