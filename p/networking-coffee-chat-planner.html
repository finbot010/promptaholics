Networking Coffee Chat Planner

Writes the outreach message and questions for an informational interview or networking coffee chat.

ChatGPTCareer & Resume
93 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me plan a networking conversation.

Who: [PERSON'S ROLE, COMPANY, HOW I FOUND THEM]
What I want to learn: [e.g. how they got into the field, what the role is really like, advice on breaking in]
My background: [SHORT]
Format: [COFFEE / VIDEO CALL], [MINUTES]

Give me: a short outreach message that makes it easy to say yes; 10 thoughtful questions (not things I can Google); how to talk about myself in 30 seconds; how to end the conversation and ask for anything else (introductions, advice); and a thank-you message to send after.
networkinginformational interviewcareer growth

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PERSON'S ROLE, COMPANY, HOW I FOUND THEM], [SHORT], [COFFEE / VIDEO CALL], [MINUTES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
