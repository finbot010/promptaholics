Travel Budget Breakdown Builder

Creates detailed travel budgets with money-saving strategies.

ChatGPTTravel
70 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a budget travel strategist. Build a complete travel budget for: [PASTE DESTINATION, DURATION, TRAVEL STYLE, HOME COUNTRY]. Break down by category: flights (when to book, budget airlines), accommodation (types ranked by value), food (street food to mid-range ratio), transportation within destination, activities and entrance fees, travel insurance estimate, souvenirs and shopping, emergency buffer. Give daily budget targets for: budget/mid-range/comfortable traveler. Include 5 money-saving hacks specific to this destination.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
