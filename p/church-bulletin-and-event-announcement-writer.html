Church Bulletin and Event Announcement Writer

Writes warm, clear announcements for church bulletins, newsletters and social media.

GeminiWriting & Content
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write announcements for our church.

Church name: [NAME]
Items this week: [EVENT, DATE, TIME, PLACE, WHO IT'S FOR, HOW TO SIGN UP — for each]
Tone: [TRADITIONAL / WARM AND MODERN]
Languages: [ENGLISH / SPANISH / BOTH]

For each item, write a bulletin version (2-3 sentences), a social media post, and a short spoken announcement for the service. Keep the details accurate to what I gave, and add a welcoming line for first-time visitors.
churchannouncementscommunity events

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME], [TRADITIONAL / WARM AND MODERN], [ENGLISH / SPANISH / BOTH].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
