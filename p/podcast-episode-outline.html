Podcast Episode Outline

Complete podcast episode outline with cold open, timed segments, clip moments, and show notes.

Create a detailed podcast episode outline for [PODCAST NAME].

Episode topic: [TOPIC]
Episode type: [SOLO / INTERVIEW / DEBATE / STORYTELLING / Q&A]
Target length: [20 / 45 / 60 / 90] minutes
Guest (if any): [NAME + BACKGROUND]
Listeners are: [DESCRIBE YOUR AUDIENCE]
Key takeaway: [WHAT LISTENERS REMEMBER AFTER]

Outline:
1. Cold open (30-60 sec): Start mid-story or with the most compelling moment
2. Intro/theme music note
3. Host intro and episode setup (2 min)
4. Guest intro — what to ask, what to avoid (interview only)
5. Main segment breakdown (5-8 topics with timing and transition notes)
6. Signature segment (if you have one)
7. Listener question or engagement moment
8. Key quote or soundbite moment to clip for social
9. Outro and CTA
10. Show notes bullet points

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PODCAST NAME], [TOPIC], [SOLO / INTERVIEW / DEBATE / STORYTELLING / Q&A], [20 / 45 / 60 / 90], [NAME + BACKGROUND], [DESCRIBE YOUR AUDIENCE], [WHAT LISTENERS REMEMBER AFTER].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
