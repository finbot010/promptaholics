The Subscription Audit Prompt

Sorts a subscription list into keep/downgrade/cancel and flags likely duplicates.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
50 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Here's a list of my monthly subscriptions and what I pay for each: '[paste list]'. Help me sort them into: clearly worth keeping, worth downgrading to a cheaper tier, and candidates to cancel — based on how each one is typically used, and flag any that look like duplicate services.
budgetingsubscriptionsexpense audit

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
