The Competitor Assassin

Ruthless competitor breakdown with fatal weaknesses, Blue Ocean strategy, and a 90-day attack plan.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding★ Featured
84 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Conduct a ruthless competitive analysis of [YOUR INDUSTRY/NICHE]. Identify the top 5 competitors and for each one analyze: (1) Their core value proposition — what they promise, (2) Their pricing model and positioning, (3) Their biggest strength you should respect, (4) Their fatal weakness you can exploit, (5) What their customers complain about most. Then give me a 'Blue Ocean' strategy — 3 specific moves I can make that none of them are doing. End with a 90-day attack plan to steal market share.
competitive analysismarket researchbusiness strategySWOT

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR INDUSTRY/NICHE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
