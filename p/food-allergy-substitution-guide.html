Food Allergy Substitution Guide

Creates comprehensive cooking substitution guides for any dietary restriction.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
67 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a culinary expert specializing in dietary restrictions. Create a complete substitution guide for cooking without: [PASTE ALLERGEN OR INGREDIENT: gluten/dairy/eggs/nuts/soy]. For each common cooking function (binding, leavening, creaminess, fat, flavor), provide: 2-3 substitutions with ratios, how the substitution changes texture/flavor, when to use each option, and which recipes work best vs need significant adaptation. Include a shopping list of pantry staples for this dietary restriction.
