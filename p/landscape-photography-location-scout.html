Landscape Photography Location Scout

Plans landscape photography shoots with professional scouting detail.

ChatGPTImage & Art
54 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a landscape photographer and location scout. Help me plan a landscape shoot at: [PASTE LOCATION]. Include: best time of day and golden hour timing, seasonal considerations, key compositions to look for, equipment checklist, weather conditions to target vs avoid, safety considerations, backup locations nearby, and 3 hero shot concepts for this location.
photographylandscapescoutingplanning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE LOCATION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
