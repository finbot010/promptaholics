Parent-Teacher Conference Prep

Prepares parents for a parent-teacher conference with questions, notes and a plan to support their child.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
78 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me prepare for a parent-teacher conference.

Child's grade: [GRADE]
What is going well: [NOTES]
My concerns: [e.g. reading level, friendships, homework battles, behavior]
What my child says about school: [NOTES]
Conference length: [MINUTES]

Give me: the 5 most important questions to ask, in priority order; how to raise my concerns without sounding defensive; what information to bring; how to agree on a plan with clear next steps; and a short follow-up email to send the teacher afterward.
parentsteachersschool

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [GRADE], [NOTES], [MINUTES].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
