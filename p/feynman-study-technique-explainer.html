Feynman Study Technique Explainer

Applies the Feynman Technique to master any concept quickly.

You are a learning coach using the Feynman Technique. Take this concept and teach it back to me as if I were a 12-year-old: [PASTE CONCEPT]. Then identify the gaps in my understanding, simplify any jargon, use 2 analogies, and give me 3 practice questions to test retention. Finally, suggest the next concept I should learn to build on this.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE CONCEPT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
