Performance Review Self-Assessment Writer

Drafts a confident, evidence-based self-assessment for your annual review or promotion case.

ClaudeCareer & Resume
99 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me write my performance review self-assessment.

Role and level: [TITLE / LEVEL]
Review period: [DATES]
What I worked on and shipped: [LIST]
Feedback I received: [QUOTES OR SUMMARY]
Goals I was set: [LIST]
What I want from this review: [RAISE / PROMOTION / NEW SCOPE]

Write: an opening summary, achievements tied to business impact, goals met and missed (honest about misses, with what I learned), how I helped others, and my goals for next period. Keep it confident but factual. Then give me 3 talking points for the review conversation and how to ask for what I want.
performance reviewpromotionself-assessment

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TITLE / LEVEL], [DATES], [LIST], [QUOTES OR SUMMARY], [RAISE / PROMOTION / NEW SCOPE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
