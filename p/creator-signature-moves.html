Creator Signature Moves

Develop 5 signature moves or stylistic elements that [CREATOR] can use consistently to build instant brand recognition. For each signature move: name it, describe exactly what it is, explain why it works for this creator's brand, show how it appears across different formats (video, written, audio), give 3 examples of how to execute it, and explain how it builds audience expectation over time. Examples of signature moves: always ending with a provocative question, always opening with a personal micro-story, a signature visual or verbal tic, always including one piece of counterintuitive advice, or a recurring format like a weekly myth-busting segment.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CREATOR].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
