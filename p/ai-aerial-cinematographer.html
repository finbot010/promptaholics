Write an AI video prompt that simulates aerial cinematography for [LOCATION/SUBJECT]. Specify: starting altitude and frame, descent or ascent pattern, reveal timing, landscape elements that create depth, time of day light quality, and the emotional impact of the scale shift from aerial to ground level.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LOCATION/SUBJECT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and send it.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
