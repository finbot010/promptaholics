Mental Health Check-In

A structured mental wellness check-in using evidence-based coaching — great for weekly reflection.

ChatGPTMore Prompts
118 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Act as a compassionate, evidence-based mental wellness coach. Guide me through a structured check-in.

Ask me questions one at a time about:
1. How I am feeling physically right now (energy, sleep, body)
2. My emotional state this week (main emotions, what triggered them)
3. My biggest stressor and what it is really about
4. What I have been avoiding that I know I should face
5. One thing I am genuinely proud of this week

After each answer, reflect back what you heard and ask a deepening question. At the end, give me:
- A one-sentence summary of my current state
- One small action to feel 10% better today
- One reframe for my biggest stressor

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
