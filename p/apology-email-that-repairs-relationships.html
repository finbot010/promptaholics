Apology Email That Repairs Relationships

Writes a sincere professional apology that genuinely repairs damaged relationships.

ClaudeEmail, Sales & Communication
106 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me write a sincere professional apology email. What happened: [DESCRIBE THE MISTAKE]. Who was affected: [RECIPIENT AND HOW THEY WERE IMPACTED]. My role in what happened: [YOUR PART HONESTLY]. What I have done or plan to do to fix it: [YOUR CORRECTIVE ACTION]. Write an apology email that: 1) Opens with the apology directly, no buildup, 2) Acknowledges specifically what went wrong without deflecting, 3) Shows understanding of the impact on them, 4) States what you are doing to fix it, 5) Commits to preventing it in the future. No I am sorry you feel that way. No passive voice around responsibility. Sincere and direct.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE THE MISTAKE], [RECIPIENT AND HOW THEY WERE IMPACTED], [YOUR PART HONESTLY], [YOUR CORRECTIVE ACTION].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
