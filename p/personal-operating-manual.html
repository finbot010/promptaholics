Personal Operating Manual

Writes a 'how to work with me' guide that helps teammates communicate and collaborate with you.

ClaudeAI Agents & Automation
75 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me write a personal operating manual ("how to work with me").

Role and team: [DETAILS]
How I like to communicate: [CHANNELS, RESPONSE TIMES]
When I do my best work: [TIMES, CONDITIONS]
What I value in colleagues: [LIST]
My blind spots or quirks: [HONEST NOTES]
How I like feedback: [PREFERENCES]

Write a one-page manual with clear sections, friendly tone, and specific examples. Then suggest 3 questions I could ask teammates so we can swap manuals.
working styleteam communicationself-awareness

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DETAILS], [CHANNELS, RESPONSE TIMES], [TIMES, CONDITIONS], [LIST], [HONEST NOTES], [PREFERENCES].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
