Customer Win-Back Campaign

4-email win-back sequence with escalating offers to re-engage churned customers gracefully.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
108 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a win-back email sequence for churned customers of [BUSINESS].

Business type: [DESCRIBE]
Average churn reason: [WHY CUSTOMERS LEAVE]
Time since they left: [WEEKS/MONTHS]
Best offer I can make: [DISCOUNT/FEATURE/UPGRADE/GIFT]
Customer LTV: $[AMOUNT]

Write a 4-email sequence:
Email 1 (Day 1): We miss you — acknowledge they left, no pressure, just checking in
Email 2 (Day 7): Here's what changed — show improvements since they left
Email 3 (Day 14): Special offer — your best re-engagement offer with clear deadline
Email 4 (Day 21): Final goodbye — close the loop, leave door open, ask for feedback

For each email: subject line, preview text, body (under 150 words), CTA
win-backchurnemailre-engagementretention

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BUSINESS], [DESCRIBE], [WHY CUSTOMERS LEAVE], [WEEKS/MONTHS], [DISCOUNT/FEATURE/UPGRADE/GIFT], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
