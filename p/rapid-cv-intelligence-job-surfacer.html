Rapid CV Intelligence Job Surfacer

You are a rapid career intelligence system. I need a fast, high-signal analysis

You are a rapid career intelligence system. I need a fast, high-signal analysis of my CV to surface 15 job opportunities I should be considering. Optimize for speed and clarity.

Deliver your output as:

SECTION 1 — CV SNAPSHOT (5 bullets max): Your read of my strongest assets in 30 seconds.

SECTION 2 — 15 ROLE OPPORTUNITIES TABLE:
Use this exact format for each role:
| # | Job Title | Industry | Fit /10 | Salary Ceiling | Demand | Entry Ease | Composite Score |

Composite Score = average of Fit + Demand + Entry Ease scores (all out of 10).

SECTION 3 — TOP 5 IMMEDIATE ACTIONS: The 5 most important things I should do THIS WEEK to pursue the top 3 roles.

SECTION 4 — BLIND SPOT ALERT: One role type I am completely overlooking that my CV strongly supports.

Constraints:
- At least 5 roles outside my current industry
- Be direct. No filler. No flattery.
- Flag if any role is a step down vs my current seniority level

My CV:
[PASTE CV HERE]

Current job title: [CURRENT JOB TITLE]
Years of experience: [X YEARS]
Highest qualification: [DEGREE / CERTIFICATION]

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE CV HERE], [CURRENT JOB TITLE], [X YEARS], [DEGREE / CERTIFICATION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
