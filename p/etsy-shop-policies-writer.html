Etsy Shop Policies Writer

Writes clear, friendly shop policies for an Etsy or handmade shop.

ClaudeMarketing & Branding
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write my shop policies.

Shop: [NAME, WHAT I SELL — e.g. handmade jewelry, digital downloads, custom prints]
Processing time: [DAYS]
Shipping: [WHERE, CARRIERS, UPGRADES]
Returns and exchanges: [MY RULES, or "suggest fair ones"]
Custom orders: [YES / NO — RULES]
Digital products: [IF ANY]

Write sections for processing and shipping, returns and exchanges, damaged or lost orders, custom orders, care instructions, and FAQs. Keep it friendly and short, protect me from common problems (custom items, wrong addresses), and remind me to match the platform's own policy settings.
etsyshop policieshandmade business

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DAYS], [WHERE, CARRIERS, UPGRADES], [MY RULES, or "suggest fair ones"], [YES / NO — RULES], [IF ANY].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
