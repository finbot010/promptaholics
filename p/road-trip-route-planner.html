Road Trip Route Planner

Plans a road trip route with driving legs, overnight stops, scenic detours and fuel or charging breaks.

GeminiTravel
78 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Plan a road trip.

Start: [CITY] End: [CITY] (or round trip)
Days available: [NUMBER]
Max driving per day: [HOURS]
Vehicle: [GAS / ELECTRIC — RANGE]
Interests: [NATIONAL PARKS / SMALL TOWNS / FOOD / BEACHES]

Give me: the route with each day's driving leg and time; overnight stop suggestions; 2-3 worthwhile detours per day with extra time needed; fuel or charging stops; and places where I should book ahead. Flag any roads that may close seasonally.
road triproutegoogle maps

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY], [NUMBER], [HOURS], [GAS / ELECTRIC — RANGE], [NATIONAL PARKS / SMALL TOWNS / FOOD / BEACHES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
