Hotel Shortlist by Neighborhood

Finds hotels in the right neighborhood for your trip style and compares them on price, location and reviews.

GeminiTravel
79 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Hotels Help me choose where to stay in [CITY] from [DATE] to [DATE].

Travelers: [WHO]
Budget per night: [AMOUNT]
Trip style: [e.g. sightseeing on foot, nightlife, quiet, family with kids, business]
Must-haves: [e.g. breakfast, free cancellation, pool, near metro]

First recommend the best 2-3 neighborhoods for my style and why. Then list 3 hotels in each with price, rating, distance to my key places: [PLACES], and a one-line pro and con. Pick one overall and one budget option.
hotelsgoogle hotelswhere to stay

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CITY], [DATE], [WHO], [AMOUNT], [PLACES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
