Isometric City Diorama

Generates a miniature isometric city diorama with iconic landmarks and weather.

ChatGPTImage & Art
62 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Present a clear, 45-degree top-down isometric miniature 3D cartoon scene of [city name], featuring its most iconic landmarks and architectural elements. Use soft, refined textures with realistic PBR materials and gentle, lifelike lighting and shadows. Integrate the current weather conditions into the environment. Use a clean, minimalistic composition with a soft solid-colored background and a bold city name title at the top.
