Rewrite this text to sound more authoritative, confident, and expert-level. Text: [PASTE]. Apply authority writing principles: remove every hedging word, replace I think/I believe with declarative statements, use the vocabulary of someone who has solved this problem 100 times, add one named principle or proprietary term, restructure from question to answer format, and end with a prediction rather than a suggestion.
