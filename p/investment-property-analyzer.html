Investment Property Analyzer

Full investment property analysis covering strategy, numbers, risks, and deal verdict.

ChatGPTReal Estate
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Analyze this real estate investment opportunity.

Strategy: [BUY-AND-HOLD/FLIP/SHORT-TERM RENTAL/COMMERCIAL]
Property: [DESCRIBE TYPE AND CONDITION]
Purchase price: $[PRICE]
Estimated repairs: $[COST]
ARV (after repair value): $[ESTIMATED]
Market: [CITY/NEIGHBORHOOD]

Analyze:
1. Is this the right strategy for this property?
2. All-in cost calculation
3. Projected profit or monthly cash flow
4. Risk factors specific to this deal
5. Exit strategy options
6. Key due diligence steps before committing
7. Verdict: strong deal, pass, or negotiate harder?
investmentpropertyreal estateanalysisstrategy

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BUY-AND-HOLD/FLIP/SHORT-TERM RENTAL/COMMERCIAL], [DESCRIBE TYPE AND CONDITION], [PRICE], [COST], [ESTIMATED], [CITY/NEIGHBORHOOD].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
