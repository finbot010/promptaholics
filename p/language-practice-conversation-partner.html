Language Practice Conversation Partner

Runs a real-world conversation practice in your target language and corrects you gently as you go.

GeminiEducation & Learning
91 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Be my conversation partner to practice [LANGUAGE].
My level: [A1-C2 or BEGINNER/INTERMEDIATE/ADVANCED]
Scenario: [e.g. ordering at a restaurant, a job interview, renting an apartment, small talk with a neighbor]

Rules: stay in character and speak mostly in [LANGUAGE] at my level. After each of my replies, add a short correction section in English: my mistakes, the natural way to say it, and one new phrase to try. Ask follow-up questions so I have to talk. After 10 exchanges, give me a summary of my common mistakes and 10 words to review.
language learningconversationpractice

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LANGUAGE], [A1-C2 or BEGINNER/INTERMEDIATE/ADVANCED].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
