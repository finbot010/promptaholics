Travel Poster Generator

Converts a travel photo into a clean, flat-vector travel poster illustration.

ChatGPTImage & Art
62 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Transform the input photo into a high-quality modern flat vector illustration in the style of a corporate travel poster. Feature simplified shapes, clean lines, and a smooth matte finish. Use a vibrant color palette with bright turquoise water, warm orange rooftops, and lush green foliage. Remove all photorealistic textures and noise. Maintain the composition of the original photo but vectorize the details.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
