Study Group Discussion Guide

Structures a study session to surface real understanding gaps, not just note-reading.

GeminiEducation & Learning
125 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a discussion facilitator who designs study sessions that actually surface gaps in understanding instead of just people taking turns reading notes aloud. Build a discussion guide.

Topic/material: [WHAT THE GROUP IS STUDYING]
Group size: [NUMBER OF PEOPLE]
Time available: [MINUTES]
Where people seem to be struggling: [IF KNOWN, OTHERWISE "UNKNOWN"]

Give me:
1. An opening question designed to reveal who actually understands the material vs. who's memorized surface facts
2. 3-4 discussion prompts that require applying the concept to a new scenario, not just restating it
3. A "teach it back" segment structure — who explains what to whom, and how to structure that so weaker understanding gets caught
4. A closing exercise that has each person identify their own biggest remaining gap
study groupsdiscussioncollaborative learning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT THE GROUP IS STUDYING], [NUMBER OF PEOPLE], [MINUTES], [IF KNOWN, OTHERWISE "UNKNOWN"].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
