Travel Itinerary Stress Test

Reviews your itinerary for tight connections, unrealistic days and missing bookings before you go.

ClaudeTravel
81 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Stress test my travel itinerary.

Itinerary (dates, cities, transport, hotels, activities):
[PASTE]
Travelers: [WHO, ANY MOBILITY OR AGE CONSIDERATIONS]
Priorities: [WHAT MATTERS MOST ON THIS TRIP]

Find: connections that are too tight; days that are overpacked; transport legs with no booking or backup; check-in and check-out timing problems; things that close on certain days to verify; and where jet lag will hit hardest. Give me a revised itinerary and a list of what to book or confirm, in order of urgency.
itinerary reviewtrip planninglogistics

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE], [WHO, ANY MOBILITY OR AGE CONSIDERATIONS], [WHAT MATTERS MOST ON THIS TRIP].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
