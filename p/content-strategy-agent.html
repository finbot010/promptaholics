Content Strategy Agent

Chief Content Strategist builds content pillars, format matrix, publishing frequency, 90-day editorial framework, unique angle, and audience growth flywheel.

ChatGPTAI Agents & Automation
94 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Act as my Chief Content Strategist. My brand: [DESCRIBE YOUR BRAND / BUSINESS]. My audience: [DESCRIBE]. My goals: [TRAFFIC / LEADS / SALES / COMMUNITY / AUTHORITY]. Build a comprehensive content strategy: the 3-5 content pillars that define my authority territory, a content type matrix showing which formats serve which goals, the optimal publishing frequency for each channel given my resources, a 90-day editorial calendar framework, my unique content angle that competitors cannot easily replicate, and an audience growth flywheel — how each piece of content builds toward the next. Include metrics to track.
content strategyagenteditorialplanningaudiencegrowth

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE YOUR BRAND / BUSINESS], [DESCRIBE], [TRAFFIC / LEADS / SALES / COMMUNITY / AUTHORITY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
