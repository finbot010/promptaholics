Creator Persona Simulator

Simulate how [CREATOR NAME] would speak, think, and respond in the following scenarios. Creator profile: [DESCRIBE THEIR VOICE, VALUES, AND STYLE]. Simulate their response to: 1. A DM from a fan asking for advice on starting out 2. A controversial question about their niche 3. A brand partnership pitch they would decline 4. Introducing themselves at a live event 5. Responding to criticism online 6. Explaining their philosophy in 60 seconds. For each scenario write the response exactly as they would — in their voice, with their vocabulary, and reflecting their values. This is a voice training document.
personacreatorsimulationvoice

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CREATOR NAME], [DESCRIBE THEIR VOICE, VALUES, AND STYLE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
