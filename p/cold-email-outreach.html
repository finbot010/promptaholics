Cold Email Outreach

High-converting cold emails that get replies.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding★ Featured
58 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a cold email to [TARGET PERSONA] for [PRODUCT/SERVICE].

- Subject: under 50 chars, creates curiosity
- Opening: personalized hook (NOT 'I hope this finds you well')
- Value prop: one sentence, one problem solved
- Social proof: one specific result or client
- CTA: single low-friction ask
- Total: under 130 words

Tone: direct, human. Zero buzzwords.
emailsalesoutreachB2B

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TARGET PERSONA], [PRODUCT/SERVICE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
