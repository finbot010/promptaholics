Mental Health Self-Assessment

Structured mental health self-assessment to understand symptoms and decide on next steps.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
112 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Guide me through a mental health self-assessment to better understand how I am doing.

I want to assess: [DEPRESSION/ANXIETY/STRESS/GENERAL WELLNESS/ALL]
Changes I have noticed: [MOOD/SLEEP/APPETITE/ENERGY/CONCENTRATION/SOCIAL WITHDRAWAL]
Duration: [HOW LONG]
Recent life events: [ANY MAJOR STRESSORS OR CHANGES]
Support system: [DESCRIBE]
Previous mental health experience: [THERAPY/MEDICATION/DIAGNOSIS]

Help me:
1. Understand what my symptoms might indicate
2. Differentiate normal responses from clinical concerns
3. Rate the urgency of professional support
4. What type of professional to see first
5. What to say when making the first appointment
6. Self-care strategies for my level of concern
7. Crisis resources if needed

Note: This is not a diagnosis — a tool to help communicate with professionals.
mental healthdepressionanxietyassessmentwellness

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DEPRESSION/ANXIETY/STRESS/GENERAL WELLNESS/ALL], [HOW LONG], [ANY MAJOR STRESSORS OR CHANGES], [DESCRIBE], [THERAPY/MEDICATION/DIAGNOSIS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
