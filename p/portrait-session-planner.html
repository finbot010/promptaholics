Portrait Session Planner

Plans a portrait session with poses, locations, timing and directions to relax the subject.

ChatGPTImage & Art
80 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan a portrait session.

Subject: [WHO — e.g. couple, family with kids, professional headshot, senior photos]
Style: [NATURAL / EDITORIAL / MOODY / BRIGHT]
Location options: [LIST]
Time and date: [WHEN]
Gear: [CAMERA, LENSES, REFLECTOR, FLASH]

Give me: the best time of day for light at these locations; a shot list of 15 poses or setups with direction cues I can say out loud; how to warm up a nervous subject; outfit advice to send beforehand; and a session timeline.
portraitsposingphotoshoot

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NATURAL / EDITORIAL / MOODY / BRIGHT], [LIST], [WHEN], [CAMERA, LENSES, REFLECTOR, FLASH].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
