Spice and Flavor Pairing Guide

Explains which herbs, spices and sauces pair with an ingredient and gives ready-to-use blends.

GeminiFood & Cooking
71 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want to cook [MAIN INGREDIENT — e.g. chicken thighs, chickpeas, salmon, eggplant] in a way that isn't boring.

Give me 5 flavor directions from different cuisines (for example Mexican, Indian, Mediterranean, Korean, Caribbean). For each: a spice blend or sauce with exact amounts for [AMOUNT OF MAIN INGREDIENT], the cooking method that suits it, one side dish, and one finishing touch (acid, herb, crunch). Keep the ingredients easy to find.
spicesflavorseasoning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AMOUNT OF MAIN INGREDIENT].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
