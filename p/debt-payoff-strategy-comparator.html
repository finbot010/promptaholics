Debt Payoff Strategy Comparator

Compares snowball vs. avalanche payoff methods using your real debt numbers.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
47 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Here are my debts: "[LIST EACH DEBT WITH BALANCE AND INTEREST RATE]". Compare the debt snowball method (smallest balance first) versus the debt avalanche method (highest interest first) specifically for my numbers, showing roughly how much faster or how much less interest I'd pay with each approach.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST EACH DEBT WITH BALANCE AND INTEREST RATE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
