Text:
[PASTE]
Grade level: [GRADE]
Purpose: [CLASS DISCUSSION / HOMEWORK / ASSESSMENT]

Give me: 8 vocabulary words with student-friendly definitions; questions at three levels (5 literal "right there," 5 inferential "think and search," 3 critical "author and me"); 2 discussion prompts; a short writing task; and an answer key. Note any content in the text that may need context or sensitivity for this age group.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE], [GRADE], [CLASS DISCUSSION / HOMEWORK / ASSESSMENT].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
