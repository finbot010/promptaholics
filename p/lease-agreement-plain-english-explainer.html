Lease Agreement Plain-English Explainer

Turns a residential lease into a clear summary of costs, rules, deadlines and the clauses a tenant should push back on.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
125 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Explain this residential lease to me as a tenant, in plain English. Not legal advice; point out where local tenant law may override the lease.

Location (city/state/country): [LOCATION]
Lease:
[PASTE LEASE]

Give me:
1. Money summary: rent, due date, late fees, deposit, pet fees, utilities I pay, and any fees that could appear later.
2. Key dates: start, end, renewal notice deadline, rent increase rules, move-out notice.
3. My obligations: repairs, maintenance, guests, subletting, noise, alterations.
4. Landlord's obligations and entry rights.
5. How I can end the lease early and what it would cost.
6. Clauses that look unusual or unfair, quoted, with why.
7. Things I should document on move-in to protect my deposit.
8. Three questions to ask the landlord before signing.
leasetenantrental

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LOCATION], [PASTE LEASE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
