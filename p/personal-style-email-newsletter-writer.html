Personal-Style Email Newsletter Writer

Highly engaging 300-400 word email newsletter with compelling subject line, personal opening, valuable content, and natural CTA.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
76 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a highly engaging email newsletter for [BRAND / NICHE] targeting [SUBSCRIBER DESCRIPTION]. Structure: subject line (under 50 characters, curiosity or benefit driven) + preview text, personal opening paragraph that feels like a friend writing not a brand, main content section with one valuable insight, story, or exclusive piece of information, secondary section with a curated resource or recommendation, and a closing CTA that feels natural not pushy. Total length: 300-400 words. Conversational, warm, genuinely useful.
email newslettercontentengagementsubscribersmarketing

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BRAND / NICHE], [SUBSCRIBER DESCRIPTION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
