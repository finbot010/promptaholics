The Study System Designer

Complete study system with diagnostic, weekly schedule, spaced repetition, mnemonics, and peak performance protocol.

ChatGPTEducation & Learning
104 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Design a comprehensive study system for mastering [SUBJECT/EXAM] in [TIMEFRAME]. Include: (1) A diagnostic assessment — 10 questions to identify my current level and weak areas, (2) A week-by-week study schedule with daily time blocks (assume [X] hours/day available), (3) The optimal learning sequence — what to study first and why, (4) Active recall questions for each major topic, (5) Spaced repetition schedule — when to review what, (6) 3 mnemonic devices for the hardest concepts, (7) Practice test strategy — when to start, how to analyze mistakes, (8) A 'Day Before' protocol for peak performance. Adapt the plan for a [LEARNING STYLE] learner.
study planexam preplearning strategyspaced repetitionacademic

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SUBJECT/EXAM], [TIMEFRAME], [LEARNING STYLE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
