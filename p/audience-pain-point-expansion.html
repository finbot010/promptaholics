Audience Pain Point Expansion

Expand each pain point into a detailed emotional and practical explanation. Pain points: [LIST PAIN POINTS]. For each pain point expand into: the surface complaint (what they say), the practical impact (how it affects their daily life), the emotional weight (how it makes them feel), the identity wound (how it affects how they see themselves), the urgency trigger (what makes it feel urgent now), and the language they use to describe it. Output as a ready-to-use research document for copywriters.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST PAIN POINTS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
