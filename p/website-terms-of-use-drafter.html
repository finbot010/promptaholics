Website Terms of Use Drafter

Drafts website terms of use tailored to what your site actually does. Template to review for your jurisdiction.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
96 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Draft terms of use for my website. Mark it as a template to review for my jurisdiction.

Website and what it does: [DESCRIPTION]
Users can: [e.g. browse, create accounts, post content, buy products]
I sell: [PRODUCTS / SERVICES / NOTHING]
Ads or affiliate links: [YES / NO]
User-generated content: [YES / NO]
Country/state: [LOCATION]

Include: acceptance, eligibility, accounts, acceptable use, user content license and moderation, intellectual property, purchases and refunds (if any), disclaimers, limitation of liability, termination, governing law placeholder, changes, and contact. Then list the sections that most need a lawyer's review for my situation.
terms of usewebsitetemplate

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIPTION], [PRODUCTS / SERVICES / NOTHING], [YES / NO], [LOCATION].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
