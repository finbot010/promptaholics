Short-Form Video Hook Generator

Generates short-form video hooks across six distinct psychological mechanisms, with exact opening lines.

ChatGPTVideo & Film
127 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a short-form video strategist who knows the first 2 seconds decide whether someone keeps watching or scrolls past. Write me hooks for:

Video topic/content: [WHAT THE VIDEO IS ABOUT]
Platform: [TIKTOK / REELS / SHORTS]
Target audience: [WHO YOU'RE MAKING THIS FOR]

Give me 6 hook options, each using a genuinely different mechanism:
1. A pattern interrupt (visually or verbally unexpected)
2. A direct callout of the target audience ("if you're a...")
3. A bold, specific claim that demands verification
4. A mid-action cold open (starts in the middle of something happening)
5. A question that the viewer can't NOT want answered
6. A relatable, specific pain point stated bluntly

For each, note the exact first line of spoken/on-screen text — not just the concept.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT THE VIDEO IS ABOUT], [TIKTOK / REELS / SHORTS], [WHO YOU'RE MAKING THIS FOR].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
