Short-Form Video Hook Rewriter

Rewrites the first three seconds of your Reels, TikToks or Shorts to stop the scroll.

ChatGPTVideo & Film
76 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Rewrite the opening of my short video.

Platform: [TIKTOK / REELS / SHORTS]
Video topic and payoff: [WHAT VIEWERS GET BY THE END]
Current opening line: [PASTE]
On-screen visual in the first 3 seconds: [DESCRIBE]

Give me 10 alternative hooks across types (curiosity, bold claim, mistake, result-first, question, story), each under 12 words, with a matching on-screen text and visual idea. Mark the 3 strongest and explain why. No clickbait the video does not deliver on.
short-form videohooksreels

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TIKTOK / REELS / SHORTS], [WHAT VIEWERS GET BY THE END], [PASTE], [DESCRIBE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
