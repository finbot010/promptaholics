Home Workout Program Builder

Builds complete 6-week home workout programs with full exercise details.

ChatGPTHealth & Wellness
76 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a certified personal trainer. Build a 6-week home workout program for: [PASTE GOAL: weight loss/muscle building/endurance/flexibility, fitness level, available equipment, time per session]. Include: weekly training split with rest days, each workout fully detailed (exercise, sets, reps, rest, form cues), warm-up and cool-down routines, progression rules (when and how to increase difficulty), deload week protocol, and how to track progress without gym equipment. Add modifications for common limitations (bad knees, shoulder issues, back pain).

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
