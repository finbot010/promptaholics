You are the Chief Brand Voice Officer at a world-class creative agency. Using the StoryBrand framework, develop a complete verbal identity and emotional tone system for [BUSINESS NAME] in the [INDUSTRY] industry.

THE STORYBRAND LENS:
- Character (Hero): [TARGET CUSTOMER DESCRIPTION]
- Problem: External: [EXTERNAL PROBLEM] | Internal: [INTERNAL FEELING] | Philosophical: [DEEPER INJUSTICE]
- Guide: Position [BUSINESS NAME] as the trusted guide, not the hero.

DELIVER THE FOLLOWING:

1. BRAND PERSONALITY SPECTRUM
- Define [BUSINESS NAME] on 4 personality dimensions (e.g., Serious↔Playful, Classic↔Modern)
- Explain where on each spectrum the brand sits and why
- Include 3 brand archetypes (primary, secondary, shadow to avoid)

2. TONE OF VOICE GUIDELINES
- 4 core tone descriptors with definitions
- For each descriptor: 1 example of ON-BRAND copy vs OFF-BRAND copy
- The 3 words the brand NEVER uses

3. BRAND VOCABULARY
- 20 power words the brand owns
- 10 phrases that are distinctly [BUSINESS NAME]
- Naming conventions for products/services/features

4. EMOTIONAL BRAND TONE MAP
- Map tone across 6 customer touchpoints: Awareness, Consideration, Purchase, Onboarding, Retention, Advocacy
- Specify emotional goal and sample microcopy for each stage

5. SLOGAN DEVELOPMENT
- 10 slogan concepts across 3 strategic directions:
  a) Functional benefit slogans (3)
  b) Emotional aspiration slogans (4)
  c) Disruptive/provocative slogans (3)
- Score each on memorability, differentiation, and brand fit (1-10)
- Recommend the top 1 with full rationale

6. BRAND STORY (250 words)
- Written in the brand voice
- Uses the hero's journey with the customer as hero and the brand as guide

  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
