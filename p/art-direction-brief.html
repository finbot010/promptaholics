Art Direction Brief

Complete art direction briefs that give designers everything they need to nail the creative vision.

ChatGPTImage & Art
81 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write an art direction brief for [PROJECT].

Project type: [BRAND IDENTITY/CAMPAIGN/WEBSITE/BOOK COVER/PACKAGING]
Brand/Client: [NAME AND DESCRIPTION]
Target audience: [WHO WILL SEE THIS]
Core message: [WHAT IT MUST COMMUNICATE]
Tone and feeling: [EMOTIONAL RESPONSE WANTED]
Competitors: [LIST]

Brief sections:
1. Project overview and objectives
2. Brand personality (3-5 adjectives with explanations)
3. Color palette direction and psychology
4. Typography direction
5. Photography/illustration style
6. What to avoid
7. Mood board description (5-7 reference images to find)
8. Technical specs and file requirements
art directioncreative briefdesignvisual identitybranding

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PROJECT], [NAME AND DESCRIPTION], [WHO WILL SEE THIS], [WHAT IT MUST COMMUNICATE], [EMOTIONAL RESPONSE WANTED], [LIST].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
