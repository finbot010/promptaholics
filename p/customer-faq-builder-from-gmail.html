Customer FAQ Builder From Gmail

Finds the questions customers ask most in your Gmail and turns them into a website FAQ.

GeminiEmail, Sales & Communication
94 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Gmail Look through customer emails from the last [NUMBER] months (from people who are not on my team) and find the questions they ask most often.

1. List the top 15 questions, with how often each appears.
2. Group them into categories.
3. Write a clear FAQ answer for each (under 80 words), based on how I have already answered them in my replies.
4. Flag questions where my answers have been inconsistent.
5. Suggest which answers should also go on the product page or checkout to stop the questions in the first place.
gmailFAQcustomer questions

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
