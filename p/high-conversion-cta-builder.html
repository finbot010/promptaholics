High-Conversion CTA Builder

Generate 20 high-converting calls-to-action for [PRODUCT/SERVICE/CONTENT]. Create 4 CTAs in each style: Urgency-based (creates FOMO or deadline), Transformation-based (focuses on the outcome), Curiosity-based (opens a loop), Identity-based (speaks to who they want to be), and Value-based (makes the next step feel like a gift not a sale). For each CTA include the button text (under 5 words), the supporting line (under 15 words), and the psychological trigger used. Star the 5 strongest.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT/SERVICE/CONTENT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
