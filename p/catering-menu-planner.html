Catering Menu Planner

Creates complete catering menu plans for any event type.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
73 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a catering consultant and executive chef. Create a catering menu plan for: [PASTE EVENT TYPE, GUEST COUNT, BUDGET PER HEAD, DIETARY RESTRICTIONS, SERVICE STYLE: buffet/plated/stations]. Include: full menu with quantities, prep timeline starting 3 days before event, equipment and serving ware checklist, food safety temperature guide, plating or serving instructions, contingency for dietary restrictions, and cost breakdown per item. Flag any items that can be prepped ahead vs must be day-of.

How to use this prompt
