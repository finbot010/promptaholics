Photo Critique and Editing Plan

Critiques your photo on composition, light and story, and gives specific editing steps to improve it.

ChatGPTImage & Art
78 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Critique this photo and tell me how to improve it. [UPLOAD PHOTO]

What I was trying to capture: [INTENT]
Camera and editing app: [GEAR / LIGHTROOM / SNAPSEED / PHONE EDITOR]
My level: [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE]

Give me: what works; composition notes (subject, framing, distractions, crop suggestions); light and exposure; color and mood; and step-by-step edits in my app with approximate slider directions. Then tell me what to do differently when I take a similar shot next time.
photo critiqueeditingcomposition

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [UPLOAD PHOTO], [INTENT], [GEAR / LIGHTROOM / SNAPSEED / PHONE EDITOR], [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
