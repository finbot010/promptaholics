**CONTEXT:**
You are an expert HTML email designer and copywriter working for an HVAC company. The audience is existing customers who may have systems that are 10 years old or older. The company wants to send a warm, friendly promotional email announcing a limited-time $500 off discount on a new HVAC system replacement. Brand colors are: Dark Green (#1B4332), Light Green (#52B788), and White (#FFFFFF).

**STYLE:**
The tone must be friendly, warm, and conversational — like a trusted local business talking to a loyal customer. Avoid corporate jargon. Use encouraging, benefit-driven language. The design must be clean, modern, and mobile-responsive.

**TASK:**
Design a complete, production-ready HTML email that includes the following sections:

1. **Header** — Company logo placeholder ([COMPANY_LOGO_URL]), brand tagline, and a bold hero banner with the headline promoting $500 off a new system replacement. Use Dark Green (#1B4332) as the hero background with White text.

2. **Personalized Greeting** — Address the customer by first name using [CUSTOMER_FIRST_NAME]. Example: "Hi [CUSTOMER_FIRST_NAME], we've got something exciting just for you!"

3. **Intro Paragraph** — A friendly 2-3 sentence paragraph explaining that if their system is over 10 years old, now is the perfect time to upgrade, and that as a valued customer they're getting an exclusive $500 off offer.

4. **Benefits Section** — List EXACTLY 6 benefits of replacing an aging HVAC system (10+ years old). For EACH benefit, use a consistent emoji icon (pick ONE style — use emojis only, no images, keep it consistent across all 6 benefits). Each benefit must include:
   - An emoji icon
   - A bold benefit title
   - A 1-2 sentence description
   
   Benefits to cover:
   - Lower energy bills
   - Improved air quality
   - More consistent home comfort
   - Fewer breakdowns and repair costs
   - Quieter operation
   - Better smart home / thermostat compatibility

5. **Promotional Offer Block** — A visually distinct call-out box (use Light Green #52B788 background, White text) that prominently displays:
   - "$500 OFF Your New System Replacement"
   - Offer expiration date placeholder: [OFFER_EXPIRATION_DATE]
   - A bold CTA button: "Claim My $500 Discount" linking to [CTA_LINK_URL]

6. **Trust/Social Proof Line** — A short line mentioning [YEARS_IN_BUSINESS] years serving the community and a customer satisfaction placeholder like "Rated [STAR_RATING]/5 by our customers."

7. **Footer** — Include:
   - Company name: [COMPANY_NAME]
   - Address: [COMPANY_ADDRESS]
   - Phone: [COMPANY_PHONE]
   - Unsubscribe link: [UNSUBSCRIBE_LINK]
   - A short friendly sign-off like "Thank you for being a valued customer!"
   - Footer background: Dark Green (#1B4332), White text

**ACTION:**
Write the complete, valid HTML email code. The email must be:
- Fully inline-styled (no external CSS, no style blocks — all styles must be inline for email client compatibility)
- Mobile-responsive using a max-width of 600px centered layout
- Using a clean table-based layout for maximum email client compatibility (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail)
- All placeholder values wrapped in [BRACKETS] for easy find-and-replace
- Emoji icons must be consistent across all 6 benefit items (use only emojis, no image tags for icons)
- CTA button must be styled as a solid rounded button with Dark Green (#1B4332) background and White text, padding 14px 28px
- Benefits section cards should have a subtle Light Green (#52B788) left border or light green background tint (#E8F5E9) to stay on brand

**OUTPUT:**
Return the complete raw HTML email code, ready to be uploaded into any email platform such as Mailchimp, Klaviyo, or HubSpot. Include a brief comment at the top of the HTML (as an HTML comment) summarizing the placeholder variables that need to be replaced before sending.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [COMPANY_LOGO_URL], [CUSTOMER_FIRST_NAME], [OFFER_EXPIRATION_DATE], [CTA_LINK_URL], [YEARS_IN_BUSINESS], [STAR_RATING], [COMPANY_NAME], [COMPANY_ADDRESS] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
