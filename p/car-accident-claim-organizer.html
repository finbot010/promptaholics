Car Accident Claim Organizer

Organizes everything you need after a car accident for your insurance claim.

ChatGPTLegal & Admin
90 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I was in a car accident on [DATE] in [LOCATION]. Nobody needs emergency help now.

What happened: [SHORT DESCRIPTION]
Other driver's info: [WHAT I HAVE]
Police report: [YES — NUMBER / NO]
Damage and injuries: [DETAILS]
My insurer: [COMPANY]

Give me: a checklist of what to collect now (photos, witness details, medical records, repair estimates); a clean timeline; what to say and not say when calling insurers; how to track expenses and time off work; and signs it's worth talking to a lawyer. Keep it calm and step by step.
car accidentinsurance claimdocumentation

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DATE], [LOCATION], [SHORT DESCRIPTION], [WHAT I HAVE], [YES — NUMBER / NO], [DETAILS], [COMPANY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
