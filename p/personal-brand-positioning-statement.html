Personal Brand Positioning Statement

Finds your positioning: who you help, how, and why you, in one clear statement and a short bio.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
78 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me define my personal brand positioning.

What I do: [WORK / SKILLS]
Who I want to work with or reach: [AUDIENCE]
Results I have helped people get: [EXAMPLES]
What makes my approach different: [NOTES]
People I do not want to be confused with: [OTHERS IN MY SPACE]

Give me: 3 positioning statements ("I help [who] [do what] by [how]"), the strongest one with reasons, a one-line bio, a 50-word bio, and 3 content themes I could own.
positioningpersonal brandniche

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WORK / SKILLS], [AUDIENCE], [EXAMPLES], [NOTES], [OTHERS IN MY SPACE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
