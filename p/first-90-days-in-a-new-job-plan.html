First 90 Days in a New Job Plan

Creates a 30-60-90 day plan to learn fast, build relationships and score early wins.

ChatGPTCareer & Resume
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm starting as [ROLE] at [COMPANY TYPE] on [DATE]. My manager's top priority for this role: [PRIORITY, IF KNOWN].

Build a 30-60-90 day plan. For each phase: what to learn, who to meet (by role), one early win to aim for, and how I'll know I'm on track. Include a list of 15 smart questions to ask my manager and teammates in the first two weeks, and a simple weekly update format to send my manager. Keep it realistic for someone who's still learning the systems.
new jobonboarding90 day plan

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ROLE], [COMPANY TYPE], [DATE], [PRIORITY, IF KNOWN].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
