Generate a complete 4x4 content matrix for [CREATOR] in [NICHE]. The matrix has 4 content themes x 4 content angles x 4 formats x 4 audience outcomes. Themes: [LIST 4 CORE TOPICS]. Angles: educational, entertaining, inspirational, and controversial. Formats: short-form video, long-form written, carousel/slides, and live/podcast. Outcomes: new followers, engagement, trust-building, and conversions. Fill every cell of the matrix with a specific content idea. Then recommend the top 5 pieces to create first based on effort vs impact, and the single best piece to lead with this week.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CREATOR], [NICHE], [LIST 4 CORE TOPICS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
