Interior Decorator

Get interior design recommendations with color schemes, furniture placement and decor ideas.

ChatGPTBusiness & Strategy
64 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want you to act as an interior decorator. Tell me what kind of theme and design approach should be used for a room of my choice. Provide suggestions on color schemes, furniture placement and other decorative options that best suit the theme in order to enhance aesthetics and comfort within the space. My first request is: [YOUR ROOM TYPE AND ANY STYLE PREFERENCES]
interior designhome decorstyle

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR ROOM TYPE AND ANY STYLE PREFERENCES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
