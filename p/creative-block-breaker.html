Creative Block Breaker

Gets you unstuck on a creative project with constraints, exercises and a tiny next step.

ClaudeImage & Art
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I am stuck on a creative project.

Project: [WHAT YOU ARE MAKING]
Where I am stuck: [DESCRIBE]
What I have tried: [LIST]
How I feel about it right now: [HONESTLY]

Give me: 3 possible reasons I am stuck (not just "lack of inspiration"); 5 short exercises with constraints to get moving (under 20 minutes each); 3 different directions the project could take; and one tiny next step I can do today in 15 minutes.
creative blockinspirationart practice

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHAT YOU ARE MAKING], [DESCRIBE], [LIST], [HONESTLY].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
