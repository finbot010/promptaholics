Eulogy Writer

Helps you write a heartfelt eulogy from your memories, at the length you need.

ClaudeWriting & Content
96 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me write a eulogy for [NAME], my [RELATIONSHIP].

About them: [WHO THEY WERE, WHAT THEY LOVED, WORK, FAMILY]
Memories and stories: [2-4 STORIES OR MOMENTS]
Things people always said about them: [TRAITS, PHRASES]
Tone: [CELEBRATION OF LIFE / SOLEMN / SOME GENTLE HUMOR]
Length: [MINUTES TO SPEAK]
Faith or traditions to honor: [IF ANY]

Write it in my voice, with a warm opening, the stories woven into who they were, and a closing that leaves people with comfort. Keep sentences easy to say aloud. Then give me tips for delivering it when emotions run high.
eulogymemorialspeech

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAME], [RELATIONSHIP], [WHO THEY WERE, WHAT THEY LOVED, WORK, FAMILY], [2-4 STORIES OR MOMENTS], [TRAITS, PHRASES], [CELEBRATION OF LIFE / SOLEMN / SOME GENTLE HUMOR], [MINUTES TO SPEAK], [IF ANY] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
