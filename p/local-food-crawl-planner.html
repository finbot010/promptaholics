Local Food Crawl Planner

Plans a walking food crawl of local specialties near you using Google Maps.

GeminiTravel
85 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Plan a food crawl in [NEIGHBORHOOD / CITY].

Time: [e.g. Saturday afternoon, 4 hours]
Budget per person: [AMOUNT]
Dietary needs: [NONE / VEGETARIAN / ALLERGIES]
I want to try: [LOCAL SPECIALTIES or "the dishes this area is known for"]

Pick 5-6 stops within walking distance in a logical order, with what to order at each, opening hours, price level and walking time between stops. Add one backup for each stop in case it is closed or full, and tips on local food etiquette.
foodrestaurantsgoogle maps

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NEIGHBORHOOD / CITY], [AMOUNT], [NONE / VEGETARIAN / ALLERGIES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
