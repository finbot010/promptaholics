Action: Curate a comprehensive, stage-by-stage resource library for the complete learning path of [SUBJECT/SKILL]. Purpose: Ensure every phase of the learner journey from [START_LEVEL] to [END_LEVEL] has high-quality, vetted resources matched to the learner's style preference of [VISUAL/AUDITORY/READING/KINESTHETIC]. Execute: For each learning phase, recommend 2-3 resources per category: free online courses, books, practice platforms, community forums, and project ideas. Prioritize resources that are up-to-date as of [YEAR], beginner-friendly where applicable, and progressively increase in complexity. Output a table with columns: Phase, Resource Name, Type, Cost, Time Investment, and Why It Fits This Stage.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [SUBJECT/SKILL], [START_LEVEL], [END_LEVEL], [VISUAL/AUDITORY/READING/KINESTHETIC], [YEAR].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
