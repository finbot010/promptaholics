Difficult Conversation Email Drafter

Drafts a clear, respectful message for a hard conversation: saying no, setting a boundary or raising a problem.

ClaudeEmail, Sales & Communication
91 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me write a difficult message.

Situation: [e.g. turning down a client, telling a colleague their work is late again, raising a pay issue]
Recipient and relationship: [WHO]
What I need to say: [CORE MESSAGE]
What I want to happen next: [OUTCOME]
What I am worried about: [FEARS]

Write a message that is clear in the first two sentences, specific about the facts, respectful, and ends with a concrete next step. Give a firmer and a softer version. Then list phrases to avoid and how to respond if they react badly.
difficult conversationsworkplaceboundaries

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WHO], [CORE MESSAGE], [OUTCOME], [FEARS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
