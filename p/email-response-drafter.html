Email Response Drafter

Professional communications assistant drafts 3 email response options — short, standard, and comprehensive — with strategic notes on each approach.

ChatGPTAI Agents & Automation
93 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Act as my professional communications assistant. Here is an email I need to respond to:

[PASTE THE EMAIL]

My relationship with this person: [COLLEAGUE / CLIENT / BOSS / VENDOR / STRANGER]
My goal for this response: [WHAT I WANT TO ACHIEVE]
The tone I want: [PROFESSIONAL / WARM / FIRM / CONCISE / DETAILED]

Draft 3 different response options: (1) Short and direct — under 75 words, (2) Standard professional — 150-200 words, (3) Comprehensive — if the situation requires detail. For each, highlight the key strategic choice made in that version.
emailresponsecommunicationprofessionalagentwriting

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE THE EMAIL], [COLLEAGUE / CLIENT / BOSS / VENDOR / STRANGER], [WHAT I WANT TO ACHIEVE], [PROFESSIONAL / WARM / FIRM / CONCISE / DETAILED].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
