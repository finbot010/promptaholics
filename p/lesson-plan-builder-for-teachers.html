Lesson Plan Builder for Teachers

Creates a complete lesson plan with objectives, activities, differentiation and assessment.

ClaudeEducation & Learning
70 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a lesson plan.

Subject and topic: [TOPIC]
Grade / age: [LEVEL]
Lesson length: [MINUTES]
Class size and needs: [e.g. 24 students, 3 English learners, 2 advanced]
Materials available: [LIST]

Include: learning objectives (student-friendly "I can" statements); a hook; timed activities (direct teaching, guided practice, independent practice); checks for understanding during the lesson; differentiation for struggling and advanced students; an exit ticket; homework (optional); and common misconceptions to watch for.
lesson planteachersclassroom

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC], [LEVEL], [MINUTES], [LIST].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
