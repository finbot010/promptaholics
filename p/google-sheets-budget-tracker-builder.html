Google Sheets Budget Tracker Builder

Designs a Google Sheets budget tracker with formulas, categories and a monthly dashboard.

GeminiPersonal Finance & Investing
87 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Design a Google Sheets budget tracker for me.

Income sources: [LIST]
Spending categories I want: [LIST or "suggest them"]
I want to track: [e.g. monthly budget vs actual, savings goals, debt balances]

Give me the exact layout: tab names, column headers, and every formula (SUMIFS by category and month, budget vs actual, % used, savings progress). Add conditional formatting rules for overspending, a dashboard tab with the charts to insert, and data validation for categories. Write it so I can build it step by step in Sheets.
google sheetsbudgettracker

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST], [LIST or "suggest them"].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
