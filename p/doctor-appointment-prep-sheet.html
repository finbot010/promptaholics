Doctor Appointment Prep Sheet

Organizes your symptoms, history and questions into a one-page sheet so you get more from a short appointment.

ClaudeHealth & Wellness
109 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me prepare for a doctor's appointment. Do not diagnose me; help me communicate clearly.

Reason for visit: [MAIN CONCERN]
Symptoms (what, where, when they started, how often, what makes them better or worse): [DETAILS]
Medications and supplements: [LIST]
Relevant history: [CONDITIONS, SURGERIES, FAMILY HISTORY]
What I am most worried about: [CONCERN]

Create a one-page sheet: a 3-sentence summary I can read out first; a symptom timeline; my medication list; the 5 most important questions to ask (ranked, in case time runs out); and space to write the answers. Add a short list of warning signs that mean I should seek urgent care instead of waiting for the appointment.
doctor visitsymptomsquestions

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MAIN CONCERN], [DETAILS], [LIST], [CONDITIONS, SURGERIES, FAMILY HISTORY], [CONCERN].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
