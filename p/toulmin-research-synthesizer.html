I have completed initial research on [RESEARCH TOPIC] across [NUMBER] sources. Here are my key findings: [LIST MAIN FINDINGS]. Using the Toulmin Argument framework, help me synthesize this research: Claim - formulate the central argument about [RESEARCH TOPIC], Grounds - identify the strongest evidence from [FINDINGS], Warrant - explain why the evidence supports conclusions about [KEY CLAIM], Backing - cite additional support from [FIELD OR DISCIPLINE] norms, Rebuttal - acknowledge counterevidence regarding [CONTESTED ASPECTS], Qualifier - define the scope and limitations of [CONCLUSIONS]. Then identify the top 3 research gaps that future studies should address.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [RESEARCH TOPIC], [NUMBER], [LIST MAIN FINDINGS], [FINDINGS], [KEY CLAIM], [FIELD OR DISCIPLINE], [CONTESTED ASPECTS], [CONCLUSIONS] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
