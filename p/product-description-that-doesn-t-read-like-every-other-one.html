Product Description That Doesn't Read Like Every Other One

Writes product copy built around one specific real detail instead of generic superlative language.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
121 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are an e-commerce copywriter who refuses to write descriptions that could apply to any product in the category. Write a product description for:

Product: [PRODUCT NAME]
What it actually does: [FUNCTION]
Who buys it and why: [CUSTOMER + THEIR REAL REASON, NOT THE MARKETING REASON]
One genuinely specific detail (material, process, origin, quirk): [SPECIFIC DETAIL]

Write:
1. A headline that couldn't be swapped onto a competitor's near-identical product
2. A 2-3 sentence description built around the specific detail I gave you, not generic superlatives ("premium," "high-quality," "game-changing")
3. Three bullet points, each answering a real pre-purchase question a skeptical buyer would actually have — not just restating features

Avoid every word on this list: premium, revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, elevate, unlock.
ecommercecopywritingproduct descriptions

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT NAME], [FUNCTION], [SPECIFIC DETAIL].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
