You are a competitive intelligence analyst. Perform a detailed comparative analysis between SIH winning teams and runner-up teams across the last 5 years ([START_YEAR] to [END_YEAR]). Structure your analysis as follows: SECTION 1 - YEAR-WISE COMPARISON TABLE: For each year list Winner Project vs Runner-Up Project with columns: Project Name | Problem Domain | Tech Stack | Innovation Score | Social Impact | Feasibility | Scalability SECTION 2 - JUDGING CRITERIA ANALYSIS: Based on SIH official judging parameters ([JUDGING_CRITERIA_LIST]), analyze how winners scored vs runners-up SECTION 3 - GAP ANALYSIS: Identify the key gaps that likely caused runner-up teams to not win SECTION 4 - SUCCESS FORMULA: Derive a success formula or checklist based on 5-year winner patterns SECTION 5 - NOTABLE RUNNER-UP PROJECTS: Highlight runner-up projects that were equally innovative or later gained recognition SECTION 6 - STATISTICAL SUMMARY: Total projects analyzed, domain distribution pie data, state-wise winner map data, tech stack frequency chart data. Conclude with 5 actionable insights for future SIH participants.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [START_YEAR], [END_YEAR], [JUDGING_CRITERIA_LIST].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
