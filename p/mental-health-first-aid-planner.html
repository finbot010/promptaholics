Design a personalized mental health maintenance plan for someone with [DESCRIBE CHALLENGES].

Design a personalized mental health maintenance plan for someone with [DESCRIBE CHALLENGES]. Include: daily practices (5 minutes), weekly practices (30 minutes), monthly practices (deeper work), the early warning signs of deterioration specific to this pattern, and the intervention ladder from self-care to professional support.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE CHALLENGES].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
