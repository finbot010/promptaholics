Improv and Party Icebreaker Games

Suggests icebreakers and improv games for your group size and setting, with clear rules.

ChatGPTLifestyle, Fun & Games
77 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Give me icebreaker and improv games.

Setting: [WORK MEETING / CLASSROOM / BIRTHDAY / WEDDING / ONLINE CALL]
Group size: [NUMBER]
How well people know each other: [STRANGERS / SOME / CLOSE]
Time: [MINUTES]
Energy: [LOW-KEY / HIGH ENERGY]

Suggest 8 games with: how to explain it in one sentence, rules, time needed, and how to make shy people comfortable. Order them from easiest to most interactive. Avoid anything embarrassing or too personal for a work setting.
icebreakersimprovparty games

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER], [STRANGERS / SOME / CLOSE], [MINUTES], [LOW-KEY / HIGH ENERGY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
