Copyright and Fair Use Explainer

Explains whether your planned use of someone else's content is likely to be a copyright problem.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
108 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me understand the copyright risk of using this content. General information, not legal advice.

What I want to use: [e.g. a song clip, a photo from Google, a quote from a book, a movie scene]
How I will use it: [e.g. YouTube review, commercial ad, school project, merch]
How much of it: [LENGTH / PORTION]
Where I live and publish: [COUNTRY]

Explain: the rules that apply in my country (fair use, fair dealing or similar), how my use scores on the key factors, the realistic risks (takedowns, claims, lawsuits), safer alternatives (licensing, royalty-free sources, creating my own), and how to ask for permission with a short template.
copyrightfair usecontent creators

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LENGTH / PORTION], [COUNTRY].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
