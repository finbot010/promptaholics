Hotel Complaint Letter for a Refund

Writes a calm, evidence-based complaint to a hotel or booking site asking for a fair refund.

ClaudeTravel
109 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a complaint to [HOTEL / BOOKING SITE] about my stay.

Booking: [DATES, ROOM TYPE, PRICE, BOOKING NUMBER]
What went wrong: [e.g. dirty room, no hot water, noise, room not as advertised, overbooked]
What I did at the time: [WHO I TOLD, WHAT THEY SAID]
Evidence: [PHOTOS, MESSAGES, RECEIPTS]
What I'm asking for: [PARTIAL REFUND, FULL REFUND, POINTS, A FREE NIGHT]

Keep it short, factual and polite but firm. Lay out the problem, the impact on my stay, and a specific request with a reasonable deadline. Then write a shorter version for the booking site's message center, and a fair public review I could post if it isn't resolved.
hotel complaintrefundcustomer service

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [HOTEL / BOOKING SITE], [DATES, ROOM TYPE, PRICE, BOOKING NUMBER], [WHO I TOLD, WHAT THEY SAID], [PHOTOS, MESSAGES, RECEIPTS], [PARTIAL REFUND, FULL REFUND, POINTS, A FREE NIGHT].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
