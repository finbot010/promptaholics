Buy vs Rent Decision Analyzer

Compares buying and renting for your situation over time, with the assumptions shown so you can adjust them.

ClaudeReal Estate
111 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Compare buying vs renting for me. Educational, not financial advice. Show every assumption so I can change it.

Home price I am considering: [PRICE]
Down payment: [AMOUNT]
Mortgage rate and term: [RATE / YEARS]
Property tax, insurance, HOA: [AMOUNTS]
Current rent for similar home: [AMOUNT]
How long I expect to stay: [YEARS]
Expected home price growth and rent increases: [ASSUMPTIONS or "use moderate assumptions"]
Return I could earn investing the down payment: [ASSUMPTION]

Show a year-by-year comparison of total cost and net worth for buying vs renting, the break-even year, and how the answer changes if I stay 3 years less or 5 years more. List the non-financial factors to weigh.
buy vs renthousingdecision

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRICE], [AMOUNT], [RATE / YEARS], [AMOUNTS], [YEARS], [ASSUMPTIONS or "use moderate assumptions"], [ASSUMPTION].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
