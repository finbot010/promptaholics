Local Competitor Service Comparison

Compares your local competitors' services, prices and reviews using Google Maps and the web.

GeminiEmail, Sales & Communication
71 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Find businesses near [ADDRESS / AREA] that compete with mine.

My business: [TYPE, SERVICES, PRICE LEVEL]

For the top 6 competitors: rating and review count, services offered, prices if listed, opening hours, and the most common praise and complaints in their reviews. Then tell me: where I can stand out, which complaints I can turn into my selling points, and 3 quick changes to my own Google Business Profile.
competitorslocal businessgoogle maps

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ADDRESS / AREA], [TYPE, SERVICES, PRICE LEVEL].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
