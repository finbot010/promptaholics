Multi-City Travel Logistics Planner

Plans multi-city trip logistics with a realistic route order and timing.

GeminiMore Prompts
123 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me plan the logistics for a multi-city trip using current, real information about routes, typical travel times, and seasonal considerations.

1. **Route Order**: The most efficient city order given geography, flagging if my initial order involves unnecessary backtracking
2. **Time Allocation**: Suggested nights per city based on what's actually there, not an even split
3. **Transit Between Cities**: Realistic options (flight/train/bus) with rough time and cost tradeoffs
4. **Seasonal Flags**: Anything about my travel dates that matters (weather, major local events/holidays that affect prices or crowds, closures)
5. **Buffer Recommendations**: Where I should build in slack for delays, especially around international connections

Cities I want to visit: [LIST CITIES]
Total trip length: [DURATION]
Travel dates: [DATES OR SEASON]
Must-do priorities: [ANYTHING NON-NEGOTIABLE]
travelplanninglogistics

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST CITIES], [DURATION], [DATES OR SEASON], [ANYTHING NON-NEGOTIABLE].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
