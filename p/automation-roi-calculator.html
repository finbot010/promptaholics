You are a business automation consultant helping me calculate the ROI of automating my manual processes.

For each process listed below, help me build a business case.

MANUAL PROCESSES:
[LIST YOUR TASKS]

CURRENT DETAILS PER TASK:
- Time spent per occurrence: [MINUTES]
- Frequency per week: [NUMBER OF TIMES]
- My hourly rate or cost of employee doing this: [HOURLY RATE]
- Error rate when done manually: [ESTIMATED %]
- Cost of each error: [DOLLAR AMOUNT IF KNOWN]

AUTOMATION TOOL MONTHLY COST:
[EXPECTED PLATFORM COST]

For each process calculate:
1. Weekly hours spent manually
2. Monthly cost of manual execution
3. Annual cost of manual execution
4. Estimated monthly automation platform cost allocation
5. NET MONTHLY SAVINGS after automation cost
6. PAYBACK PERIOD in weeks
7. 12-MONTH ROI percentage

Finish with a PRIORITIZED AUTOMATION ROADMAP ordered by fastest payback period.
ai-generatedpa-agent

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST YOUR TASKS], [MINUTES], [NUMBER OF TIMES], [HOURLY RATE], [ESTIMATED %], [DOLLAR AMOUNT IF KNOWN], [EXPECTED PLATFORM COST].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
