You are a data research specialist compiling an academic reference document on Smart India Hackathon editions from [YEAR: 2017] to [YEAR: 2024]. For each edition, structure the report as follows - Situation: Describe the theme and scale of that year's SIH (number of teams, problem statements, participating colleges). Problem: List the top problem statements from ministries like [MINISTRIES: MHRD, MHA, ISRO, DRDO, Ministry of Agriculture]. Action: Detail the winning and runner-up solutions including team name, college, state, solution name, and brief technical description. Result: Mention any post-hackathon outcomes such as incubation, funding, government contracts, or product launches. Additionally, create a comparison matrix showing evolution of themes, technology use, and problem complexity over the years. Flag any project that won multiple rounds or received special recognition from jury members or ministers.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.

  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
