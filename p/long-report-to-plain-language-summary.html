Long Report to Plain-Language Summary

Turns a long, technical document into a plain-language summary anyone can understand.

ClaudeWriting & Content
74 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Summarize this document in plain language.

Audience: [e.g. general public, customers, parents, non-technical managers]
Reading level: [e.g. grade 8]
Length: [WORDS]
Document:
[PASTE]

Give me: the main point in 2 sentences; the key findings or changes as short bullets; what it means for the reader; any actions they need to take; and a glossary for terms I cannot avoid. Keep numbers accurate and flag anything in the original that is ambiguous instead of guessing.
summariesplain languagereports

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WORDS], [PASTE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
