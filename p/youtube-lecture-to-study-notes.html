YouTube Lecture to Study Notes

Uses Gemini's YouTube access to turn a lecture video into structured notes with timestamps and quiz questions.

GeminiEducation & Learning
80 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@YouTube Take this lecture video and turn it into study notes: [PASTE YOUTUBE LINK]

My course and level: [COURSE / LEVEL]

Give me:
1. A summary in 5 bullet points.
2. Structured notes by section, with timestamps so I can jump back.
3. Key terms with definitions as used in the video.
4. Any formulas, examples or diagrams described, written out clearly.
5. 8 quiz questions with answers.
6. Anything the video states that I should double-check against my textbook.
youtubenotesstudy

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE YOUTUBE LINK], [COURSE / LEVEL].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
