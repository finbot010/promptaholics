Moving Day Planner From Gmail and Calendar

Pulls your moving dates and bookings from Gmail and builds a week-by-week moving plan in Google Calendar.

GeminiReal Estate
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Gmail Find my emails about my upcoming move (lease, movers, utilities, internet, storage) and @Google Calendar help me plan it.

Move date: [DATE]
From: [CITY/ADDRESS AREA] To: [CITY/ADDRESS AREA]
Household: [PEOPLE, PETS]

1. Summarize what is already booked from my emails (company, date, time, confirmation details).
2. List what is not booked yet.
3. Create a week-by-week plan from 6 weeks out to 1 week after the move, including address changes, utilities, packing by room, and deposits.
4. Suggest calendar reminders with dates for each deadline.
movingchecklistgoogle calendar

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DATE], [CITY/ADDRESS AREA], [PEOPLE, PETS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
