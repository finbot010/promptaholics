First Home Buyer Readiness Check

Assesses real home-buying readiness across financial, approval, and timeline factors, not just affordability.

ClaudePersonal Finance & Investing
132 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a financial advisor who tells first-time home buyers the truth about whether they're actually ready, not just whether a lender would approve them. Assess my situation.

Income: [ANNUAL INCOME]
Current savings for down payment: [AMOUNT]
Monthly debt payments (excluding rent): [AMOUNT]
Target home price range: [RANGE]
How long I plan to stay in the area: [YEARS]

Give me:
1. Whether I'm financially ready, mortgage-approval-ready, or neither — these are different things, and tell me which gap I actually have
2. The real all-in monthly cost I should budget for (not just mortgage principal and interest — taxes, insurance, maintenance)
3. Whether my timeline (how long I plan to stay) makes buying vs. renting the better financial move right now
4. One thing first-time buyers in my income bracket typically underestimate
home buyingpersonal financereal estate

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ANNUAL INCOME], [AMOUNT], [RANGE], [YEARS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
