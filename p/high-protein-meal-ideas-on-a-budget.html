High-Protein Meal Ideas on a Budget

Gives cheap, high-protein meal ideas with simple recipes and a shopping list.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
72 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Give me high-protein meal ideas on a budget.

Diet: [OMNIVORE / VEGETARIAN / VEGAN / PESCATARIAN]
Budget per day for food: [AMOUNT]
Cooking time: [MINUTES PER DAY]
Foods I dislike: [LIST]

Give me 10 meals with approximate protein per serving and cost, simple recipes, 5 high-protein snacks, a shopping list of the cheapest protein sources, and batch-cooking tips. If I have a medical condition, I will check with a doctor or dietitian.
high proteinbudget mealsfitness food

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [OMNIVORE / VEGETARIAN / VEGAN / PESCATARIAN], [AMOUNT], [MINUTES PER DAY], [LIST].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
