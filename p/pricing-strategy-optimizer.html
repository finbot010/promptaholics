Pricing Strategy Optimizer

Comprehensive pricing strategy analysis that helps businesses charge what they are worth.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me optimize my pricing strategy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE].

Current price: [PRICE]
Target customer: [CUSTOMER]
Main competitor pricing: [COMPETITORS]
My unique value: [VALUE PROP]

Provide:
1. Psychological pricing analysis (is my current price working against me?)
2. Three alternative pricing models with pros/cons
3. How to justify a higher price without losing customers
4. Upsell and bundle opportunities I am missing
5. The exact language to use when a customer says 'that is too expensive'
6. What data I should track to know when to raise prices
pricingbusinessrevenuestrategy

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT/SERVICE], [PRICE], [CUSTOMER], [COMPETITORS], [VALUE PROP].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
