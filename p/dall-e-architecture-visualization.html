Write DALL-E 3 prompts for architectural visualization of: [PASTE BUILDING TYPE AND STYLE]. Create 4 prompts: 1) Exterior day shot ('Architectural photography of [building], [style: modernist/brutalist/art deco/biophilic], natural daylight, [materials], photorealistic, --ar 16:9'), 2) Exterior night with lighting, 3) Interior common space, 4) Detail/texture close-up.
