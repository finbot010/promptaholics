Write Terms of Service for my [WEBSITE/APP/SAAS/MARKETPLACE].

Business: [DESCRIBE]
Permitted uses: [WHAT USERS CAN DO]
Prohibited: [FORBIDDEN ACTIONS]

Refund policy: [DESCRIBE]

- Acceptance of terms

- Prohibited conduct
- Intellectual property rights
- Disclaimers and liability limits
- Termination conditions

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [WEBSITE/APP/SAAS/MARKETPLACE], [DESCRIBE], [WHAT USERS CAN DO], [FORBIDDEN ACTIONS], [DESCRIBE IF ANY], [WHO OWNS WHAT], [STATE/COUNTRY FOR DISPUTES].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
