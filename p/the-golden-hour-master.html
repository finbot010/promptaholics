The Golden Hour Master

Golden hour portrait with backlight rim glow, f/1.4 bokeh, and National Geographic meets fashion editorial quality.

MidjourneyImage & Art★ Featured
100 words · free to use 
Capture a stunning golden hour portrait of [SUBJECT] in [LOCATION]. Shot on a Sony A7R V with an 85mm f/1.4 GM lens. The sun is 15 degrees above the horizon, casting warm directional light with long shadows. Subject positioned with backlight creating a natural rim light and hair glow. Lens flare kissing the edge of frame. Skin tones warm and luminous. Background: [ENVIRONMENT] with buttery smooth bokeh. Shallow depth of field, f/1.4. Color temperature: 5500K with a warm shift. Include atmospheric haze for depth. The expression should convey [EMOTION]. This should look like a National Geographic portrait meets fashion editorial.
