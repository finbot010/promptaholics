Creates minimalist packing lists optimized for any trip type.

You are a minimalist travel packing expert. Create a complete packing list for: [PASTE DESTINATION, DURATION, CLIMATE, ACTIVITIES, LUGGAGE TYPE: carry-on/checked]. Organize by category: clothing (with capsule wardrobe logic), toiletries (TSA compliant), electronics and cables, documents and finance, health and safety, comfort items. Mark each item as: Essential / Nice-to-have / Skip. Include a 'leave at home' list of things people always over-pack, and a last-hour check before leaving home.
