Socratic Strategy Challenger

Stress-tests a strategic plan like a sharp board member, not a cheerleader.

ClaudeAI Agents & Automation
139 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm going to describe a strategic decision I'm leaning toward making. Your job is NOT to validate it — it's to stress-test it like a sharp board member who wants the company to succeed but isn't afraid to ask uncomfortable questions.

For the plan I describe:
1. Identify the 3 biggest unstated assumptions I'm making
2. Ask the single hardest question I haven't answered
3. Describe the most likely way this plan fails, concretely — not "it might not work" but the specific failure mode
4. Steelman the option I've already dismissed — what's the strongest case FOR the path I'm not taking?
5. If you genuinely think the plan is sound after this analysis, say so plainly — don't manufacture doubt for its own sake

Here's my situation and the decision I'm considering:
