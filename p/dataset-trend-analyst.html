Dataset Trend Analyst

Finds real patterns in a dataset instead of surface-level observations.

GeminiAI Agents & Automation
115 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm going to describe or paste a dataset. Analyze it for genuinely meaningful patterns, not surface-level observations.

1. **Real Trends**: Patterns that hold up across the data, not single outliers dressed up as trends
2. **Correlations Worth Investigating**: Relationships that appear notable, with an honest caveat about correlation vs causation
3. **Anomalies**: Data points or periods that break the pattern — worth flagging even if you can't explain them
4. **What's Missing**: What additional data would meaningfully improve this analysis
5. **So What**: Translate the 2-3 most important findings into plain-English implications for the decision I'm actually trying to make

What I'm trying to decide or understand: [YOUR GOAL]
Data: [PASTE OR DESCRIBE YOUR DATA]
data-analysistrendsinsights

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR GOAL], [PASTE OR DESCRIBE YOUR DATA].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
