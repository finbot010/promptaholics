Investment Portfolio Starter

Educational guide to building a beginner investment portfolio from scratch.

ClaudePersonal Finance & Investing
98 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a financial educator (note: this is educational, not personalized financial advice). Help me understand how to build a starter investment portfolio. My age: [AGE]. Monthly amount to invest: [AMOUNT]. Time horizon: [WHEN DO YOU NEED THIS MONEY]. Risk tolerance: [CONSERVATIVE / MODERATE / AGGRESSIVE]. Current accounts: [401K, IRA, BROKERAGE]. Educate me on: 1) Account priority order and why, 2) A simple 3-fund portfolio in plain language, 3) What expense ratios to look for, 4) How to automate contributions, 5) Common beginner mistakes, 6) How to rebalance and how often. Consult a licensed advisor for personalized recommendations.
investingportfolioindex fundspersonal finance

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AGE], [AMOUNT], [WHEN DO YOU NEED THIS MONEY], [CONSERVATIVE / MODERATE / AGGRESSIVE], [401K, IRA, BROKERAGE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
