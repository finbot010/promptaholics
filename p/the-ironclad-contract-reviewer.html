The Ironclad Contract Reviewer

Contract review with green/yellow/red risk ratings, worst-case scenarios, and counter-language for every clause.

ChatGPTLegal & Admin★ Featured
119 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Review this contract/agreement and analyze it from my perspective as the [YOUR ROLE — freelancer / vendor / tenant / employee / buyer]: [PASTE CONTRACT OR DESCRIBE KEY TERMS]. For each major clause, provide: (1) Plain English Translation — what this actually means in real-world terms, (2) Risk Level — green (standard), yellow (worth negotiating), or red (potentially dangerous), (3) What Could Go Wrong — the worst-case scenario this clause enables, (4) Suggested Counter-Language — how I could revise it to better protect my interests. Flag any missing protections I should request be added. End with a 'Top 3 Non-Negotiables' — the clauses I should push back on hardest before signing. Note: Review with a licensed attorney before signing.
contract reviewlegal analysisred flagsnegotiationterms

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE CONTRACT OR DESCRIBE KEY TERMS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
