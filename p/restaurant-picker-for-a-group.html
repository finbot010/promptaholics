Restaurant Picker for a Group

Finds restaurants that work for everyone in a group: location, budget, diets and booking.

GeminiFood & Cooking
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Find a restaurant for a group dinner.

Area: [NEIGHBORHOOD / CITY]
Date and time: [WHEN]
Group size: [NUMBER]
Budget per person: [AMOUNT]
Dietary needs: [e.g. 1 vegan, 1 gluten-free, 1 nut allergy]
Vibe: [QUIET / LIVELY / KID-FRIENDLY / CELEBRATION]

Give me 5 options with cuisine, price level, rating, distance, whether they take reservations for my group size, and how well they handle each dietary need based on menus and reviews. Recommend one and draft a short message to the group with the plan.
restaurantsgroup dinnergoogle maps

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NEIGHBORHOOD / CITY], [WHEN], [NUMBER], [AMOUNT], [QUIET / LIVELY / KID-FRIENDLY / CELEBRATION].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
