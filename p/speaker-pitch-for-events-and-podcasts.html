Speaker Pitch for Events and Podcasts

Writes a pitch to get booked as a podcast guest or event speaker.

GeminiMarketing & Branding
83 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a pitch to be a guest on [PODCAST OR EVENT NAME].

About the show or event: [AUDIENCE, RECENT EPISODES OR THEMES]
My expertise: [WHAT I KNOW]
3 talk or episode ideas: [OR "suggest them"]
Proof I'm worth booking: [RESULTS, PAST TALKS, AUDIENCE SIZE]

Give me a subject line and a pitch email under 150 words that shows I know the show, 3 specific episode titles with what listeners will learn, and a short follow-up message. Make it about their audience, not me.
podcast guestspeakingpitch

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PODCAST OR EVENT NAME], [AUDIENCE, RECENT EPISODES OR THEMES], [WHAT I KNOW], [OR "suggest them"], [RESULTS, PAST TALKS, AUDIENCE SIZE].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
