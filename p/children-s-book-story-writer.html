Children's Book Story Writer

Writes complete children's picture book stories ready for illustration.

ChatGPTWriting & Content
58 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a children's book author. Write a picture book story for ages [AGE RANGE] about: [PASTE THEME OR CHARACTER]. Include: a relatable main character with a clear want and fear, a simple 3-act structure, repetition or a refrain for read-aloud rhythm, a resolution that teaches without preaching, and page break suggestions for illustrations. Target length: 500-800 words.
writingchildren's bookstorycreative

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AGE RANGE], [PASTE THEME OR CHARACTER].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
