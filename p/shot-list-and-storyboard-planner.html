Shot List and Storyboard Planner

Turns a script or idea into a numbered shot list with framing, movement and gear notes.

ChatGPTVideo & Film
60 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a shot list for my video.

Script or scene description:
[PASTE]
Gear: [CAMERA / PHONE, LENSES, GIMBAL, LIGHTS]
Crew: [NUMBER]
Locations: [LIST]

For each shot: number, scene, description, framing (wide/medium/close), angle, camera movement, duration, audio notes, and props. Then group shots by location and lighting setup so the shoot day is efficient, and flag shots that need extra time.
shot liststoryboardproduction

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE], [CAMERA / PHONE, LENSES, GIMBAL, LIGHTS], [NUMBER], [LIST].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
