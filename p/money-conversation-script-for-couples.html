Money Conversation Script for Couples

Gives couples a calm, structured script for talking about money, debt and shared goals.

ChatGPTPersonal Finance & Investing
89 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help my partner and me have a productive money conversation.

Our situation: [LIVING TOGETHER / MARRIED / ENGAGED]
Topics we need to discuss: [e.g. debt, splitting bills, saving for a house, different spending habits]
What usually goes wrong when we talk about money: [DESCRIBE]

Give us: a structure for a 45-minute conversation; opening questions that are not blaming; a fair way to compare income, debts and goals; 3 ways to split shared costs with pros and cons; a simple shared-goals worksheet; and phrases to use when it gets tense.
couplesmoney talkrelationships

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIVING TOGETHER / MARRIED / ENGAGED], [DESCRIBE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
