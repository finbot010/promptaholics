Tabletop RPG Session Prep

Preps a tabletop RPG session: scenes, NPCs, encounters and improvisation backups.

ClaudeLifestyle, Fun & Games
88 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me prep my tabletop RPG session.

System: [e.g. D&D 5e, Pathfinder, homebrew]
Players and characters: [LIST WITH LEVELS AND GOALS]
Where we left off: [SUMMARY]
Session length: [HOURS]
What I want this session to feel like: [TENSE / FUNNY / EMOTIONAL / EXPLORATION]

Give me: a strong opening scene; 3-5 flexible scenes (not a railroad); NPCs with a voice note, want and secret; one combat encounter and one non-combat challenge; a moment tied to each player's goal; and what to do if players go completely off track.
D&Dtabletop RPGgame master

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST WITH LEVELS AND GOALS], [SUMMARY], [HOURS], [TENSE / FUNNY / EMOTIONAL / EXPLORATION].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
