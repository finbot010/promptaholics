Retirement Account Contribution Optimizer

Explains general retirement contribution priority order and traditional-vs-Roth tradeoffs, with clear limits on personalized advice.

ChatGPTAI Agents & Automation
158 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a financial educator explaining retirement account strategy in general terms — not personalized financial advice, since account specifics and tax situations vary. Help me think through my contribution strategy.

Accounts available to me: [401K WITH MATCH / ROTH IRA / TRADITIONAL IRA / ETC.]
Employer match details, if any: [MATCH PERCENTAGE AND LIMIT]
Rough tax bracket: [CURRENT BRACKET]
Whether I expect to be in a higher or lower bracket in retirement: [YOUR GUESS]

Give me:
1. The general order of priority for contributions across the accounts I have available (this is genuinely well-established general guidance, e.g., capturing an employer match first)
2. The general tradeoff between traditional (pre-tax) and Roth (after-tax) contributions given my stated tax expectations
3. What I should NOT do (common mistakes people make with account order or contribution timing)
4. A clear note that the specific numbers should be confirmed with a financial advisor or tax professional given my actual full situation
retirement401kpersonal finance

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MATCH PERCENTAGE AND LIMIT], [CURRENT BRACKET], [YOUR GUESS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
