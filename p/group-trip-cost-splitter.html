Group Trip Cost Splitter

Sets up a fair way to split shared trip costs and a Google Sheet to track who owes whom.

GeminiTravel
84 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm organizing a group trip and need to split costs fairly.

People: [NAMES]
Shared costs so far: [ITEM, AMOUNT, WHO PAID]
Complications: [e.g. some stay fewer nights, couples share a room, someone doesn't drink]

Suggest a fair splitting method for each type of cost, then build a Google Sheets layout with formulas that show each person's share, what they've paid and the final "who pays whom" settlement in the fewest transfers. Add a short friendly message I can send the group explaining the system.
group travelsplitting costsgoogle sheets

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NAMES], [ITEM, AMOUNT, WHO PAID].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
