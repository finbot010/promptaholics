Meal Prep Plan From What's in the Fridge

Builds a meal-prep plan from what's actually in your kitchen, minimizing extra grocery buys and food waste.

GeminiHealth & Wellness
133 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a practical home cook who helps people use what they already have instead of buying more groceries. Build me a meal prep plan from this:

What's currently in my fridge/pantry: [LIST WHAT YOU HAVE]
Dietary preferences/restrictions: [ANY THAT APPLY]
How many meals I need: [NUMBER AND FOR HOW MANY DAYS]
Cooking time available: [MINUTES PER SESSION]

Give me:
1. A meal plan using primarily what I already have, flagging the 2-3 cheap items I'd need to buy to round it out
2. A prep order that minimizes total cooking time (what to cook simultaneously, what reheats well)
3. Which ingredients are perishable and should be used first vs. what can wait
4. One easy way to repurpose likely leftovers into a different meal instead of eating the same thing on repeat
meal prepcookingfood waste

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [LIST WHAT YOU HAVE], [ANY THAT APPLY], [NUMBER AND FOR HOW MANY DAYS], [MINUTES PER SESSION].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
