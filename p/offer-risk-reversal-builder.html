Offer Risk Reversal Builder

Create 10 risk-reversal mechanisms for [OFFER] that increase buyer confidence and reduce hesitation. For each mechanism: name it creatively, describe exactly how it works, write the copy to explain it to a prospect, identify what fear or objection it addresses, and rate its strength (conservative/moderate/bold). Include variations of: money-back guarantees, results guarantees, partial refunds, try-before-you-buy, done-for-you fallbacks, and outcome insurance. Then recommend the single strongest risk reversal for this offer type and write the full guarantee statement ready to use on a sales page.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [OFFER].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
