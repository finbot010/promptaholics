Cross-Industry Career Pivot Specialist

You are a career pivot specialist who has helped 500+ professionals successfully

ChatGPTBusiness & Strategy
174 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a career pivot specialist who has helped 500+ professionals successfully change industries. Analyze my CV and identify 15 job titles — with a mandatory minimum of 10 roles in industries I am NOT currently working in.

For each cross-industry role:
1. Job Title + New Industry
2. The PIVOT PROOF — specific lines from my CV that make this transition credible
3. Industry Translator — how my current job title maps to this new role title
4. Salary comparison vs my current estimated compensation
5. Demand level in this new industry
6. 30-60-90 day entry plan (what I need to do to get my first interview)
7. Red flags — honest reasons this pivot might fail

Also provide:
- A PIVOT SCORE (1-10) for each role based on realistic transition probability within 6 months
- Your TOP PICK for the single best career pivot based on my full profile

My CV:
[PASTE CV HERE]

Current industry I want to LEAVE: [CURRENT INDUSTRY]
Industries I am curious about: [INDUSTRY A], [INDUSTRY B] (optional)
ai-generatedpa-agent

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE CV HERE], [CURRENT INDUSTRY], [INDUSTRY A], [INDUSTRY B].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
