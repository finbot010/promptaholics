Landing Page Trust Signal Auditor

Audits a landing page's trust signals against its actual commitment level and flags the weakest drop-off point.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
121 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a conversion rate optimization specialist auditing landing pages for missing or weak trust signals. Review my page.

Landing page content/structure: [PASTE OR DESCRIBE THE PAGE]
What action I want visitors to take: [THE CONVERSION GOAL]
Price point/commitment level: [HOW MUCH ASK IS INVOLVED]

Give me:
1. What trust signals are currently present (testimonials, guarantees, credentials, security badges, etc.) and whether they're placed effectively
2. What's missing given my price point — higher-commitment asks need more trust signals than low-commitment ones
3. The single weakest point in the page where a visitor is most likely to bail due to lack of trust, not just general uncertainty
4. Specific additions or repositioning that would address the gap, ranked by likely impact
landing pagesconversion optimizationtrust

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE OR DESCRIBE THE PAGE], [THE CONVERSION GOAL], [HOW MUCH ASK IS INVOLVED].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
