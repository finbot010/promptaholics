Salary Negotiation Script

Complete salary negotiation script with responses to every common pushback.

ClaudePersonal Finance & Investing
109 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a career coach and salary negotiation expert. Help me negotiate a higher salary. Current salary or offer: [AMOUNT]. Market rate for my role: [RESEARCH AT GLASSDOOR OR LEVELS.FYI]. My key achievements: [LIST 3-5 QUANTIFIED RESULTS]. Write: 1) The exact script when the offer comes in (never accept on the spot), 2) How to counter with a specific number and the reasoning, 3) Responses to the top 5 pushbacks (that is our budget, everyone gets the same, we cannot go higher), 4) How to negotiate non-salary benefits if they will not move on base, 5) The exact words to close gracefully. Role-play the hardest part of the conversation.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AMOUNT], [RESEARCH AT GLASSDOOR OR LEVELS.FYI], [LIST 3-5 QUANTIFIED RESULTS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
