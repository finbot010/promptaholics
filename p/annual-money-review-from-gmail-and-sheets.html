Annual Money Review From Gmail and Sheets

Pulls receipts and statements from Gmail into a yearly money review with what changed and what to fix.

GeminiPersonal Finance & Investing
80 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Gmail Find receipts, bills, subscription renewals and statement notifications from [START DATE] to [END DATE].

My spending categories (or "suggest them"): [CATEGORIES]

Summarize my year: total spending by category (estimate where only partial data exists), biggest one-time purchases, recurring costs that went up, subscriptions I added, and bills that changed. Format it for Google Sheets. Then give me 5 specific changes for next year and a simple monthly check-in routine. Mark which numbers are incomplete because receipts may be missing.
annual reviewbudgetgmail

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [START DATE], [END DATE], [CATEGORIES].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
