Small Claims Court Prep

Complete small claims court prep — filing, evidence, scripts, and strategy.

ChatGPTLegal & Admin
94 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me prepare for small claims court.

My claim: [DESCRIBE — contractor didn't finish, seller won't refund, tenant damage]
Amount: $[AMOUNT] | State: [STATE]
Evidence I have: [LIST]
Defendant's likely argument: [WHAT THEY WILL SAY]

Provide:
1. Whether small claims is the right venue
2. Maximum claim limit in my state
3. How to file step by step
4. How to organize and present evidence
5. Simple opening statement script
6. How to counter their defense
7. What to bring on the day
8. What to do if I win but they don't pay
small claimscourtdisputelegalmoney

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AMOUNT], [STATE], [LIST], [WHAT THEY WILL SAY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
