Freelance Service Agreement Drafter

Drafts a clear freelance service agreement with scope, payment terms, revisions, IP and termination you can adapt.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
138 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Draft a freelance service agreement I can adapt. Mark it clearly as a template that should be reviewed for my jurisdiction.

Me: [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS], [COUNTRY/STATE]
Client: [CLIENT NAME]
Service: [WHAT YOU WILL DELIVER]
Price and structure: [FIXED / HOURLY / RETAINER — AMOUNTS]
Deposit: [e.g. 50% upfront]
Timeline: [START, MILESTONES, DEADLINE]
Revisions included: [NUMBER]
Who owns the work and when: [e.g. client owns final files after full payment]

Include sections for: scope and out-of-scope work, deliverables, payment schedule and late fees, revision limits and how extra work is billed, client responsibilities (feedback deadlines, assets), IP transfer on full payment and my right to show the work in a portfolio, confidentiality, termination by either side with kill fee, limitation of liability, and signatures.

After the draft, list the 5 clauses I should fill in carefully and why.
freelancecontracttemplate

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS], [COUNTRY/STATE], [CLIENT NAME], [WHAT YOU WILL DELIVER], [FIXED / HOURLY / RETAINER — AMOUNTS], [START, MILESTONES, DEADLINE], [NUMBER].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
