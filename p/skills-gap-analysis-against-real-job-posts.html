Skills Gap Analysis Against Real Job Posts

Compares your skills against current job postings for your target role and plans what to learn first.

GeminiCareer & Resume
81 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Search current job postings for [TARGET ROLE] in [LOCATION / REMOTE] and compare them with my skills.

My skills and experience: [LIST OR PASTE RESUME]

Tell me: the 10 skills and tools mentioned most often in current postings (with roughly how often); which I have, which I partly have, and which I lack; the 3 gaps that matter most; the fastest credible way to close each (project, course, certificate); and how to word my existing experience to match the postings honestly.
skills gapjob marketupskilling

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TARGET ROLE], [LOCATION / REMOTE], [LIST OR PASTE RESUME].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
