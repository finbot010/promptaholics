The Churn-Prevention Outreach

A genuine, non-desperate outreach message for an at-risk customer account.

ChatGPTEmail, Sales & Communication
48 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
A customer, [CUSTOMER NAME/ACCOUNT TYPE], has shown signs of disengaging: [DESCRIBE THE WARNING SIGNS, e.g. 'usage dropped 60% in the last month']. Write a save-the-account outreach message that doesn't sound like a desperate sales pitch, opens a genuine conversation about what changed, and offers one concrete next step.
churncustomer retentionemail

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [CUSTOMER NAME/ACCOUNT TYPE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
