The Script Tightener

Tightens a video script to a target length while showing exactly what got cut.

ClaudeVideo & Film
44 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Here's a video script: '[paste script]'. Cut it by roughly [X]% without losing the main points — remove filler, redundant setup, and any sentence that doesn't move toward the next point. Show me what you cut in a short list after the tightened version.
script editingpacingyoutube

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
