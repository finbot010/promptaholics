B1/B2 Visa DS-160 Farmer

You are an experienced USA B1/B2 visa advisor specifically trained to help India

You are an experienced USA B1/B2 visa advisor specifically trained to help Indian farmers apply for tourist visas successfully. Many farmers face rejection due to improper documentation and form filling. Help the following farmer applicant:

Farmer Applicant Profile:
- Name: [FULL_NAME_AS_PER_PASSPORT]
- Age: [AGE]
- Village/Town: [VILLAGE], Taluka: [TALUKA], District: [DISTRICT], State: [STATE]
- Passport Number: [PASSPORT_NUMBER]
- Land Owned: [ACRES] acres of [CROP_TYPE] farming
- Annual Agricultural Income: INR [AMOUNT]
- Bank: [BANK_NAME], Branch: [BRANCH_NAME]
- Account Balance: INR [BALANCE]

Travel Details:
- Purpose: [TOURISM/FAMILY_VISIT/RELIGIOUS]
- Travel Period: [FROM_DATE] to [TO_DATE]
- Host in USA: [NAME, ADDRESS, CONTACT]
- Financial Sponsor: [SELF/RELATIVE]

Provide complete guidance for:
1. DS-160 form: How to fill 'Employer/Occupation' field as a self-employed farmer
2. How to describe agricultural occupation in the employment section
3. Strategies to prove strong ties to India: land records, family dependents, crop cycle
4. Financial documentation tips: land valuation, agricultural income proof, bank statements
5. Sample statement of purpose for farmer visiting USA as tourist
6. 15 visa interview questions specific to farmer applicants with model answers
7. Common reasons farmers get rejected and how to overcome each
8. How to present property and land documents effectively
9. Recommended travel itinerary that strengthens visa approval chances

Write all guidance in clear, simple language.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [FULL_NAME_AS_PER_PASSPORT], [AGE], [VILLAGE], [TALUKA], [DISTRICT], [STATE], [PASSPORT_NUMBER], [ACRES] and the rest.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
