Wedding Budget Breakdown

Splits a wedding budget into categories and shows where to save without losing what matters.

ClaudePersonal Finance & Investing
80 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Build our wedding budget.

Total budget: [AMOUNT]
Guests: [NUMBER]
Location: [CITY / DESTINATION]
Style: [e.g. backyard, beach, ballroom, small and intimate]
Our top 3 priorities: [e.g. food, photos, music]
Who's contributing: [US / FAMILY — AMOUNTS]

Give me a category-by-category budget table (venue, food, drinks, photo/video, attire, music, flowers, stationery, rings, favors, tips, buffer), with more money going to our priorities. List the hidden costs couples often miss, 10 ways to save on the low-priority items, and a payment timeline.
wedding budgetwedding planningcosts

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AMOUNT], [NUMBER], [CITY / DESTINATION], [US / FAMILY — AMOUNTS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
