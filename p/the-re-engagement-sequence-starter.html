The Re-Engagement Sequence Starter

Builds a 3-part win-back sequence instead of a single generic 're-engagement' blast.

ClaudeEmail, Sales & Communication
51 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Draft a 3-email re-engagement sequence for subscribers who haven't opened anything from [brand/newsletter] in 60+ days. Email 1: remind them why they signed up. Email 2: give something genuinely useful with no ask. Email 3: a direct 'still want to hear from us?' with a one-click way to confirm or unsubscribe.
