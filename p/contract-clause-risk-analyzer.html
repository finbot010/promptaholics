Contract Clause Risk Analyzer

Analyzes a contract clause by clause and flags what to negotiate before you sign.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
143 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a contract review specialist. I'm pasting a contract below. Analyze it clause by clause and produce:

1. **Risk Summary Table** — for each substantive clause: what it says (plain English), who it favors, and a risk rating (Low/Medium/High) for the party I represent: [MY ROLE - e.g. the vendor/the client/the employer]
2. **Red Flags** — any clauses that are unusual, one-sided, or missing standard protections (e.g. no limitation of liability, unbounded indemnification, auto-renewal without notice)
3. **Negotiation Priorities** — rank the top 5 clauses I should push back on, with a specific suggested redline for each
4. **Plain-English Summary** — a 3-paragraph summary a non-lawyer on my team could read in 2 minutes

Do not provide formal legal advice or claim to replace an attorney — flag clearly where I should get real legal review before signing.

Contract:
[PASTE CONTRACT TEXT]

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MY ROLE - e.g. the vendor/the client/the employer], [PASTE CONTRACT TEXT].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
