Recruiter

Get recruiting strategies or job search advice from an experienced recruiter perspective.

ChatGPTBusiness & Strategy
61 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I want you to act as a recruiter. I will provide some information about job openings or my career situation, and it will be your job to come up with strategies for sourcing qualified applicants or improving my candidacy. This could include social media outreach, networking events, or career fair strategies. My first request is: [YOUR RECRUITING OR JOB SEARCH SITUATION]
recruitinghiringHR

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR RECRUITING OR JOB SEARCH SITUATION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
