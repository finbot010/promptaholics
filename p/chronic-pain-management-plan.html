Chronic Pain Management Plan

Holistic chronic pain management covering movement, diet, mind-body, and doctor communication.

98 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me build a chronic pain management approach.

Type of pain: [BACK/JOINT/NERVE/FIBROMYALGIA/HEADACHES]
Duration: [HOW LONG] | Pain average: [1-10]
Current treatments: [MEDICATIONS/PHYSICAL THERAPY]
What helps: [ANY RELIEF STRATEGIES]
Impact on life: [WORK/SLEEP/RELATIONSHIPS/MOBILITY]

Provide a holistic plan:
1. Evidence-based non-medication approaches
2. Movement and exercise modifications (stay active safely)
3. Sleep strategies specific to pain sufferers
4. Diet and anti-inflammatory approaches
5. Mind-body techniques with evidence for pain
6. How to talk to doctors about pain effectively
7. Pacing strategies to avoid boom-bust cycles
8. Mental health strategies for chronic pain

Note: Consult your healthcare provider before changing treatment.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [BACK/JOINT/NERVE/FIBROMYALGIA/HEADACHES], [HOW LONG], [1-10], [MEDICATIONS/PHYSICAL THERAPY], [ANY RELIEF STRATEGIES], [WORK/SLEEP/RELATIONSHIPS/MOBILITY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
