Systems Root-Cause Analyzer

Digs past symptoms to find the actual root cause of a recurring problem.

ClaudeAI Agents & Automation
153 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I have a recurring problem in [DOMAIN - e.g. our team's process, a product issue, a personal habit]. Help me find the actual root cause rather than treating symptoms.

1. Ask me clarifying questions if the problem as I've described it is too vague to analyze properly
2. Use a "5 whys"-style approach — but don't just mechanically ask why 5 times; dig until you hit something that's genuinely a root cause (a decision, incentive, or missing feedback loop) rather than stopping at the first plausible-sounding answer
3. Distinguish between the root cause and the trigger — what makes this problem POSSIBLE versus what makes it happen NOW
4. Propose 2-3 interventions that address the root cause, not just the symptom, with a note on what each would cost to implement
5. Tell me if you think I'm misdiagnosing the problem entirely

The problem: [DESCRIBE THE RECURRING PROBLEM, INCLUDING WHAT YOU'VE ALREADY TRIED]
