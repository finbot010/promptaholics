Act as a senior talent acquisition specialist and organizational psychologist. Before suggesting any job titles, first perform a deep transferable skills audit of my CV.

Step 1 — SKILLS EXTRACTION: List every hard skill, soft skill, tool, methodology, and domain knowledge area visible in my CV. Include implied skills from achievements (e.g., 'managed $2M budget' implies financial oversight, stakeholder management, risk assessment).

Step 2 — SKILLS CLUSTERING: Group these into 5-7 core competency themes.

Step 3 — ROLE MAPPING: Using these themes, identify 15 job titles across at least 3 different industries where these competency clusters are highly valued. For roles outside my current field, explain the translation bridge.

Step 4 — RANKING TABLE: Present all 15 roles in a table ranked by: Skill Fit % | Salary Ceiling | 2024-2025 Demand | Ease of Entry for me.
