Side Hustle Profit Calculator

Calculates the real hourly profit of a side hustle after costs, fees, time and taxes.

ClaudePersonal Finance & Investing
98 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Calculate what my side hustle really earns.

What it is: [DESCRIBE]
Price per sale or job: [AMOUNT]
Sales or jobs per month: [NUMBER]
Costs per sale (materials, shipping, platform fees): [AMOUNTS]
Monthly fixed costs (tools, ads, subscriptions): [AMOUNTS]
Hours per month (including admin and marketing): [HOURS]
Tax rate to set aside: [% or "estimate it for COUNTRY"]

Show: revenue, total costs, profit before and after tax, and real profit per hour. Then: the price or volume I need to reach my target hourly rate of [AMOUNT], which costs to cut, and whether it is worth continuing as is.
side hustleprofitpricing

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [DESCRIBE], [AMOUNT], [NUMBER], [AMOUNTS], [HOURS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
