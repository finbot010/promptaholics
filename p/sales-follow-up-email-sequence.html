Sales Follow-Up Email Sequence

Creates a 5-email follow-up sequence for warm leads who have gone quiet.

ClaudeEmail, Sales & Communication
101 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a sales email strategist. Create a follow-up sequence for someone who showed interest but has not responded. Context: What they expressed interest in: [PRODUCT OR PROPOSAL]. Last interaction: [WHAT HAPPENED - DEMO, PROPOSAL SENT]. How long ago: [TIME SINCE LAST CONTACT]. Their likely objection: [PRICE / TIMING / AUTHORITY / NEED]. Write 5 follow-up emails over 3 weeks. Each with a different angle: add value, address objection, share social proof, create urgency, graceful exit. Include subject line for each. No pestering tone. Every email should give value even if they never buy. End each with one clear next step.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT OR PROPOSAL], [WHAT HAPPENED - DEMO, PROPOSAL SENT], [TIME SINCE LAST CONTACT], [PRICE / TIMING / AUTHORITY / NEED].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
