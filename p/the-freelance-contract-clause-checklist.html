The Freelance Contract Clause Checklist

Provides a checklist of standard freelance contract clauses to check for gaps.

ChatGPTLegal & Admin
59 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm about to sign a contract as a freelancer for [type of work]. List the standard clauses a freelance contract should have (payment terms, IP ownership, kill fee, revision limits, late payment terms) so I can check which ones are missing from what I've been sent. Not legal advice — a checklist to bring to a lawyer if needed.
