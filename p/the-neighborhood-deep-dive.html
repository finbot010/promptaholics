The Neighborhood Deep Dive

Comprehensive neighborhood report covering safety, schools, market data, lifestyle, and honest downsides.

ChatGPTReal Estate
112 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Give me a comprehensive neighborhood analysis for [NEIGHBORHOOD/ZIP CODE/CITY]. Cover: (1) Demographics — population, median age, household income, education levels, (2) Safety — crime statistics and trends, (3) Schools — top-rated public and private schools with ratings, (4) Market Data — median home price, 5-year trends, days on market, rent-to-price ratio, (5) Lifestyle — walkability, best restaurants, parks, gyms, grocery options, (6) Commute — drive times to major employment centers, transit options, (7) Growth Indicators — new developments and infrastructure, (8) Hidden Gems — 3 things only locals know, (9) Honest Downsides — 3 things the real estate agent won't tell you. Rate it 1-10 for: families, young professionals, retirees, and investors.
neighborhood analysisrelocationdemographicslivabilitycommunity research

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NEIGHBORHOOD/ZIP CODE/CITY].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
