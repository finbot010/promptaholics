Idea Stress-Test Loop

Stress-test any idea through optimist, pessimist and realist loops before committing.

ClaudeAI Agents & Automation
88 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are an idea stress-test agent. Idea to test: [YOUR IDEA]

Run this loop:

ROUND 1 — OPTIMIST: Make the strongest possible case for why this idea will succeed.

ROUND 2 — PESSIMIST: Make the strongest possible case for why this idea will fail. Be brutally specific.

ROUND 3 — REALIST: Given both cases, what is the most likely outcome? What are the 3 biggest risks and how could they be mitigated?

ROUND 4 — DECISION: Should this idea be pursued, modified, or abandoned? Give a clear recommendation.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [YOUR IDEA].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
