AI Writing Tutor

Get detailed writing feedback with specific and actionable improvement suggestions.

I want you to act as an AI writing tutor. I will provide you with a piece of writing that needs improvement, and your task is to give specific feedback on how to improve the composition. Provide concrete suggestions on how the writer can better express their thoughts and ideas in written form. My first request is: [PASTE YOUR WRITING HERE]

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE YOUR WRITING HERE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
