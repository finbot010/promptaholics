Retirement Savings Gap Check

Estimates whether your current saving puts you on track for retirement and what changes would close a gap. Educational only.

ClaudePersonal Finance & Investing
108 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Check whether I am on track for retirement. Educational only; show every assumption so I can adjust it, and tell me to confirm with a financial professional.

Age now and target retirement age: [AGES]
Current retirement savings: [AMOUNT]
Monthly contribution (including employer match): [AMOUNT]
Annual income: [AMOUNT]
Income I want in retirement (today's money): [AMOUNT]
Expected pensions or government benefits: [AMOUNT]
Assumed return and inflation: [or "use moderate assumptions"]

Show the projected balance, the income it could support, the gap (if any), and 3 ways to close it (save more, retire later, adjust spending) with the effect of each. Add a sensitivity table for lower and higher returns.
retirementsavingsplanning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AGES], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
