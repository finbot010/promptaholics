Documentary Interview Question Planner

Plans interview questions that draw out stories and emotion for a documentary or brand film.

ClaudeVideo & Film
77 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Plan interviews for my documentary or brand film.

Film subject: [TOPIC]
Interviewee: [WHO, RELATIONSHIP TO STORY]
Story I hope to tell: [ARC]
Interview length: [MINUTES]

Give me: a warm-up section; 20 open questions in an order that builds from facts to feelings; follow-ups that get stories instead of opinions ("tell me about a time..."); how to get usable soundbites (ask them to repeat the question in their answer); sensitive topics to approach carefully; and a closing question.
documentaryinterviewsstorytelling

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC], [WHO, RELATIONSHIP TO STORY], [ARC], [MINUTES].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
