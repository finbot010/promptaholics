Short Film Script Doctor

Gives a short film script detailed notes on structure, character, dialogue and what can be shot on a small budget.

ClaudeVideo & Film
88 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Give my short film script notes like a professional script doctor.

Script:
[PASTE]
Target length: [MINUTES]
Budget level: [MICRO / LOW]
What I want the audience to feel at the end: [FEELING]

Notes on: the opening (does it hook in 60 seconds?), the protagonist's want and obstacle, scene-by-scene purpose (cut anything without one), dialogue that explains too much, the ending, and production problems for my budget (locations, extras, night shoots). End with the 5 changes that would improve it most, and rewrite the weakest scene as an example.
screenwritingshort filmscript feedback

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE], [MINUTES], [MICRO / LOW], [FEELING].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
