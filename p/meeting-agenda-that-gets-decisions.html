Meeting Agenda That Gets Decisions

Builds a focused meeting agenda with timed items, owners and the decision each item needs.

ClaudeEmail, Sales & Communication
64 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a meeting agenda.

Meeting purpose: [PURPOSE]
Attendees and roles: [LIST]
Length: [MINUTES]
Topics to cover: [LIST]
Pre-reading available: [DOCS]

For each item: the decision or output needed, owner, time box, and what attendees should prepare. Put decisions first, updates last (or move updates to email). Include a 5-minute wrap-up with actions and owners, and the invitation text explaining why each person is needed.
meetingsagendadecision making

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PURPOSE], [LIST], [MINUTES], [DOCS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
