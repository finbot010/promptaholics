Research Paper Assistant

Academic paper research and outline with thesis options, section structure, and source-finding strategy.

ChatGPTEducation & Learning
93 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me research and outline an academic paper on [TOPIC].

Paper type: [RESEARCH PAPER/THESIS/ESSAY/LITERATURE REVIEW]
Field: [SUBJECT AREA]
Thesis: [YOUR CENTRAL CLAIM — or ask me to help develop one]
Length: [WORD COUNT/PAGES]
Citation style: [APA/MLA/CHICAGO]
Academic level: [HIGH SCHOOL/UNDERGRADUATE/GRADUATE]

Provide:
1. Refined thesis statement (3 options)
2. Full paper outline with section headings
3. Key arguments per section
4. Types of sources to find
5. 10 specific search queries for academic sources
6. Common counterarguments to address
7. Transition sentences between sections

Note: Research assistance only. All writing should be your own.
researchacademicwritingthesispaper

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TOPIC], [RESEARCH PAPER/THESIS/ESSAY/LITERATURE REVIEW], [SUBJECT AREA], [WORD COUNT/PAGES], [APA/MLA/CHICAGO], [HIGH SCHOOL/UNDERGRADUATE/GRADUATE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
