Nature Escape Finder

Finds parks, trails and quiet green spaces near you for a restorative break, matched to your time and energy.

GeminiHealth & Wellness
80 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Find nature escapes near [ADDRESS / AREA].

Time available: [e.g. 1 hour, half day, full day]
Energy level: [GENTLE WALK / MODERATE HIKE / CHALLENGING]
Getting there: [WALK / TRANSIT / CAR]
Want: [WATER / FOREST / VIEWS / QUIET / DOG-FRIENDLY]

List 6 places with travel time, trail length and difficulty if known, facilities (restrooms, parking), how busy they get, and the best time to go. Add a short plan for the outing and what to bring.
naturehikinggoogle maps

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ADDRESS / AREA], [GENTLE WALK / MODERATE HIKE / CHALLENGING], [WALK / TRANSIT / CAR], [WATER / FOREST / VIEWS / QUIET / DOG-FRIENDLY].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
