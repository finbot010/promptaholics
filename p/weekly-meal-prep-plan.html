Weekly Meal Prep Plan

Plans a week of meals you can batch-cook in one session, with a shopping list and prep order.

ChatGPTFood & Cooking
75 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Create a weekly meal prep plan.

People: [NUMBER]
Meals to prep: [LUNCHES / DINNERS / BREAKFASTS]
Prep time available: [e.g. 2 hours on Sunday]
Budget: [AMOUNT]
Preferences and restrictions: [DETAILS]
Containers and freezer space: [DETAILS]

Give me: the menu for the week; a shopping list by store section; a step-by-step prep order for the session so things cook in parallel; how long each dish keeps in the fridge and which to freeze; and reheating instructions.
meal prepweekly planbatch cooking

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [NUMBER], [LUNCHES / DINNERS / BREAKFASTS], [AMOUNT], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
