Power of Attorney Prep Questions

Prepares you and a family member to discuss power of attorney with a lawyer.

ClaudeLegal & Admin
94 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm helping [MYSELF / MY PARENT / A RELATIVE] set up power of attorney in [COUNTRY / STATE].

Situation: [e.g. planning ahead, health is declining, living abroad, managing property]

Explain the common types (financial, health care, durable, limited) in plain English and when each is used. Then give me questions to discuss as a family (who to choose, backup agents, how much authority, when it starts), the documents to bring to the lawyer, and how to talk about it respectfully with an older parent. Not legal advice — rules vary a lot by place.
power of attorneyaging parentsplanning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MYSELF / MY PARENT / A RELATIVE], [COUNTRY / STATE].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
