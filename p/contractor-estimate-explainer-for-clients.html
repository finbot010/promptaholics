Contractor Estimate Explainer for Clients

Turns a contractor quote into a clear, client-friendly explanation that builds trust.

ClaudeMarketing & Branding
94 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
I'm a [TRADE — e.g. roofer, plumber, remodeler] and need to explain this estimate to a client:

[PASTE ESTIMATE LINE ITEMS AND TOTAL]

Client: [HOMEOWNER, WHAT THEY ASKED FOR]
Why some items cost what they do: [MATERIALS, PERMITS, LABOR, DISPOSAL]
Options: [GOOD / BETTER / BEST, IF ANY]
Timeline and payment terms: [DETAILS]

Write a client-friendly summary that explains each section in plain words, what's included and not included, the options side by side, the timeline, payment schedule, and next steps. Then a short follow-up text for 3 days later. No jargon, no pressure.
contractorsestimatesclient communication

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [TRADE — e.g. roofer, plumber, remodeler], [PASTE ESTIMATE LINE ITEMS AND TOTAL], [HOMEOWNER, WHAT THEY ASKED FOR], [MATERIALS, PERMITS, LABOR, DISPOSAL], [GOOD / BETTER / BEST, IF ANY], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
