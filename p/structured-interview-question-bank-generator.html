Structured Interview Question Bank Generator

Generates a structured interview question bank mapped to the role's real requirements.

ChatGPTBusiness & Strategy
114 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Generate a structured interview question bank for hiring a [ROLE]. 

1. **Must-Have Screening Questions** (3-4): Quick questions that filter out clear non-fits early
2. **Competency Questions** (5-6): Behavioral questions ("Tell me about a time...") mapped to the specific skills this role requires, not generic ones
3. **Technical/Role-Specific Questions** (4-5): Concrete scenarios relevant to this exact role's day-to-day work
4. **Red Flag Follow-Ups**: For each competency question, one strong follow-up to ask if the initial answer sounds rehearsed or vague
5. **What Good Looks Like**: A brief rubric note under each question describing a strong vs weak answer

Role: [JOB TITLE]
Seniority level: [LEVEL]
Most important skills for this specific role: [LIST TOP 3-4 SKILLS]
hiringinterviewsrecruiting

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ROLE], [JOB TITLE], [LEVEL], [LIST TOP 3-4 SKILLS].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
