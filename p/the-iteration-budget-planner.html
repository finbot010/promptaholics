The Iteration Budget Planner

Sets a clear stopping condition before starting an iterative task, to avoid endless refinement.

ClaudeAI Agents & Automation
44 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
For [task], estimate how many iterations or passes this realistically needs to reach a good result, and what should change between each pass (not just 'try again'). Define what 'good enough to stop' looks like before starting, so we don't over-iterate on diminishing returns.
agentplanningloop engineering

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  3. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
