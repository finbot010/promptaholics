Video Shoot Location Scout

Finds filming locations near you that match your shot list, with practical notes on light, crowds and permits.

GeminiVideo & Film
82 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Help me find filming locations near [AREA] within [DISTANCE].

Project: [e.g. music video, short film, product video, vlog]
Scenes I need: [e.g. empty street at night, rooftop, forest path, industrial wall, cafe interior]
Crew size and gear: [DETAILS]

For each scene: 2-3 location options with address, what makes it fit, the best time of day for light, likely crowds, parking, and whether it may need a permit (and who to contact). Add a shoot-day route that minimizes travel between locations.
locationsfilminggoogle maps

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AREA], [DISTANCE], [DETAILS].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
