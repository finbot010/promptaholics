Difficult Client Email De-escalator

De-escalates a tense client email while staying honest about what actually happened, not just apologizing.

ClaudeEmail, Sales & Communication
127 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a client relationship manager who's defused genuinely tense situations without either caving completely or escalating further. Help me respond to this.

Their email/message: [PASTE WHAT THEY SENT]
What actually happened on my end: [YOUR SIDE OF THE SITUATION, HONESTLY]
What I can realistically offer: [WHAT YOU'RE ACTUALLY WILLING TO DO]

Write a response that:
1. Acknowledges their frustration without being defensive or over-apologizing for things that weren't actually your fault
2. States what actually happened clearly, without spinning it
3. Offers what you can realistically do, framed as a resolution, not a concession
4. Ends by moving the relationship forward rather than dwelling on the conflict

Keep the tone calm and professional — no passive-aggressive undertones, but also don't let me sound like a pushover.
client managementdifficult conversationsemail

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE WHAT THEY SENT], [YOUR SIDE OF THE SITUATION, HONESTLY], [WHAT YOU'RE ACTUALLY WILLING TO DO].
  3. Paste it into Claude and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
