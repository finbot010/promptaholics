Sales Page Copywriter Pro

Complete long-form sales page with all sections — headline through FAQ — written in direct response style with emotional benefits and multiple CTAs.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
79 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Write a complete long-form sales page for [PRODUCT / SERVICE / OFFER]. Include every section: above-the-fold headline and subheadline, the visceral problem section (make them feel it), the dream outcome section, credibility and authority establishment, the solution reveal, feature-benefit breakdown (at least 6 benefits written emotionally), objection handling section, social proof integration points, pricing presentation with anchor, guarantee section, FAQ addressing top 5 objections, and multiple CTAs. Write every word in direct response copywriting style — conversational, urgent, benefit-driven.
sales pagecopywritingconversionlanding pagedirect response

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT / SERVICE / OFFER].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
