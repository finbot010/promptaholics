Pricing Strategy Builder

Complete pricing strategy with model selection, tier structure, psychology, and competitive positioning.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
106 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me design the optimal pricing strategy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE].

Business type: [SAAS/AGENCY/PHYSICAL PRODUCT/COURSE/MARKETPLACE]
Target customer segments: [LIST 2-3 SEGMENTS]
Current pricing (if any): $[PRICE]
Competitors' pricing: [DESCRIBE]
My cost to deliver: $[COST]
Customer LTV goal: $[AMOUNT]

Analyze and recommend:
1. Best pricing model (flat fee / usage / tiered / freemium / value-based)
2. Recommended price point with psychological pricing rationale
3. Tier structure (if applicable — what goes in each tier)
4. Anchor pricing strategy
5. Trial or freemium recommendation
6. Annual vs monthly discount structure
7. What competitors get wrong about pricing that I can exploit
8. How to raise prices without losing customers
pricingstrategyrevenuebusinessmonetization

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PRODUCT/SERVICE], [SAAS/AGENCY/PHYSICAL PRODUCT/COURSE/MARKETPLACE], [LIST 2-3 SEGMENTS], [PRICE], [DESCRIBE], [COST], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
