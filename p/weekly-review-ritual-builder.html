Weekly Review Ritual Builder

Builds a realistic weekly review ritual sized to your actual time budget, with a fallback for shorter weeks.

ChatGPTAI Agents & Automation
127 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a productivity coach designing a weekly review ritual that actually gets done, not an idealized 90-minute process nobody sticks with. Build me one.

Time I realistically have for this each week: [MINUTES]
My current system (if any) for tasks/projects: [TOOL OR "NONE"]
Where things currently fall through the cracks: [WHAT KEEPS SLIPPING]

Give me:
1. A step-by-step weekly review sequence that fits my actual time budget, in priority order (so if I run out of time, the most important steps happened first)
2. Specific questions to ask at each step, not just "review your tasks"
3. One trigger/cue to actually do this consistently (a specific day/time, tied to an existing habit)
4. What to cut first if I only have half the time some weeks
weekly reviewproductivityplanning

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [MINUTES], [TOOL OR "NONE"], [WHAT KEEPS SLIPPING].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
