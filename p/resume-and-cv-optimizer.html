Resume & CV Optimizer

ATS-optimized resume with quantified achievements.

ChatGPTMarketing & Branding
52 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
You are a career coach and former recruiter. Optimize this resume for [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY TYPE].

[PASTE RESUME]

Provide:
1. ATS keyword analysis
2. STAR-method bullet rewrites
3. Quantification opportunities
4. Skills section recommendations
5. Summary rewrite
6. Red flags to remove
7. Score out of 10

Target: [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]
resumeCVcareerjob search

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [JOB TITLE], [COMPANY TYPE], [PASTE RESUME], [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
