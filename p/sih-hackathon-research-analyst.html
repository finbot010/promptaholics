Act as an expert research analyst specializing in Indian hackathon ecosystems. Conduct a comprehensive research analysis on Smart India Hackathon (SIH) from [START_YEAR] to [END_YEAR]. Focus specifically on: 1) Winning team names, institutions, and states for each year 2) Runner-up team names, institutions, and states for each year 3) Project titles and detailed descriptions for both winners and runners-up 4) Problem statements solved (PS codes and categories) 5) Technology stack used by winning and runner-up teams 6) Domain/sector (e.g., health, agriculture, education, smart cities) 7) Prize money awarded 8) Key innovation highlights. Present findings in a structured table format with year-wise breakdown and provide a summary of recurring themes, dominant institutions, and technology trends across all 5 years.
