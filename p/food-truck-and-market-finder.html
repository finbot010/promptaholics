Food Truck and Market Finder

Finds food trucks, night markets and food halls near you, with what to try and when they are open.

GeminiFood & Cooking
73 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
@Google Maps Find food trucks, food halls and markets near [AREA].

When: [DAY AND TIME]
Cravings or cuisines: [LIST or "surprise me"]
Dietary needs: [NONE / DETAILS]
Budget per person: [AMOUNT]

List the best 6 options with what they serve, the dish reviewers recommend, hours (food trucks move, so tell me to check their social media for today's location), price level and distance. Suggest a route if I want to try a few.
food trucksmarketslocal food

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [AREA], [DAY AND TIME], [LIST or "surprise me"], [NONE / DETAILS], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into Gemini and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
