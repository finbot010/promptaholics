Rewrite this text in a minimalist, clean, high-clarity style. Text: [PASTE]. Apply minimalist writing rules: cut every word that doesn't change the meaning, use the simplest word that carries the full weight, one idea per sentence, no adverbs (show don't modify), replace abstract nouns with concrete actions, delete the first sentence if it doesn't add to the second, and make white space do work. Provide the minimalist rewrite, a word count reduction percentage, and a list of every cut made.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [PASTE].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
