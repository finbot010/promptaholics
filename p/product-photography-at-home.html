Product Photography at Home

Sets up a cheap home product photo studio and a shot list that sells, for your specific product.

ChatGPTImage & Art
86 words · free to use
Open in ChatGPTOpen in Claude
Help me take product photos at home.

Product: [WHAT, SIZE, MATERIAL — e.g. shiny, clear, textured]
Where the photos will be used: [ETSY / AMAZON / WEBSITE / INSTAGRAM]
Gear: [PHONE / CAMERA, ANY LIGHTS]
Budget for props and setup: [AMOUNT]

Give me: a home studio setup with window light or cheap lights, how to handle reflections or my product's tricky material, background options, the 8 shots every listing needs, lifestyle shot ideas, and editing steps for consistent, accurate colors. Note marketplace image rules to check.
product photose-commerceDIY studio

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt with the button above.
  2. Replace the parts in brackets with your own details: [ETSY / AMAZON / WEBSITE / INSTAGRAM], [PHONE / CAMERA, ANY LIGHTS], [AMOUNT].
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT and send it. It also works in other AI chat tools.
  4. Not quite right? Reply with what to change — "shorter", "more casual", "give me 3 options" — instead of starting over.
